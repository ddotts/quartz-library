Abyssal, Celestial, Draconic, Deep Speech, Infernal, Primordial, Sylvan, and Undercommon are collectively called planar languages. They are associated with beings, traditions, or environments whose origins do not lie wholly within the prime material plane. Unlike [[Common Language|Common]] and [[Deresian]], they are usually classified by cosmological relationship rather than state or region.
