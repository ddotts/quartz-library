Abyssal, Celestial, Draconic, Deep Speech, Infernal, Primordial, Sylvan, and Undercommon are collectively called planar languages. They are associated with beings, traditions, or environments whose origins do not lie wholly within the prime material plane. Unlike [[Common Language|Common]] and [[Deresian]], they are usually classified by cosmological relationship rather than state or region.

- **Bugyn yaria** is a variant of Draconic associated with the old clans of the [[Bugyn Orda]].
- **Issiz** blends Deep Speech and Old Orcish. Only the desert families of [[Ping]] know it.
- **Old Sylvan** is still spoken in surviving fey settlements in the harshest parts of [[Procne]]. Fey presence on the planet predates its colonization.
- **Elvish** is a simplified version of Sylvan, in use mostly as part of Arcane tradition
