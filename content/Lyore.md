# Lyore

- **Capital:** [[Quexez]]
- **Population:** Approximately 310 million residents.
- **Species:** [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 66%; Aasimar 6%; Centaur 4%; Giff 4%; Leonin 4%; Loxodon 4%; Minotaur 4%; Owlin 4%; Satyr 4%.
- **Languages:** [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 78%; [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 64%; [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 52%; [[Planar Languages|Celestial]] 34%; [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 10%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 9%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 6%; [[The Moiety]] 11%; local traditions 25%; atheism 49%.

## Overview

Lyore is an arid, moonless world rich in deep-layer minerals.

[[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] law, legacy Aasimar authorities, and [[Haulers Guild]] freight overlap on Lyore. Lyore exports resonant minerals and can meet its basic needs locally. It imports timber and machinery. [[Good Doctor]] supplies equipment and is still negotiating a headquarters move.

## Appearance

From orbit, Lyore is a rust-and-ochre globe scored with pale mineral scars; [[The Medium]] cuts across its nightside like a black inland sea streaked with pearlescent light.

## The nightside and its people

Its nightside is submerged in [[The Medium]], creating pitch-black nights crossed by occasional pearlescent “shines.” Legacy Aasimar inhabitants arrived through planar shifts from the celestial plane and formed mobile tribes around sacred water towers. They preserve their own kinship, ritual, and community authorities, while later settlement placed trade, mining administration, and intercity travel under institutions tied to distant powers. The [[Valley of Gods]] is the largest surviving legacy settlement and the only water-tower community still continuously active.

## Settlement

Most settlements lie between forty and sixty degrees latitude, balancing short days and cooling aether against polar cold. Lyore is the principal source of [[Resonant Minerals]]: piezoelectric crystalline materials that react to aether flow. The modern population is predominantly mining-oriented and [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]]-aligned, with smaller [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] and legacy communities.

## Frontier migration

Centaurs, Giff, Leonin, Loxodons, Minotaurs, Owlin, and Satyrs reached Lyore through successive waves of mining, hauling, mercenary work, and NOX settlement. The [[Nova Independence War]] attracted people seeking distance from established governments, while independence and the AC 961 consolidation encouraged families to leave ships for Quexez, Valgrath, and the mining settlements. Their descendants usually organize through regional, occupational, Orda, or NOX institutions rather than species colonies.

## Architecture and Quexez

Architecture is low and heavy for thermal control and reflects a culture shaped by [[The Medium]] nights. [[Quexez]] remains the largest city. [[Valgrath]] is growing rapidly because the [[Viewport Gate]] connects it with its twin city, [[Vile]], on [[Lisden]].

## Regional geography

The main ore haul road links the capital in [[Quexez Basin]] with [[Mesa de Vetas]] to the east. Loaded trucks descend from the mining plateau past [[Valgrath]] and enter the basin by way of Bell Junction. At Valgrath, the [[Viewport Gate]] crosses to [[Vile]] on Lisden.

The A75 heads south from the basin across the flats. [[Whirling Winds]] stands near abandoned Buena Brisa, before the turnoff to Juniper Wells on the edge of the [[Carvaz Springlands]]. Farther into the spring country, caravan routes converge at Rāhband. Yādgār-e Āb stands beside a dry pool on the far side of the spring country.

The desert crossing to the [[Valley of Gods]] begins at Rāhband. Aasimar patrol the final approaches and guide invited visitors to the largest surviving legacy Aasimar settlement, where freshwater streams feed shaded groves and orchards around Lyore’s only continuously active sacred water tower. [[The Medium]] covers Lyore’s nightside and occasionally breaks into pearlescent shines.

## Physical characteristics

- **Diameter:** Approximately 7,450 km.
- **Surface gravity:** Approximately 0.91 g.
- **Mean density:** Approximately 8.58 g/cm³.

An unusually large metallic core beneath a thin rocky mantle accounts for its exceptional density. Its accessible deep-layer deposits support extensive mining.

## Sports

[[Night Safīna]] represents Lyore in Ironball. The **Kirstal Trove** is a Raging Dust circuit through mineral flats, crystal-cut service tunnels, and reflective quarry roads. [[Granite Veil]] sponsors the race and funds the structural surveys that determine each season's legal route.
