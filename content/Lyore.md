# Lyore

## Overview

Lyore is an arid, moonless world rich in deep-layer minerals.

> **Appearance:** From orbit, Lyore is a rust-and-ochre globe scored with pale mineral scars; [[The Medium]] cuts across its nightside like a black inland sea streaked with pearlescent light.

Its nightside is submerged in [[The Medium]], creating pitch-black nights crossed by occasional pearlescent “shines.” Legacy Aasimar inhabitants arrived through planar shifts from the celestial plane and formed mobile tribes around sacred water towers. The [[Valley of Gods]] is the largest surviving legacy settlement and the only water-tower community still continuously active.

Most settlements lie between forty and sixty degrees latitude, balancing short days and cooling aether against polar cold. Lyore is the principal source of [[Resonant Minerals]]: piezoelectric crystalline materials that react to aether flow. The modern population is predominantly mining-oriented and [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]]-aligned, with smaller [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] and legacy communities.

Architecture is low and heavy for thermal control and reflects a culture shaped by [[The Medium]] nights. [[Quexez]] remains the largest city. [[Valgrath]] is growing rapidly because the [[Viewport Gate]] connects it with its twin city, [[Vile]], on [[Lisden]].
