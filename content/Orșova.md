**Demonyms** · Orșovan / orșovano, -a

Orșova is Typhon’s capital and deepest hanging city, in [[Orșovei Reach]]. Public lamps mark the winding descent, with shops clustered on interior landings.

The winding descent from [[Brücker Pforten]] passes the Hanging Acres before reaching the city.
