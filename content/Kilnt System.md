# Kilnt System

## Overview

- **Blue Coast node:** AC 566 (The [[Odyssey]]).

Kilnt pairs two radically different inhabited worlds: cold, lightly populated [[Demeter]] and oceanic, industrially productive [[Nereid]]. [[Tartarus]] and [[Desponia]] place incarceration and information infrastructure at opposite poles of Demeter, while Nereid supplies organic matter, oil, and preserved food through [[Newland–Yuriel]]. The system joined [[Blue Coast|the Coast]] in AC 566, during the opening decades of the [[Imperium]] and before the [[Space Union]] existed; its infrastructure has therefore served several political orders.

Kilnt's strategic value lies less in a single capital than in functions other systems depend on and cannot easily replace. Considering the vast distance between Kilnt and the rest of [[Transpace]], culturally Kilnt is closer to [[Nova Sector]], but the system at large is uninterested in joining [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]]. [[Desponia]] and [[Tartarus]] create an uncomfortable feedback loop: the same interstellar institutions that commit prisoners also depend on the planet's largest datacenter for records and coordination. Control over identity, sentence, and archive is concentrated in one remote system even when formal authority is divided.
