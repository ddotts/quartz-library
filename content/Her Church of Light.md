The Church of Light is the continuation of the Church of Morrigan founded on [[Avergwyn]]. It is also known as Her Church of Light, the New Church of Morrigan, and simply the Church.

Modern doctrine recognizes Morrigan as an [[Ascended Godhood|Ascended God]] and emphasizes individual goodness and her exemplary life. It also teaches that Morrigan reincarnates and searches for her successors. The recognized line is Morrigan (1000 BP–0 BP), Francesca Morrigan (AC 120–300), Dolores Morrigan (AC 715–present), and Eileen Morrigan (AC 980–present).

## Demographics

The Church has approximately **1.41 billion members** across the surveyed resident and associated populations around AC 998. This estimate excludes people aboard major ships and artificial habitats that were not assigned local religious shares, and it does not count [[Eoccenon]]'s last-known or [[Palade]]'s historical population as current members.

Its largest estimated memberships are on [[Avergwyn]] at about 725 million, the [[Broken Isles]] at about 137 million, and [[Procne]] at about 126 million. Its highest local shares are on Avergwyn at 50%, [[Sator]] at 30%, and [[Tybalt]] at 24%.
