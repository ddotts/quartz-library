The Church of Light is the continuation of the Church of Morrigan founded on [[Avergwyn]]. It is also known as Her Church of Light, the New Church of Morrigan, and simply the Church.

Modern doctrine recognizes Morrigan as an [[Ascended Godhood|Ascended God]] and emphasizes individual goodness and her exemplary life. It also teaches that Morrigan reincarnates and searches for her successors. The recognized line is Morrigan (1000 BP–0 BP), Francesca Morrigan (AC 120–300), Dolores Morrigan (AC 715–present), and Eileen Morrigan (AC 980–present).

## The Ecros and Morrigan traditions

The Church of Ecros was the early form of Her Church of Light in [[Schil]], alongside the Church of Morrigan in Dindessia. The Schil church venerated Father Ecros and his four daughters, with Morrigan as its principal deity. In Dindessia only Morrigan was recognized as divine. Her father and sisters were honored as saints.

Schil devotion associated Gwyneth with dawn, nature and family, Morrigan with midday, peace and hope, Gisela with dusk, youth and warriors, and Goizane with midnight, knowledge and law. Its accounts remember the Perfidia as an attempt to seize Morrigan's power and sever humanity's connection to the divine. Schil devotional accounts identify Refugio del Paso as the sanctuary where Gwyneth sheltered people from both sides of the Perfidia and was betrayed by the Tyrant. The sanctuary stands above a side approach to Paso de Obern. Goizane confronted the Tyrant at Eigach's tribunal. Gisela and her host are said to have sacrificed themselves to invoke the Himmelgruft, also known as Gisela's Spear, and end the Perfidia. Schil accounts say Morrigan withdrew afterward to prevent further conflict in her name.

## Diaspora institutions

The Church spread through [[Din]] and its communities beyond [[Avergwyn]]. Its schools, charitable houses, clergy, festivals, and succession rites remain principal carriers of Dindessian culture. Local congregations adapt to the worlds where they live while preserving institutional ties to Din and the recognized line of Morrigan.

## Demographics

The Church has approximately **1.41 billion members** across the surveyed resident and associated populations around AC 998. This estimate excludes people aboard major ships and artificial habitats that were not assigned local religious shares, and it does not count [[Eoccenon]]'s last-known or [[Palade]]'s historical population as current members.

Its largest estimated memberships are on [[Avergwyn]] at about 725 million, the [[Broken Isles]] at about 137 million, and [[Procne]] at about 126 million. Its highest local shares are on Avergwyn at 50%, [[Sator]] at 30%, and [[Tybalt]] at 24%.
