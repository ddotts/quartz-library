The New Frontier Powers are [[NOX ULTRA]], the [[Bugyn Orda]], and the [[Tsuiseki]]. The category is based less on residence in the [[Nova Sector]] than on their position beyond territory controlled by the [[Space Union]] and at the greatest remove from [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] influence.

The three powers do not form a government, treaty organization, or unified military alliance. NOX and the Orda cooperate after a civil war and remain institutionally separate. The Tsuiseki maintain formal neutrality and intervene according to their religious mandate. Their shared position outside the two dominant institutional systems nevertheless gives them a recognizable place in galactic politics.

The Tsuiseki also belong to the wider [[Established Order]] as a recognized non-Nova power. The categories overlap because the Established Order describes the post-AC 855 diplomatic system, while the New Frontier describes distance from Union control and CCS influence.
