# Ironball

Ironball is a team ball sport played with a magically heavy iron ball whose dangerous mass and momentum remain relevant under unusual gravity. Clubs field squads under the same identity in two leagues.

## Leagues and circuits

The Prime League emphasizes unarmed contact, positioning, leverage, and handball-like passing. The Elite League permits weapons and spellcasting inside layered safety wards. Both leagues use circuit **0** for zero-gravity stadiums, **1** for planetary stadiums, and **X** for exceptional environments. Matches are identified by league and circuit, such as Prime 1 or Elite X.
