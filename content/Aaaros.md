# Aaaros

## Overview

Moon of the gas giant [[Oomi]]; thin but breathable atmosphere.

> **Appearance:** Aaaros is a soot-gray moon beneath a thin copper haze, its abandoned megastructures visible from orbit as impossibly straight black lines crossing ancient craters.

> **Appearance — Oomi:** [[Oomi]] is a deep violet gas giant crossed by chalk-white bands, with tiny Aaaros tracing a swift orbit close above its clouds.

Aaaros contains several abandoned mechanical mega-structures — ruins of previous civilizations. Site of the [[Battle of Aaaros]] (AC 532), where [[Redmond Daeson]] rose to prominence as a commander during the [[First Galactic War]] (AC 500–546). Aaaros combines two histories: The Umbra and Fomori left mechanical megastructures, and the Battle of Aaaros in AC 532 made it the place where Redmond Daeson emerged as the leading commander of the independence war.

Its thin but breathable atmosphere makes the ruins accessible enough for repeated occupation, salvage, and military use without making the moon conventionally habitable. Imperial and [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] engineers had centuries to study the structures before [[Ertes System|Ertes]] rejoined the [[Universal Federation]], so modern knowledge is distributed among public archives, corporate successors, and sealed military records. Any technology recovered here carries a provenance problem: discovery, wartime seizure, and corporate inheritance may each point to a different legal owner.
