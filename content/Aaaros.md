## Overview

Aaaros is a moon of the gas giant [[Oomi]], with a thin but breathable atmosphere.

## Appearance

Aaaros is a soot-gray moon beneath a thin copper haze, its abandoned megastructures visible from orbit as impossibly straight black lines crossing ancient craters.

**Oomi.** [[Oomi]] is a deep violet gas giant crossed by chalk-white bands, with tiny Aaaros tracing a swift orbit close above its clouds.

## Ancient ruins and the war

The Umbra and Fomori left mechanical megastructures across Aaaros. Centuries later, the [[Battle of Aaaros]] in AC 532 brought [[Redmond Daeson]] to prominence as a commander in the [[First Galactic War]] (AC 500–546).

## Access and ownership

Its thin but breathable atmosphere makes the ruins accessible enough for repeated occupation, salvage, and military use without making the moon conventionally habitable. Imperial and [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] engineers had centuries to study the structures before [[Ertes System|Ertes]] rejoined the [[Universal Federation]], so modern knowledge is distributed among public archives, corporate successors, and sealed military records. Any technology recovered here carries a provenance problem: discovery, wartime seizure, and corporate inheritance may each point to a different legal owner.
