The Universal Puzzle is the central unresolved subject of [[Tsuiseki]] religious and investigative work.

The Followers of the Path are in a pilgrimage to the center of the universe, and on its path they look for what they call the [[Galax Fringes|Fringes]], planet-wide arcane puzzles that contain clues for a greater universe-wide puzzle and the path to the center of the universe.

The [[Cathars]] search for clues to it in ancient artefacts, dangerous magic, and extraplanar phenomena. The Tsuiseki mandate permits intervention when such clues may be involved, even though the order otherwise claims political neutrality.
