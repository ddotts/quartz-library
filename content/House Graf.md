By the 14th century AP, the Graf were a minor Elumere house under Cane protection. One account associates their loss of court standing with an heir's turn toward alchemy and science.

Dona Graf founded [[deGraf's]] in Schil in 1432 AP. By AC 998, Graf is a longstanding family name whose fortunes have varied over the centuries. Its noble status lapsed, and surviving public records do not trace an unbroken family line from the old Elumere house.
