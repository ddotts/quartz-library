# Amrita Bramwell

Amrita Bramwell is a Naya Milessian endurance driver from an Eden produce-hauling family. She has a medium build, shaved temples, bright route tattoos on both forearms, and a plain civilian-style helmet. [[Atlas Group]] sponsors her.
