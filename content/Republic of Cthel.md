The Republic of [[Cthel System|Cthel]] is an independent [[Space Union]] member governing the Cthel System. Cthel belonged to the [[Imperium]] and the [[Space Commerce Federation]] before regaining its independence. Its single Union vote often decides contests between the [[Concessionary Bloc]] and [[Old World Powers]].
