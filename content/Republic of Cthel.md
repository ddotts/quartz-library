# Republic of Cthel

## Overview

The Republic of [[Cthel System|Cthel]] is an independent [[Space Union]] member governing the Cthel System. Cthel belonged to the [[Imperium]] and the [[Space Commerce Federation]] before regaining its independence. Its single Union vote often decides contests between [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]]-aligned systems and the older federations.
