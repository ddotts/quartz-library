Artemis Araval was a [[Nightwatch]] Company captain during the closing phase of the [[Nova Civil War]]. Araval's Company cooperated with [[Amanda Asgae]]'s [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] clan to defeat the extremist takeover of [[Lavaadil]], helping create the conditions for the AC 961 settlement.
