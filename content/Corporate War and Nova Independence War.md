# Corporate War and Nova Independence War

## Overview

The [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] fought the [[Space Union]], [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]], and the [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] while seeking political and commercial control of [[Farspace]] and Nova. The [[Nova Independence War]] began in AC 797 but became operationally inseparable from the wider [[Corporate War]] after AC 800. The SCF initially treated Nova as secondary, allowing NOX and the Orda to entrench.

Both wars ended with the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]]'s defeat in AC 855. The treaties signed that day on [[Eden]] dissolved the SCF, recognized Nova and [[Farspace]] independence, returned [[Ertes System|Ertes]] to the [[Universal Federation]], and enabled formation of the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]].
