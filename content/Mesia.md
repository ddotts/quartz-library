Mesia lies east of [[Ecria]] on [[Avergwyn]].

## Geography

The Geian peninsula has sheltered inlets along the shore facing [[Ecria]]. The Kasian coast lies farther south, where harbor approaches open into the Great Ocean. Islands lie off the continent’s southern coast.

Ships from Gelianum reach the sheltered Geian inlets. From there, coastal sailings continue south to the Kasian harbors and offshore islands, and a road climbs east toward Ashapura on the [[Devraj Plain]].

## History

In the 14th century AP, the Geian Duchy occupied the western peninsula and the Kasian Free States held territory to its south. By AC 998, Kasian is a federation within the [[Empire of Avergwyn]]. Geia remains a duchy of the [[Melissian Federation]].
