# Nilüfer Rostami

Nilüfer Rostami is a retired Ping Orc Iron Fist champion from a Nila-city family with continuing desert kinship ties. Heavy-built, with a broken right tusk and old hand fractures, she appears publicly in patterned dome-cloth robes. [[Malli]] sponsored her career. She now hosts a public fitness programme and directs free youth exercise halls in several domes.
