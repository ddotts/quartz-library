**Demonyms** · Samudratati / samudratatí

Samudratat is [[Eden]]’s western seaboard, where bluffs divide the coast into sheltered bays. Fishing hamlets occupy the lee of the inland hills.

The name means roughly “Seacoast” in Melissian.

Samudratat is the galaxy’s best-known holiday coast. Visitors take ferries between quiet villages and sheltered beaches, or follow hiking trails across the bluffs.

An eastbound railway from Naukāghāt crosses the coastal hills to [[Navan Plain]]. Barges from [[Nadipradesh]] unload at a transshipment quay on the coast.

## Naukāghāt

**Demonyms** · Naukaghati / naukaghatí

Naukāghāt is a ferry city in Samudratat. Ferry slips fill the sheltered waterfront. Passenger halls face small hotels, and repair yards occupy the working end of the harbor.

The name means roughly “Boat Landing” in Melissian.

The railway leaves the harbor eastbound for Navanagar in [[Navan Plain]].
