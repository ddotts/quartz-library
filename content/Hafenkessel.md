**Demonyms** · Hafenkesselian / hafenkesselense

Hafenkessel is a tundra freight coast on [[Sator]]. Protected landings along Sator’s tundra coast connect to inland freight roads. [[Porto Estiva]] handles traffic for the [[Rotator Stations]].

The name means roughly “Harbor Basin” in Schil.

Hafenkessel is Sator’s commercial center. Its ports move cargo and crews along the coast and onward to [[Opera]], the [[Rotator Stations]], and destinations outside Aldan.

Separate freight roads connect [[Porto Estiva]] to the quarry terraces of the [[Greifen Shelves]] and to Nietental in [[Aschenhang]].
