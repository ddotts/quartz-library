# Faswell–Kron

Faswell–Kron is a [[Tybalt]] construction and extraction company founded in the late AC 400s and the only historically Goblin-owned member of [[Collective Commercial Space]]. It participated in the [[Imperium]] and the [[Space Commerce Federation]] but avoided sanctions by remaining outside their wars. [[Ral Kronwell]] leads the company.

## Sports sponsorships

Faswell–Kron sponsors Raging Dust driver [[Brigitte Mohn]] and Tybalt's [[Tybalt#Magneetvlucht|Magneetvlucht]] and [[Tybalt#Yellow Line|Yellow Line]]. The program highlights construction, extraction, and safe route building under Tybalt's unstable industrial conditions. Faswell–Kron also contributes to [[Portas#Los Cuatro Senderos|Los Cuatro Senderos]].
