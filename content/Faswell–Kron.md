# Faswell–Kron

## Overview

Faswell–Kron is a [[Tybalt]] construction and extraction company founded in the late AC 400s and the only historically Goblin-owned member of [[Collective Commercial Space]]. It participated in the [[Imperium]] and the [[Space Commerce Federation]] but avoided sanctions by remaining outside their wars. [[Ral Kronwell]] leads the company.
