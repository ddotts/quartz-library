The Flederwolf succeeded the Fischer in Wiednausen. Their public association with the Order of the Lycan was known at the time, though its nature was closely held.

The family is remembered through its association with the order and Wiednausen's accounts of the Fischer succession.

## Campaign notes

### Avergwyn

- The family's custom was to send the second child into the Order of the Lycan. The order's connection to lycanthropy was concealed from the public.
