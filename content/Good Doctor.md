## Overview

Good Doctor was founded on [[Quamridge Mass]] in AC 948. Good Doctor supplies Nova with industrial equipment, tools, land vehicles, mining and combat ships, firearms, and edged weapons. Common-grade products make it widespread among [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] affiliates and the [[Alliance of Worlds]] despite [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] membership.

## A possible move to Lyore

Is negotiating a move to [[Lyore]], which would make it the first [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] corporation headquartered in Nova. Mining vessels outsell its combat ships nine to one and remain its principal revenue source. Founder [[Abel Hawkins]] murdered [[Emily Montenegro]] in AC 984. [[Lee Brown]] replaced him as CEO, and the scandal ended the company's gains against [[deGraf's]].

## Rivalry with DAWAN

[[DAWAN]] has since targeted its markets aggressively.
