Father Ezequiel heads the [[Hospitaller Knights]] and serves on the [[Tsuiseki Trinity]] aboard [[The Odyssey]]. His arm of the order maintains humanitarian operations, works closely with [[Red Feather]], and faces the practical challenge of sustaining the Odyssey beyond reliable outside aid in the [[Nodal Trough]].
