Vile is a city on [[Lisden]] in [[Bellecour Weald]], on its border with the [[Vandermere Lowlands]]. Lisden’s end of the [[Viewport Gate]] and the [[Quartz Library]] are both in Vile.

The monorail crosses the border at Vile, linking Bellecour with Vletdam and [[Prealis]]. The [[Viewport Gate]] crosses to [[Valgrath]] on [[Lyore]], Vile’s twin city. The connection is driving the growth of both settlements.
