# Doutzen Doutzen Doutzen

Doutzen Doutzen Doutzen is a Firan Creole Kobold driver and former lower-level mechanic on [[Quamridge Mass#Weightworks|Weightworks]]. Small and rust-scaled, with oversized hearing protection, they drive from a seat built into the vehicle's structural cage. [[Good Doctor]] sponsors them.
