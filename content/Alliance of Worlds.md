## Overview

Alliance of Worlds is a coalition of planetary representatives, civic organizations, and armed movements from worlds whose members consider them poorly represented by sector-level powers. Alliance of Worlds was founded at the [[First CCS Summit]] in AC 904 in response to [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] expansion and the spread of [[CCS Concessionary Governance|concessionary governance]] in [[Farspace]]. Alliance of Worlds seeks to protect planetary sovereignty against stellar organizations.

## Organization and reach

Active in Deres, [[Barei System|Barei]], [[Firan System|Firan]], and [[Aldan System|Aldan]]. Alliance of Worlds has no permanent headquarters. Meetings rotate between member worlds, and the host provides security. Alliance of Worlds lacks a unified military but coordinates auxiliary militias drawn from affiliated local groups, which [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] authorities generally classify as insurgent cells.

## Allies and opposition

Alliance sources report receiving informal [[Tsuiseki]] support and occasional assistance from Nova. The Tsuiseki do not publicly confirm this support, while Nova's involvement is limited by distance and its own piracy problems. The [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] classifies it as a non-state actor. Members disagree over how aggressively to confront CCS power.

## Open Channel 9

Alliance of Worlds broadcasts [[Open Channel 9]], an encrypted advocacy and coordination stream transmitted over [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] trade frequencies. Authorized receivers extract voice and data; others hear commerce or static. The Alliance treats the channel as a public-information service, while the CCS classifies it as propaganda and makes identifying its listeners a major security priority.
