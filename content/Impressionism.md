## Origins

Impressionism arose when [[Rationalism|rationalist]] ideas, developed through [[Psionics|psionics]], were applied back to magic. Rationalism made thought the actuator, and psionics showed that understanding could alter reality without reproducing a conventional ritual. Impressionism answered that the practitioner can do so because the practitioner is already part of reality.

## The Portas presentation

Cathar [[Rho Lovelize]], a mage and information theorist at the [[Excelsior Research Wing]] aboard [[The Odyssey]], presented this argument at the AC 276 [[Inauguration of Portas University of Arcane Arts]], eight years after the Odyssey reached [[Higys System|Higys]]. Impressionism treats reality as both actuator and medium: reality can read and change itself from within. A spell is therefore less an external force imposed on the world than an edit performed by one part of reality upon another.

## Information and interpretation

The school's emphasis on readable structure made errors of interpretation as important as failures of power. It also made the boundary between language and magic unusually thin, since a sufficiently exact description could become part of the change it described.
