Prealis is the capital of [[Lisden]]. It stands on cliffs above [[Moonlake]] and preserves the planet's most extensive pre-spaceflight old town.

Prealis stands at the cliff edge of the [[Vandermere Lowlands]]. House fronts line civic terraces above the old town. Narrow craft houses and Sylvan-marked stairs descend toward [[Moonlake]], with covered passages connecting newer civic buildings to the older streets. The monorail connects Prealis to Vletdam and [[Vile]].
