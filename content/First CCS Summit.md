The First [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] Summit was held in AC 904. The [[Alliance of Worlds]] formed during the summit in response to the expansion of the [[Concessionary Bloc]] in [[Farspace]]. The meeting also became part of the continuing dispute over [[Tsuiseki]] extradition rights.
