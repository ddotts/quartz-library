The Old Frontier is the region comprising [[Transpace]], [[Farspace]], and the [[Aldan System]]. It is a spatial and historical classification, unlike the political categories of the [[New Frontier Powers]], the [[Established Order]], and the [[Old World Powers]]. It describes a shared expanse of space rather than membership in a political order or alignment.

The name recalls the first “new frontier” toward which the civilised world expanded beyond the [[Core System]]. It encompasses the space traversed by [[The Odyssey]] before the vessel changed course toward the center of the galaxy, leaving Transpace for the [[Nova Sector]] and beyond.
