# Ping

- **Capital:** [[Dome Mostyn]]
- **Population:** Approximately 380 million residents.
- **Species:** [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 78%; Genasi 6%; additional Orc concentration 10%; Triton 6%.
- **Languages:** [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 82%; [[Deresian|Bloc Deresian]] 58%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 50%; [[Source Language|Source]] 35%; [[Planar Languages|Deep Speech]] 22%; [[Planar Languages|Issiz]] 20%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 14%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 7%; [[The Moiety]] 10%; local traditions 34%; atheism 35%.

## Overview

Ping is a desert world where most people live inside domes.

The [[Sultans of Ping]] rule the Nila cities. The [[Barei System]] seats the [[Space Union]], and the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] holds extraction rights. [[DAWAN]] operates from [[Dome Mostyn]]. [[Pink Salt]] and machinery pass through [[OSP3]], Ping’s orbital trading station. Ping’s domes rely on imported food and on imported water and metals for their utilities.

## Appearance

Ping is a rose-pink desert globe whose salt plains flare like mirrors under direct light, while reflected magic produces transient auroras in otherwise clear skies.

## Pink Salt

Desert sand is [[Pink Salt]] — an arcane compound that reflects radio waves and magic. Ping is named for its reflective properties: appears to "answer" any radio or arcane signal, making it the first planet visited by every space civilization. Desert filled with unstable magic that has been reverberating for eons; this arcane instability is believed to have attracted the Illithid parasite that incapacitated [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]] in orbit (AC 403; quarantine broken AC 616).

## Domes and industry

[[DAWAN]] ([[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] manufacturer, sole member with full integrated military-industrial portfolio) built the [[Dome Yüceil]] here, serving as its headquarters from AC 955 until the [[Plane Shift Disaster]] at the [[Third CCS Summit]] (AC 970), after which DAWAN relocated to the [[Dome Mostyn]]. [[Pink Salt]] reflects both radio and magic, causing Ping to answer signals and making it the first detectable target for civilizations approaching the region. The same property leaves ancient magic reverberating through the deserts, so the planet is valuable as a communications landmark, an extractive resource, and a hazardous arcane environment.

## Life inside the domes

Life and administration concentrate inside domes because the desert itself is unstable; [[DAWAN]]'s move into [[Dome Yüceil]] in AC 955 placed a major military-industrial headquarters inside that risk. The AC 970 shift of the entire dome to the Shadowfell confirmed that Ping's danger is not limited to the harsh climate. DAWAN's relocation to [[Dome Mostyn]] preserves its presence on Ping while turning dome design, evacuation, and planar monitoring into corporate-security priorities.

## Desert cities and Caleb’s pilgrimage

Every desert city on Ping bears the prefix _Nila_. The [[Sultans of Ping]] rule these cities, which include Nila Argentum and Nila Risator. Becket is the closest dome to the start of the [[Nila Pilgrimage Corridor]].

In _The Sultans of Ping_, Caleb recounts his pilgrimage and what he learned about Ping's ancient history. Some desert residents and historians treat the book as a religious tome. Others in both groups see it as a foreigner's distortion of local traditions. Nila Aureum, which Caleb describes as the culmination of his pilgrimage, remains a matter of debate.

Issiz is a mixture of [[Planar Languages|Deep Speech]] and Old Orcish. Only Ping’s desert families know it.

## Old desert peoples

Genasi, Orc, and Triton communities predate most modern dome settlement. Orc communities contributed the Old Orcish stratum preserved in Issiz, while Genasi traditions developed around Ping's reverberating elemental and magical conditions. Triton histories preserve a relationship with buried waters, deep reservoirs, saltwater caverns, and ancient cistern routes beneath the desert. Dome expansion divided these peoples between the Nila cities and mobile desert communities whose control of water, sacred places, and family law depends on remaining outside permanent dome authority.

## Regional geography

[[Su Defteri]] lies inland and east of the western domes of the [[Safiye Chain]], between Ping’s northern and southern desert communities. All travel between domes goes by shuttle via [[OSP3]], where passengers and freight transfer to a shuttle down to the destination dome. Aircraft cannot cross the desert. Dome Mostyn and the former site of Dome Yüceil stand along the western corridor, with the Dead Pens in an outlying concession beyond the former Yüceil site.

On Su Defteri’s northern, desert-facing edge, [[Dome Becket]] gives access to the [[Nila Pilgrimage Corridor]] through the Mar de Polvo. The southern desert trail into [[Kırık Yarlar]] passes below Listening Needles before entering the ravines. Desert dwellers risk the open-desert caravan trails, and armored hover vehicles cross only rarely. On the pilgrimage corridor and between the Nila settlements, people normally travel on foot, by hover bike, or by mount. Steel ribs above dome streets carry maintenance walks, while buried pump rooms, filtration galleries, and air trunks feed shafts to the roof systems.

## Physical characteristics

- **Diameter:** Approximately 8,030 km.
- **Surface gravity:** Approximately 0.91 g.
- **Mean density:** Approximately 7.96 g/cm³.

A large metallic core accounts for its high density.

## Sports

[[Mostyn Mirage]] represents Ping in Ironball.

### Fractal Test Run

The **Fractal Test Run** is an Aerial X exhibition circuit whose gates are reconfigured between heats by illusion, moving architecture, and controlled spell effects. [[Ruby Abyss]] sponsors the course and publishes the verified route only after each race has ended.

### Ping Spelljammer

**Ping Spelljammer** is a prominent Aether Duels event fought across a shifting field of suspended platforms. Competitors must manage movement and line of sight as carefully as spell selection. [[Boxe–Cane–Kaste]] sponsors the event and its warding crews.
