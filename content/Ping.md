# Ping

## Overview

Desert planet; life survives mostly in domes.

> **Appearance:** Ping is a rose-pink desert globe whose salt plains flare like mirrors under direct light, while reflected magic produces transient auroras in otherwise clear skies.

Desert sand is [[Pink Salt]] — an arcane compound that reflects radio waves and magic. Ping is named for its reflective properties: appears to "answer" any radio or arcane signal, making it the first planet visited by every space civilization. Desert filled with unstable magic that has been reverberating for eons; this arcane instability is believed to have attracted the Illithid parasite that incapacitated [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]] in orbit (AC 403; quarantine broken AC 616).

[[DAWAN]] ([[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] manufacturer, sole member with full integrated military-industrial portfolio) built the [[Dome Yüceil]] here, serving as its headquarters from AC 955 until the [[Plane Shift Disaster]] at the [[Third CCS Summit]] (AC 970), after which DAWAN relocated to the [[Dome Mostyn]]. [[Pink Salt]] reflects both radio and magic, causing Ping to answer signals and making it the first detectable target for civilizations approaching the region. The same property leaves ancient magic reverberating through the deserts, so the planet is valuable as a communications landmark, an extractive resource, and a hazardous arcane environment.

Life and administration concentrate inside domes because the desert itself is unstable; [[DAWAN]]'s move into [[Dome Yüceil]] in AC 955 placed a major military-industrial headquarters inside that risk. The AC 970 shift of the entire dome to the Shadowfell confirmed that Ping's danger is not limited to the harsh climate. DAWAN's relocation to [[Dome Mostyn]] preserves its presence on Ping while turning dome design, evacuation, and planar monitoring into corporate-security priorities.
