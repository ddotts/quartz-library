- **Population:** No surface residents; approximately 20,000 people live in associated extraction habitats.

Soplo is a pale-gold gas giant in the [[Lagal System]], marked by soft, widely spaced cloud bands and a faint dusty ring. Smaller extraction sites operate around it.
