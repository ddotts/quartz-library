Anais Lamar was a researcher at the [[Eden University of the Universe]]. At the AC 889 [[Eden Summit of Universal Academics]], Lamar presented a containment working designed for conflicting material and astral measurements near the [[Supervoid]]. When the working altered the rule by which its own stages were sequenced, Lamar treated the result as evidence that magical actuators, media, and rules are conventions within reality. Her account gave [[Surrealism]] its first stable formulation.
