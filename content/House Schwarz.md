House Schwarz administered Eigach during the Orchidee reign. Grant Schwarz held the mayoralty, and the family drew its influence from governing the capital and its close ties to the crown.

The family's former administrative role survives through the municipal institutions of Eigach. The mayoralty has long since ceased to be a family office.
