# Red Feather

## Overview

Red Feather is the galaxy's universal free-healthcare provider. Red Feather was founded on [[Avergwyn]] in the late 1400s AP as a planetary public service. Galactic expansion strained its resources, leading it to require government funding in exchange for access. The arrangement has remained beneficial enough for Red Feather to remain both free and universal.

The [[Broken Isles]] do not pay the service fee. Red Feather nevertheless continues operating there under its [[Red Feather Conflict-Zone Vow|vow to serve regions in conflict]]. Supporters describe this as the fulfillment of Red Feather's humanitarian mandate; critics argue that it allows the [[Deres Confederation]] to defer the cost of providing medical services. The organization also operated in [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] and [[Imperium]] territory during periods when those governments had not joined its service area, citing the same conflict-zone vow. Red Feather operates from an expanding demiplane. The [[Tome of Living Legends]] serves as its central patient database and communications hub.

Field medics are the only personnel able to open planar portals into the Red Feather demiplane. Hospitaller chapter-houses provide logistics at [[Blue Coast Node|Blue Coast nodes]]. [[deGraf's]] privately supplies unbranded prosthetics for patients who could not otherwise obtain them.
