# Nadita Solanki

Nadita Solanki is an Eden-born Aarakocra pilot active in Aerial 1. She has rust-red plumage, narrow flight goggles, and a cockpit configured for wing clearance. She excels on [[Eden#Zenith Marg|Zenith Marg]] and in demonstrations of civilian-safe flying. [[Atlas Group]] sponsors her.
