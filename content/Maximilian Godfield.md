Maximilian Godfield was president of the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] during the [[Third CCS Summit]]. Godfield disappeared during the [[Plane Shift Disaster]] at [[Dome Yüceil]] in AC 970. The available records do not establish whether Godfield died or remained within the shifted dome.
