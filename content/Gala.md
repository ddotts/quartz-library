# Gala

## Overview

Gala is [[Weiß]]'s capital ship, not the capital of the [[Deres Confederation]]. It belongs to the oldest and wealthiest [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] member and supports many of Weiß's administrative seats. Weiß operates the galaxy's largest conventional transport fleet, making Gala especially relevant in a system where Coast access does not solve difficult movement among fragments and astral hazards. The ship's proprietary remote-delivery and [[Weiß Demiplane Storage|demiplane-storage technology]] is uniquely suited to the [[Broken Isles]], where destinations may be materially near but separated by unstable planar relationships.

Its presence would combine logistics, extensive traffic records, and political influence: [[Weiß]] can supply isolated communities while mapping cargo and movement more completely than the Confederation itself. [[Cofradia]]'s deep local roots and control of Deresian administrative positions make Gala a likely site of negotiation—and rivalry—between two of the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]]'s most powerful members.
