**Demonyms** · Vetan / vetense

Mesa de Vetas is a mining plateau on [[Lyore]]. Mine roads cross a high table of exposed rock and descend past service yards to [[Valgrath]] and the [[Viewport Gate]].

The name means roughly “Vein Mesa” in Dindessian.

The gate makes the region a cultural link with Lisden. [[Resonant Minerals|Resonant stone]] from the mines provides an alternative energy source on both worlds and is exported for use in magitech. Researchers continue to investigate its uses.

The ore road descends past [[Valgrath]] and runs west to Bell Junction in the [[Quexez Basin]]. At Valgrath, the [[Viewport Gate]] connects the district to [[Vile]] on Lisden.
