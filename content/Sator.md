- **Population:** Approximately 260 million residents.
- **Languages:** [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 72%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 55%; [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 48%; [[Common Language|Common (Union)]] 32%; [[Planar Languages|Primordial]] 10%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 6%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 30%; [[The Moiety]] 9%; local traditions 7%; atheism 48%.

Sator is a habitable but rocky world in the [[Aldan System]], marked by severe volcanic activity. From orbit it appears charcoal and rust colored, wrapped in ash bands, with volcanic chains glowing across the nightside and settlements concentrated on cooler highlands. Sator and its resource-rich companion [[Opera]] support the [[Rotator Stations]] between them. [[Vineflare]] maintained its headquarters on Sator from AC 97 to AC 700.
