Sator is a habitable but rocky world in the [[Aldan System]], marked by severe volcanic activity. From orbit it appears charcoal and rust colored, wrapped in ash bands, with volcanic chains glowing across the nightside and settlements concentrated on cooler highlands. Sator and its resource-rich companion [[Opera]] support the [[Rotator Stations]] between them. [[Vineflare]] maintained its headquarters on Sator from AC 97 to AC 700.

## Schil migration

Sator received a large migration from [[Schil]] during the early settlement of Aldan. Schil workers, engineers, and secular institutions became central to the volcanic settlements and later shipyards. Sator eventually became a second Schil cultural center, preserving traditions that weakened on Avergwyn while reshaping them around industrial work, technical education, and life on an unstable world.

[[deGraf's]], founded in Schil, was among the institutions that followed this migration. Its workshops, clinics, and industrial supply networks helped connect the new settlements to Avergwyn. Its later headquarters on [[Typhon]] extended that connection along the same route taken by Satorian settlers into Cthel.

When [[Potemkin 1]] reached [[Cthel System|Cthel]], Sator supplied many of the settlers and technical crews who followed. Cthel's Schil inheritance therefore descends largely from Sator's diaspora rather than from a separate migration directly from Avergwyn.
