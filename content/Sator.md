# Sator

- **Capital:** [[Porto Estiva]]
- **Population:** Approximately 260 million residents.
- **Species:** [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 62%; additional Dwarf concentration 6%; Gnome 4%; Bugbear 4%; Goblin 4%; Hobgoblin 4%; Goliath 4%; Dragonborn 4%; Kobold 4%; Construct 4%.
- **Languages:** [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 72%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 55%; [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 48%; [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 32%; [[Planar Languages|Primordial]] 10%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 6%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 30%; [[The Moiety]] 9%; local traditions 7%; atheism 48%.

Sator is a habitable but rocky world in the [[Aldan System]], marked by severe volcanic activity. From orbit it appears charcoal and rust colored, wrapped in ash bands, with volcanic chains glowing across the nightside and settlements concentrated on cooler highlands. Sator and its resource-rich companion [[Opera]] support the [[Rotator Stations]] between them. [[Vineflare]] maintained its headquarters on Sator from AC 97 to AC 700.

Aldan institutions and Schil labor associations shape Sator’s industry. [[deGraf's]] maintains workshops on the planet, which formerly housed [[Vineflare]]’s headquarters. Sator exports processed minerals, components, enamelware, vehicles, and machinery. Sator produces some food locally and imports additional food, medicines, and timber.

## Schil migration

Sator received a large migration from [[Schil]] during the early settlement of Aldan. Schil workers, engineers, and secular institutions became central to the volcanic settlements and later shipyards. Sator eventually became a second Schil cultural center, preserving traditions that weakened on Avergwyn while reshaping them around industrial work, technical education, and life on an unstable world.

Dwarves, Gnomes, Bugbears, Goblins, Hobgoblins, Goliaths, Dragonborn, and Kobolds joined this movement as workers, families, and members of Schil associations. Goblin communities included migrants from the Veekz Islands. Constructs entered the corridor through the shipyards and industrial institutions that increasingly recognized them as workers and residents rather than equipment.

[[deGraf's]], founded in Schil, was among the institutions that followed this migration. Its workshops, clinics, and industrial supply networks helped connect the new settlements to Avergwyn. Its later headquarters on [[Typhon]] extended that connection along the same route taken by Satorian settlers into Cthel.

When [[Potemkin 1]] reached [[Cthel System|Cthel]], Sator supplied many of the settlers and technical crews who followed. Cthel's Schil inheritance therefore descends largely from Sator's diaspora rather than from a separate migration directly from Avergwyn.

## Regional geography

From [[Porto Estiva]] on the tundra coast of [[Hafenkessel]], one freight road climbs inland to the quarry terraces of the [[Greifen Shelves]]. A separate valley road leads to Nietental in [[Aschenhang]], east and downwind of the terraces. When ash closes the valley road, travelers from the Shelves can take the high back route through Hinterjoch, which comes out on the slopes above Nietental. Glashufen’s geothermal growing houses stand uphill from the fabrication city.

## Sports

[[Estivan Glutwacht]] represents Sator in Ironball.

### Pyre Steig

The **Pyre Steig** is a Raging Dust circuit over volcanic service roads, ash terraces, and old industrial inclines. [[deGraf's]] sponsors the race and maintains public repair depots along the route.

### Blade Feuer

**Blade Feuer** is a renowned Steel League tournament staged in a restored foundry hall. Its bouts emphasize weapon control, formal challenges, and the industrial craft behind each combatant's equipment. [[Stinson's]] sponsors the event and its public arms-safety exhibitions.
