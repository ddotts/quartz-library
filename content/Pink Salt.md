Pink Salt is an arcane mineral covering the deserts of [[Ping]]. It reflects radio signals and magic, making the planet appear to answer transmissions and turning it into the first detectable target for civilizations approaching [[Barei System|Barei]]. The same property causes ancient magic to reverberate through the desert and may have attracted the Illithid parasite involved in the [[OSP3]] Incident.
