Common is the largest civil language family and the usual bridge among the settled systems. It is not culturally neutral. Its major varieties follow old centers of population and power, and each carries political associations beyond pronunciation or vocabulary.

- **Milessian** is the language of the [[Universal Federation]]. Its prestige rests on the Federation's age, institutions, and claim to continuity with the earliest spacefaring civilization.
- **Dindessian**, also called **Din Common**, is the civil language of [[Din]], the modern Dindessian Empire. [[Her Church of Light]] reinforced it beyond [[Avergwyn]], while Schil communities preserved separate regional and diaspora traditions. Dindessian is also used by institutions of the [[Empire of Avergwyn]], but it does not define the Empire's other constituent powers.
- **Bloc**, previously known as **Imperial**, spread among the members of the [[Imperium]]. It remained useful after the Imperium's fall because many of the same systems, corporations, officers, and legal traditions passed into the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] and later the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]]. Since the [[Concessionary Bloc]] emerged, the variety has increasingly been called Bloc to symbolically sever its governments from their Imperial past.
- **Thieves' Cant** is a social register rather than a territorial language. Its vocabulary changes with the networks that use it, allowing criminal, clandestine, and marginal communities to communicate across otherwise incompatible Common varieties.

Common spread through administration, trade, military service, and the [[Blue Coast]]. Translation among its varieties is normally possible, but legal and technical terms are less interchangeable than ordinary speech. Imperial-era concepts preserved in the [[Daeson Directive]], for example, may carry meanings that a modern Bloc rendering hides or softens.
