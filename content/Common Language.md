Common is the largest civil language family and the usual bridge among the settled systems. It is not culturally neutral. Its major varieties follow old centers of population and power, and each carries political associations beyond pronunciation or vocabulary.

- **Milessian** is the language of the [[Universal Federation]]. Its prestige rests on the Federation's age, institutions, and claim to continuity with the earliest spacefaring civilization.
- **Dindessian** is spoken in parts of [[Avergwyn]]. It remains a regional marker within the wider political life of the [[Federation of Avergwyn and Aldan|Federation of Avergwyn and Aldan]].
- **Union**, previously known as **Imperial**, spread among the members of the [[Imperium]]. It remained useful after the Imperium's fall because many of the same systems, corporations, officers, and legal traditions passed into the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] and later the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]]. Since the CCS formed, the variety has increasingly been called Union—after the Union of Commerce—to symbolically sever it from the CCS's Imperial past.
- **Thieves' Cant** is a social register rather than a territorial language. Its vocabulary changes with the networks that use it, allowing criminal, clandestine, and marginal communities to communicate across otherwise incompatible Common varieties.

Common spread through administration, trade, military service, and the [[Blue Coast]]. Translation among its varieties is normally possible, but legal and technical terms are less interchangeable than ordinary speech. Imperial-era concepts preserved in the [[Daeson Directive]], for example, may carry meanings that a modern Union rendering hides or softens.
