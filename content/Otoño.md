Otoño is an errant city that developed from the Astral settlement of Tedingari. It is home to [[EZ]], and the [[Universal Credit Bank]] has declared assets there since the late AC 800s. Its present political status remains unclear.

## Campaign notes

### Avergwyn

- Tedingari was a dwarven settlement stranded in the [[Astral Sea]], with artificial daylight and facilities for working mind crystals. It was paired with Civitas Animarum, which supplied soul crystals.
- Scholars in the Cogitum Ward studied whether observation could stabilize reflections and how those reflections differed from spontaneous fractures. Dome Lucet contained the Lapidarius and Renacimiento facilities.
- Ob Trexiris reports describe an occupation and an attempt to use the planar anchor near the inverted lake to reach a convergence point. Escape through the anchor remained theoretical.
- A later account attributes an effort to draw ancient gods into the Astral Sea to an Alhoon colony using captive souls. The ritual's outcome remains unclear.
