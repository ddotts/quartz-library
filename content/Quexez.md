Quexez is the largest city on [[Lyore]].

Ore roads descend into the [[Quexez Basin]] toward public scales and assay yards. Low masonry blocks and shaded arcades face the market, and freight roads skirt the residential courts. Hooded lamps mark junctions when the Medium erases the horizon. The scales and dispatch houses handle disputed weights before consignments leave the basin. Reservoirs above the streets serve residents and industry, whose demands compete during shortages. Carrier inns, tool shops, and canteens fill the streets between yards and residential courts. Toward the basin walls, the city gives way to convoy layovers, reservoirs, and service yards. Weighing and dispatch bring NOX documentation and Guild contracts together.

Trucks from [[Mesa de Vetas]] enter the basin via Bell Junction before reaching the city’s scales.
