# Adela Morante

Adela Morante is a Procne Genasi Aether duelist from a storm-belt family whose history predates much of modern Portas administration. Her gray-blue skin, wind-tossed hair, and hem-weighted coat suit outdoor casting. [[Boxe–Cane–Kaste]] sponsors her.
