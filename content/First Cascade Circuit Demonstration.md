## Demonstration

The First Cascade Circuit Demonstration was led by [[Wyatt Tell]] at [[Vineflare]] in AP 1448. Linked low-level sigils reproduced multiple effects of higher-order spells, proving that a complex magical working could be divided into a repeatable sequence.

## Legacy

The demonstrated circuits were inefficient, highly specialized, and difficult to repair, but trained crews could reproduce them. Their descendants became the [[Tell Circuits]] used aboard [[Potemkin 1]]. The demonstration established the empirical basis of [[Proto-realism]] and marks the beginning of magic's first industrial revolution.
