# Nightwatch

## Overview

Nightwatch began as a citizen militia and became [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]]'s principal military and security force. Nightwatch operates as a federation of Companies. Each captain retains control of their crew while accepting a shared charter, communications standard, and command structure. Nightwatch began formalizing around AC 930 after the wars of the previous century.

Formalization introduced clearer Company contracts and institutional command procedures. Recruitment expanded beyond mercenary Companies, producing more trained personnel but also increasing equipment costs. Regional authorities and powerful guilds funded new recruits in exchange for security around their own interests, creating patronage and corruption.

Older Companies often opposed those arrangements, but the new bureaucracy also slowed responses to piracy around [[Lisden]]. The [[Nova Civil War]] was used to justify both tighter integration among Companies and a stronger central command. The [[Free Companies]] form a broad alliance of mostly independent mercenary crews and provide one route into full Company status.

Non-citizens may join. Many former pirates entered under the post-AC 961 amnesty as local Guardsmen, Company recruits, or organized [[Free Companies]]. [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] supplies standardized communications, identification, and base funding; each Company maintains its own ships and weapons. Performance is measured through a public Security Index published from the [[Zizek]] each quarter.

[[Nightwatch Rank System|Company ranks]]:

- CR5: Junior [[Free Companies|Free Company]].
- CR4: Junior Company.
- CR3: Senior [[Free Companies|Free Company]].
- CR2: Senior Company.
- CR1: Elite Company.
- CR0: Commanding Company.

[[Nightwatch Rank System|Individual ranks]]:

- Guardsman: local ground force.
- MR5 Officer: local administrator or senior Guardsman; member of a CR4 Company.
- MR4 Lieutenant: commander of a CR5–4 Company or member of a CR3 Company.
- MR3 Commander: commander of a CR3 Company or member of a CR2 Company.
- MR2 Captain: commander of a CR2 Company or second-in-command of a CR1 Company.
- MR1 Commodore: commander of a CR1 Company or second-in-command of a CR0 Company.
- MR0 Admiral: commander of a CR0 Company.

The lower command chamber includes every Commodore and Admiral. The higher chamber, introduced after the [[Nova Civil War]], contains five Admirals: two selected by performance, two by vote, and one elected by the lower chamber. The [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] Chancellor is the highest disciplinary authority, although any Commodore or Admiral may convene a field tribunal.

[[Lantern Officers]] are civilian observers, often retired [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] senators, assigned to some CR2 and CR1 Companies. They cannot issue commands but may veto orders that violate the [[Nightwatch Charter]].
