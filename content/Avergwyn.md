# Avergwyn

- **Population:** Approximately 1.45 billion residents.
- **Species:** [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 76%; Firbolg 4%; Harengon 4%; Leonin 4%; Minotaur 4%; Fairy 4%; Eladrin 4%.
- **Languages:** [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 82%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 58%; [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 24%; [[Deresian|High Deresian]] 22%; [[Planar Languages|Celestial]] 18%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 6%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 50%; [[The Moiety]] 21%; local traditions 8%; atheism 15%.

Avergwyn is a habitable world with diverse biomes and the oldest surviving concentration of civil cultures in known space. Its political life is organized through the constituent members of the [[Empire of Avergwyn]], but none represents the whole planet.

## Geography and trade

The Great Ocean separates [[Ecria]] in the west from [[Mesia]] in the east. On Ecria, the [[Maelor March]] occupies frontier hills in [[Schil]]. A market road from [[Obern]] reaches the March and continues east into the interior. A second road runs south to the March from [[Western Ecria]], where travelers can also cross the Cordillera del D’Ailadon at Paso Antiguo. The Corcel Negro Desert and Largo Pesar lie beyond the western mountains.

Along Ecria’s southern coast, the road past Andam continues east to Gelianum in [[Din]]. From Gelianum, a river road leads inland to Amarovado’s ford in the [[Valdoura Basin]]. Ships cross the Great Ocean from Gelianum to the sheltered inlets of the Geian peninsula. From those inlets, coastal sailings continue south to the Kasian coast and its offshore islands, while an inland road climbs east onto the [[Devraj Plain]] toward Ashapura.

Schil’s industrial towns and the workshops of the Melissian Federation supply machinery to other parts of Avergwyn. Cloth and wine also move between the Empire’s constituent markets.

## Historical geography

[[Ecria]] and [[Mesia]] are continents of Avergwyn. Ecria was the setting of the [[Great War of Ecria]], fought between the Kingdom of Schil and the Dindessian Empire in the 14th century AP.

## The Empire of Avergwyn

The [[Empire of Avergwyn]] is formally ruled by the old Dindessian imperial line but operates as a confederation among its seven members. The throne gives the planet a unified external face and checks the accelerationism of the Melissian Federation without directly administering every constituent power.

The Melissian Federation also belongs to the [[Universal Federation]], while the Empire of Avergwyn does not. This creates conflicts of interest within the planet and throughout the [[Space Union]]. The Empire belongs to the [[Confederation of Avergwyn and Aldan]], which remains one of the Universal Federation's closest allies despite their rivalry.

[[Vineflare]] maintains an apprentice school on the planet.

## Expansion

[[Potemkin 1]], the first Potemkin-class mothership, was built here and launched in AP 1500. Migration from Schil became especially important on [[Sator]], whose workers and engineers later formed a major part of the settlement of [[Cthel System|Cthel]]. The later [[The Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] and [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]] were built at the [[Rotator Stations]] in the [[Aldan System]].

Firbolg, Harengon, Leonin, Minotaur, Fairy, and Eladrin communities participated only lightly in the first expansion. Potemkin recruitment favored governments, technical institutions, and working communities able to supply large coordinated migrations, while these peoples retained stronger ties to local lands, institutions, and kinship networks. Their limited off-world presence reflects historical recruitment and political choice rather than an inability to travel.

## Sports

[[Legatus]] and [[United Meteor Hammers]] are Avergwyn's two major Ironball clubs.

### Imperio Rally

The **Imperio Rally** is a Raging Dust endurance circuit connecting old administrative roads, upland settlements, and modern freight corridors. [[Vineflare]] sponsors the race and its public charging camps.

### Poise Masima

**Poise Masima** is a celebrated Iron Fist tournament built around balance, restraint, and the ability to recover position without leaving the marked ring. [[Embrace Group]] sponsors the event, which rotates among Avergwyn's principal cultural centers.
