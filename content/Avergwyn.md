- **Languages:** [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 82%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 58%; [[Common Language|Common (Union)]] 24%; [[Deresian|Pilgrim Deresian]] 22%; [[Planar Languages|Celestial]] 18%.

Avergwyn is a habitable world with diverse biomes. [[Potemkin 1]], the first Potemkin-class mothership, was built here and launched in AP 1500. The later [[The Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] and [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]] were built at the [[Rotator Stations]] in the [[Aldan System]]. Available records remain incomplete.
