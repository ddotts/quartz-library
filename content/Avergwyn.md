Avergwyn is a habitable world with diverse biomes and the oldest surviving concentration of civil cultures in known space. Its political life is organized through the constituent members of the [[Empire of Avergwyn]], but none represents the whole planet.

## The Empire of Avergwyn

The [[Empire of Avergwyn]] is formally ruled by the old Dindessian imperial line but operates as a confederation among its seven members. The throne gives the planet a unified external face and checks the accelerationism of the Melissian Federation without directly administering every constituent power.

The Melissian Federation also belongs to the [[Universal Federation]], while the Empire of Avergwyn does not. This creates conflicts of interest within the planet and throughout the [[Space Union]]. The Empire belongs to the [[Confederation of Avergwyn and Aldan]], which remains one of the Universal Federation's closest allies despite their rivalry.

## Expansion

[[Potemkin 1]], the first Potemkin-class mothership, was built here and launched in AP 1500. Migration from Schil became especially important on [[Sator]], whose workers and engineers later formed a major part of the settlement of [[Cthel System|Cthel]]. The later [[The Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] and [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]] were built at the [[Rotator Stations]] in the [[Aldan System]].
