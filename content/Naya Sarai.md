**Demonyms** · Nayasarai / nayasaraí

Naya Sarai is Eden’s capital in [[Vidyagiri]]. Instrument shops share courtyards with teaching labs, and collections occupy former civic buildings.

The name means roughly “New Inn” in Naya Milessian and recalls the travelers’ inn in the neighborhood where visiting scholars still rent rooms.

The ridge line from Navanagar in [[Navan Plain]] climbs into the capital from the southwest.
