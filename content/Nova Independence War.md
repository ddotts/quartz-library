The Nova Independence War began in AC 797 as the struggle to end [[Space Commerce Federation]] control over Nova. After AC 800 it became operationally inseparable from the [[Corporate War]]. Its conclusion in AC 855 secured Nova's formal independence and confirmed the position of [[NOX ULTRA]] and the [[Bugyn Orda]] in the sector.
