The Fomori are ancient extraplanar beings described as creatures of bone, called Nona, and creatures of metal, called Ferrum. They fought the [[Deep Gods]], partially sealing them at the cost of their own near-extinction. Remnants of both groups were encountered as late as 1400 BP.
