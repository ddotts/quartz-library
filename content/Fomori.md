The Fomori are ancient extraplanar beings described as creatures of bone, called Nona, and creatures of metal, called Ferrum. They fought the [[Deep Gods]], partially sealing them at the cost of their own near-extinction. Remnants of both groups were encountered as late as 1400 BP.

## Ancient scriptures on assimilation

Fragmentary Schil scriptures describe the shedding of flesh as a measure of standing among the Fomori. The surviving passages distinguish replacement by Icor, replacement by metal, and the complete shedding of flesh. They describe Icor as a fuel and alchemical medium and ascribe power over the Deep Gods to machines it fueled.

A separate account attributed to Mishra Cane says that Iron armed humanity against Bone and that the conflict exposed mortals to entities he called the Senses. In this account grafts bound their bearers to the designs of the powers that made them. How either account relates to the Nona and Ferrum described elsewhere remains uncertain.
