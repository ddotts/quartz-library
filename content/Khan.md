# Khan

## Overview

The [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]]'s capital ship and a dreadnought rebuilt from a captured [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] frame. Usually carries roughly one-third of the active [[Dragonbrood]]. Its uninterrupted bow-to-stern spinal corridor, called [[Throat|the Throat]], allows [[Burkhan Luu Buga|the Burkhan]] to cross the ship in his full draconic form.
