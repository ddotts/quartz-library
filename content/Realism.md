## Response to classicism

Realism arose in response to [[Classicism]] during the development of [[The Odyssey|Potemkin 2]]. Classicism could preserve reliable rituals, and [[Proto-realism|proto-realist]] engineers could decompose some of them, but neither explained where magical energy originated or why components taken from different schools could interact.

## The Aether Programme

The [[Aether Programme]] of AP 1698–1705 brought together [[Vineflare University]] and the newly founded [[Sator Polytechnic]]. Its researchers established the aether plane as a plane of potential rather than another classical magical domain. Potemkin 2 entered development in AP 1700 while the programme was still underway, allowing each revision of aether theory to be tested against an active generation-ship project.

## The realist model

[[May Farday]], a natural philosopher from [[Avergwyn]] attached to Sator Polytechnic, presented the realist synthesis in AP 1711. Realism treats ritual as the actuator and aether as the medium, reducing magic to two fundamental operations: **evocation**, bringing untapped potential into being, and **transmutation**, arranging what has been evoked or what already exists. Different magical rules are not separate domains of nature; they are different procedures acting upon the same potential. This model became the basis of [[Applied Magic]].
