**Demonyms** · Defterian / defterense

Su Defteri is an old dome basin on [[Ping]]. Old domes cluster in a dusty bowl around communal wells. [[Dome Becket]] stands on the desert-facing edge nearest the pilgrimage route.

The name means roughly “Water Register” in Issiz.

The basin is home to many of Ping’s working- and middle-class dome residents and links their settlements to the desert routes.

Freight from Dome Āb reaches Dome Çarşı in the [[Safiye Chain]] via [[OSP3]]. Pilgrims head north from [[Dome Becket]] on foot, by hover bike, or by mount. Desert dwellers who take the southern trail to [[Kırık Yarlar]] pass below Listening Needles.

## Dome Āb

**Demonyms** · Abian / abiense

Dome Āb is a civic water dome in Su Defteri. Water stores occupy the dome’s base. The water council meets above shared courtyards and negotiates emergency supplies for nearby domes.

The name means roughly “Water Dome” in Issiz.

Freight bound for Dome Çarşı in the [[Safiye Chain]] transfers between shuttles at [[OSP3]].
