**Demonyms** · Bellecouran / bellecourense

Bellecour Weald is a wooded district on [[Lisden]] associated with the old Houses. Timber tracks meet among widely spaced trees. [[Vile]] stands at the border with the [[Vandermere Lowlands]], where the [[Viewport Gate]] connects Lisden with Lyore.

Bellecour is roughly “Fair Court” in Sylvan.

The old Fairy Kingdoms occupied the Weald, and some of their castles survive. The gate at Vile now connects Lisden and Lyore through trade and everyday travel.

The monorail serves Bellecour and [[Vile]], then crosses east into the [[Vandermere Lowlands]] at the border.

## Bellecour

**Demonyms** · Bellecouran / bellecourense

Bellecour is a forest market town in Bellecour Weald. Estate offices and timber stalls face a clearing market. Joiners keep shops along the approaches. The monorail connects Bellecour to [[Vile]] and the [[Vandermere Lowlands]].

The name means roughly “Fair Court” in Sylvan.
