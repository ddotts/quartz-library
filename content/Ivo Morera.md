# Ivo Morera

Ivo Morera is an Aarakocra Aerial 0 pilot born in the gas-giant station chain. He speaks Belter Deresian in family settings and is distinguished by violet-dyed wing tips, a compact pressure suit, and habitual hand contact with the cockpit frame in zero gravity. [[Weiß]] sponsors him under a contract excluding intelligence-service access to telemetry.
