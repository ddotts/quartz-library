## Overview

Maguuma is an overgrown world whose surface is largely jungle.

## Appearance

Maguuma is a green-black world veined by enormous brown rivers, with industrial clearings and extraction fires shining through the canopy like small wounds.

## Extraction

One of the largest organic matter farms and oil extraction operations in the galaxy. [[Stinson's]] ([[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] luxury goods corporation) holds an extraction agreement with the [[Universal Federation]] for Maguuma's high-quality wood; F (CCS food producer) is based here and owns the biggest share of mass food chains in the galaxy. Maguuma's planetary jungle is simultaneously an ecosystem and an extraction base: biomass and oil support mass markets, while Stinson's agreement targets scarce high-quality wood for luxury production.

## Food production

[[Malli]] is based on the planet and controls a major share of mass food chains through plant products and insect farms, placing everyday food supply beside elite resource extraction. Federation oversight must balance strategic production against destruction of the living system that makes that production possible. The contrast between Malli and [[Stinson's]] makes Maguuma a clear example of [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] members occupying different layers of the same resource chain.
