# Maguuma

- **Administrative capital:** [[Vanlekh]]
- **Population:** Approximately 400 million residents.
- **Languages:** [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 74%; [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 58%; [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 38%; [[Planar Languages|Primordial]] 24%; [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 18%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 9%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 6%; [[The Moiety]] 11%; local traditions 13%; atheism 61%.

## Overview

Maguuma is an overgrown world whose surface is largely jungle.

The [[Universal Federation]] oversees Maguuma’s extraction economy. [[Malli]] produces mass-market food, and [[Stinson's]] operates a local workshop and holds a concession for premium timber. Maguuma produces food at export scale and also exports oils, biomass, and luxury wood. It imports machinery and precision parts for production.

## Appearance

Maguuma is a green-black world veined by enormous brown rivers, with industrial clearings and extraction fires shining through the canopy like small wounds.

## Extraction

One of the largest organic matter farms and oil extraction operations in the galaxy. [[Stinson's]] ([[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] luxury goods corporation) holds an extraction agreement with the [[Universal Federation]] for Maguuma's high-quality wood; F (CCS food producer) is based here and owns the biggest share of mass food chains in the galaxy. Maguuma's planetary jungle is simultaneously an ecosystem and an extraction base: biomass and oil support mass markets, while Stinson's agreement targets scarce high-quality wood for luxury production.

## Food production

[[Malli]] is based on the planet and controls a major share of mass food chains through plant products and insect farms, placing everyday food supply beside elite resource extraction. Federation oversight must balance strategic production against destruction of the living system that makes that production possible. The contrast between Malli and [[Stinson's]] makes Maguuma a clear example of [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] members occupying different layers of the same resource chain.

## Regional geography

The three regions share a watershed. Water drains from the forested divides of [[Varṣāvana]] over the Mud Harp and into [[Khetvana]], the cultivated basin below. Travelers carry loads along the portage beside the falls. In the basin, the Grafting Loom stands at the forest edge and the Bees Knees processes insect meal near Mūl Chauk’s packing sheds.

Below Khetvana, tributary barges join the main river as it spreads into the [[Gād Maidan]] floodplain. Indrapur receives freight at its barge ramps, and the ochre clay seam at Labio Ocre stands on the next bend downstream.

## Physical characteristics

- **Diameter:** Approximately 8,600 km.
- **Surface gravity:** Approximately 0.75 g.
- **Mean density:** Approximately 6.13 g/cm³.

Lower gravity permits taller growth across its extensive jungle canopy.

## Racing

### Monsoon Floodrun

The **Monsoon Floodrun** is a Raging Dust circuit for sealed ground vehicles, staged when seasonal rain turns forest roads into rivers and lowland crossings into moving hazards. [[Malli]] sponsors the race, water-rescue crews, and temporary clinics along the route.
