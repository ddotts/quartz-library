The Nonary Rotation is the nine-year route followed by the [[Space Union]] [[Omnicron|capital ship Omnicron]]. The ship visits the systems represented by the Union's nine standing seats, making its itinerary a constitutional practice as well as a logistical schedule. The rotation has been interrupted only once, in AC 854 during the final year of the [[Corporate War]].
