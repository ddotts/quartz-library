EZ is an [[Otoño]] weapons company admitted to [[Collective Commercial Space]] in the late AC 900s despite strong opposition from [[Weiß]]. It manufactures disposable charged weapons designed to become explosives when depleted. Public records identify only a regulatory headquarters address and the name of founder and chief executive [[Gile]]; alleged ties to piracy remain unproven.
