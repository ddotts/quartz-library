## Overview

Eoccenon is a barren, tidally locked obsidian moon whose mantle contains [[Infernal Iron]].

## Appearance

Eoccenon is a lightless shard of black glass, its broad impact fractures flashing silver or furnace-red whenever they catch [[Bahamut]]'s reflected light.

## Infernal Iron

The only known natural deposit of [[Infernal Iron]] on the material plane; elsewhere it is associated with the Nine Hells. The surface has almost no regolith. Impacts create broad shatter fields in the exposed glass. Three abandoned [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] extraction sites remain on the far side after limited mining attempts failed.

## Working conditions

Low gravity makes surface work physically manageable, but vacuum and solar exposure make it hazardous.
