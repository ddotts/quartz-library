# Murder of Emily Montenegro

## Overview

[[Good Doctor]] founder and CEO [[Abel Hawkins]] murdered [[Emily Montenegro]]. [[Lee Brown]] replaced Hawkins. The scandal reversed Good Doctor's gains against [[deGraf's]] in industrial and consumer markets.

[[DAWAN]] ended its friendly rivalry with [[Good Doctor]] and began targeting its products aggressively.
