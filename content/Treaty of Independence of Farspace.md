# Treaty of Independence of Farspace

## Overview

The Treaty of Independence of [[Farspace]] was part of the AC 855 settlement that followed the defeat of the [[Space Commerce Federation]]. It prohibited the restoration of SCF governmental authority in Farspace. Whether later [[CCS Concessionary Governance]] recreates the prohibited form of control remains a central legal and political dispute.
