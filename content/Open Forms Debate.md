## Debate

The Open Forms Debate took place aboard the [[Omnicron|Capital Ship Omnicron]] in AC 711. Traveling lecturer [[Shannon Claude]] argued that practitioners could compose personal rituals from parts of several inherited magical systems, establishing [[Expressionism]] in response to the rigidity of [[Romanticism]].

## Political setting

The [[Space Union]] and its mobile capital had been created in AC 697, and the [[Corporate War]] would not begin until AC 727. The Omnicron's early peaceful rotations allowed scholars from former Imperial, Federation, and independent traditions to compare practices without placing the debate inside a belligerent capital.
