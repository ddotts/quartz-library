# Mark One

Mark One is a Cthelic Construct and active Steel League duelist who learned swordwork through a mutual-aid hall rather than a manufacturer. Their dark steel frame bears a cloth civic sash and deliberately visible impact plates replaced between rounds. [[DAWAN]] sponsors them under a contract barring consciousness-copy claims.
