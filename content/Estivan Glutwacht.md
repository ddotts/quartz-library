# Estivan Glutwacht

Estivan Glutwacht is a [[Sator]] Ironball club supported by fabrication cities, highland settlements, and Schil-rooted labor associations. Its mixed-species identity follows the Schil–Sator migration corridor. [[deGraf's]] sponsors both squads.

## Notable players

### Ilio Nullman

Ilio Nullman is a retired Prime League captain built and recognized as a resident through Satorian shipyard institutions. His brass-and-ceramic Construct body retains the replaceable forearm plates from his final season. He was known for precise positioning rather than superior mechanical force. deGraf's maintained his replacement-part contract; he now analyzes ward failures and accessibility rules on public Ironball broadcasts.
