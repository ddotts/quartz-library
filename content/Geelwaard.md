# Geelwaard

**Demonyms** · Geelwaarder / geelwaardense

Geelwaard is a reclaimed lowland district on [[Tybalt]], where dikes hold back sulfur-rich water. Separate intakes and covered reservoirs serve filtration stations above the flood line. Water boards ration treated supplies during outages, and crews use manual bypasses through magnetic reversals.

The name means roughly “Yellow Riverland” in Firan Creole. The name refers to the yellow mineral deposits that trace old floodlines across the lowlands.

The yellow mineral deposits draw visitors to the lowlands. Their filtration works supply the treated water Tybalt depends on.

The canal from [[Veen Reach]] reaches Geeldam’s navigation lock. Barges carry treated water upstream to the Reach.

## Geeldam

**Demonyms** · Geeldammer / geeldamense

Geeldam is a polder town built around a navigation lock in Geelwaard. The lock meets the main street beside a sulfur-stained dike. Pump buildings face the raised road and boat yards occupy the protected bank.

The name means roughly “Yellow Dike” in Firan Creole. It takes its name from the sulfur-stained dike beside the town’s lock in Geelwaard.

The downstream canal from [[Veen Reach]] meets the navigation lock.

## Sports

### King in Yellow

**King in Yellow** is a Mixed War Arts tournament hosted in Geelwaard and known for a yellow-clad reigning champion who enters only after the open bracket has produced a challenger. The event permits martial arts, weapons, and spellwork under one ruleset. [[BLACK LABEL]] sponsors the tournament and its layered containment wards.
