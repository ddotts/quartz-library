# Cofradia

## Overview

Cofradia is the oldest major corporation of the [[Broken Isles]]. Its founding predates reliable Deresian chronology because of the Observation Paradox. Cofradia supplies civilian and transport vessels, navigation, and computation. Cofradia was built capital ships for the [[Imperium]] and formally stopped military construction after the AC 698 peace. Postwar records indicate that it later resumed [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] battleship production without public disclosure during the [[Corporate War]].

Cofradia members have continuously held Deresian administrative offices since 1800 AP. Cofradia coordinates current [[Vanguard Corps|Vanguard]] operations in the [[North Sea]]. Cofradia leads exploration and research in the [[Outer Nodal Trough]].

[[Aldarro Ralei]] has served as CEO since AC 971.
