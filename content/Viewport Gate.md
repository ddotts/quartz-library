# Viewport Gate

## Overview

The Viewport Gate links [[Valgrath]] on [[Lyore]] with its twin city [[Vile]] on [[Lisden]]. The connection is accelerating the growth of both cities across the unusual planetary geometry of the [[Nough System]].
