House Orchidee held the crown of Schil during the 14th century AP. The surviving written record names King Morales Orchidee during the [[Great War of Ecria]]. The house employed the Spine of the World and the [[Storm Guild]], two of the guilds in service to the court houses. Its guilds defeated the Dissidence at Saira Da, opening talks between Morales and the Warrior Empress Morrigan Din.

The Orchidees survive in the record as a royal dynasty. Their name still carries weight in debates about the old monarchy, even where the crown's former powers now belong to Schil's civic institutions.
