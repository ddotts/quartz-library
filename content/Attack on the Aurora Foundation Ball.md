Masked attackers assaulted the Aurora Foundation Masquerade Ball in AC 998. The Masquerade Mercenaries, an unnamed [[Free Companies|Free Company]], repelled them.

Alleged participants were Linna Fallsing, an Aurora Foundation recruiter; Ignis Silverbreath, a [[Universal Credit Bank]] accountant; Silas Commes, a senior [[Cofradia]] pilot; John Rock, a [[Silver Meridian]] manager; and an unidentified harpy mercenary from the Lyore Badlands. Their disparate affiliations led investigators and the press to suspect a larger organization, although none claimed responsibility.
