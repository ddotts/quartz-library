Rho Lovelize was a Cathar mage and information theorist at the [[Excelsior Research Wing]] aboard [[The Odyssey]]. At the AC 276 [[Inauguration of Portas University of Arcane Arts]], Lovelize applied the findings of [[Psionics|psionics]] back to magic and argued that reality can read and change itself from within. This presentation established [[Impressionism]].
