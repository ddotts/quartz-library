## Overview

Thunderdome is the largest pirate vessel in the galaxy, assembled over decades from salvaged hulls and components. Thunderdome is maintained by the [[Court Families]], an unstable alliance of pirate bands that serves as the nearest thing to a pirate government. Smaller vessels pay tribute in exchange for protection, docking, and refit access.

## A costly target

[[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] and the [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] avoid a direct attack because all sides expect catastrophic mutual losses. Thunderdome is under the [[Open Door]], anyone who reaches the ship under their own power receives the protection of pirate law. Approaching the vessel remains dangerous and is normally met with force. [[Nightwatch]] Companies and Inquisitors have both visited under this custom.
