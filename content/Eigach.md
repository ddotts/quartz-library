Eigach is the capital of [[Schil]]. Its historical center grew around the royal citadel and La Aguja, where four walls met and divided the city into seasonal districts. By the 14th century AP, the Spring district contained the Queen's Garden and the Paseo Blanco. Portus Magus Library stood in the Winter academic quarter. In Autumn, Trova la Pace rose above its amphitheater. The Summer district's Respiradero drew on the Innerer See and fed the Grand Canal toward the port, where the Marble Market occupied a former imperial-style temple.

Eigach remains Schil's political center. The old walls and La Aguja still define the historical center. Portus Magus preserves records of the kingdom, while the Grand Canal and the surviving Respiradero works show how the capital was powered before the current age.

The citadel remains the seat of Schil’s government. Housing is sparse within the old walls, which enclose a largely preserved historical center. Most urban growth has taken place beyond the walls. La Aguja’s towers overlook the center from the wall walks.

The road from [[Obern]] descends from Paso de Obern into Eigach. The original shipyard of [[Potemkin 1]] occupied a sheltered inlet outside the city.

## Seasonal districts

**Spring.** The Queen’s Garden and Paseo Blanco anchor the district. Historical accounts also place l’Etelage and Pajar de las Valquirias here.

**Winter.** Plaza de la Harmonía fronts the academic quarter around Portus Magus Library. The historical register records Ottavio Antiques and the library’s scrying-mirror rentals. It places the Militum campus to the north, Arcanum to the west, and Mercantura to the south.

**Autumn.** Trova la Pace occupies a multilevel grove above its amphitheater. The district includes Estanyo, Piedra, and Abedul Ahogado near the Innerer See. Campaign-era records place La Costurera Ciega beneath La Aguja, a Baldochi shop in Estanyo, and a food bank in Piedra. A surviving receipt gives the importer Valiena an address on Gran Parada. [[Her Church of Light|Church]] tradition places Goizane’s confrontation with the Tyrant at Eigach’s tribunal.

**Summer.** The Respiradero drew water from the Innerer See and used geysers and evaporation to power the city. Water returned by the Grand Canal toward the port warehouses. The Marble Market occupies a former imperial-style temple near the port.
