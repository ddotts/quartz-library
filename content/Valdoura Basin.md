**Demonyms** · Valdouran / valdourano, -a

The Valdoura Basin is an eastern river basin in [[Din]] on [[Ecria]]. Willows line the gravel channels, and old meanders mark field boundaries between millraces.

The river road from coastal Gelianum reaches the basin at Amarovado’s ford. The [[Dindessian Temple of knowledge]] stands on an upper terrace above the town.

## Amarovado

**Demonyms** · Amarovadan / amarovadense

Amarovado grew from a ford named for the local saint Amaro. The market occupies a stone terrace above the ford. Mill yards stand below it, while school courtyards sit above the flood line.

The local saint Amaro gave his name to the ford and, later, to the market town.

The river road from Gelianum crosses at the ford. The [[Dindessian Temple of knowledge]] occupies an upper terrace.
