Dome Yüceil was a major settlement on [[Ping]] and the headquarters of [[DAWAN]] from AC 955 to AC 970. During the [[Third CCS Summit]], the entire dome shifted into the Shadowfell in the [[Plane Shift Disaster]]. Hundreds died, several prominent figures disappeared, and the devastated site was abandoned by DAWAN.
