Valgrath is a rapidly growing city on [[Lyore]] connected by the [[Viewport Gate]] to its twin city [[Vile]] on [[Lisden]].

Valgrath stands at the foot of [[Mesa de Vetas]], where plateau haul roads descend toward the [[Viewport Gate]]. [[Entresuelo]] lies below the city and [[Workshop 505]] operates in it.

The ore road from the mesa passes through Valgrath before heading west to Bell Junction and [[Quexez]]. The [[Viewport Gate]] links the city directly to [[Vile]].
