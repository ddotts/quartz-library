**Demonyms** · Greifener / greifenés, -esa

The Greifen Shelves are terraced quarry uplands on [[Sator]], with sheep paths and grazing valleys between worked belts of stone.

Enamelware and sheep wool from the shelves are known across the galaxy. The region also supplies Sator with food, ore, utensils, and clothing.

The main freight road descends to [[Hafenkessel]]. The high back route through Hinterjoch leads into [[Aschenhang]] above Nietental.

## Bryn y Lloches

**Demonyms** · Llochesan / llochesense

Bryn y Lloches is a cooperative upland city in Greifen Shelves. Cooperative housing and workshops share sheltered yards and storage. Growers tend heated beds on the terraces.

The name means roughly “Shelter Hill” in Schil.

## Emailhof

Emailhof is an enamelware kiln yard in Greifen Shelves. The shared enamelware yard in Bryn y Lloches brings sheet-metal workers and enamellers together. Independent kilns pool orders for export through the [[Rotator Stations]] and take hand-painted commissions.

The name means roughly “Enamel Yard” in Schil. The name refers to the shared courtyard where the enamellers work and sell.

## Hinterjoch

Hinterjoch is a basalt passage in Greifen Shelves. Basalt columns flank this narrow saddle. Paths around fallen blocks provide a back route when ash closes the main road.

The Schil name means roughly “Back Saddle” and refers to the route used when ash closes the main road.

The saddle route exits in [[Aschenhang]] above Nietental.
