# Hauke Siefken

Hauke Siefken is a Tybalt Dwarf driver from a family connected to independent hydraulic workshops. Dense-built, with a forked beard bound under his helmet and a mechanical wrist brace for high-gravity steering, he is known for finishing [[Tybalt#Yellow Line|Yellow Line]] after total navigation failure. [[deGraf's]] sponsors him.
