# Racing

Professional racing is divided between the Aerial League and Raging Dust.

## Aerial League

The Aerial League uses spacecraft. Aerial 0 races take place on zero-gravity courses, Aerial 1 on planetary courses, and Aerial X in exceptional environments or purpose-built extreme courses.

Notable pilots include [[Nadita Solanki]], [[Ivo Morera]], [[Leonor Bellver]], [[Danica Radović]], [[Tullio Sastre]], [[BEST MAN]], and the retired [[Owain Prys]].

### Circuits

- **Aerial 0:** [[Oomi|Gasline Deadshot]], [[Opera|Hourglass Run]], and [[Broken Isles|Shatterdance]].
- **Aerial 1:** [[Eden|Zenith Marg]], [[Tybalt|Magneetvlucht]], and [[Typhon|Sonnenkehre]].
- **Aerial X:** [[Festo|Cinderwhip Line]], [[Procne|Tempest Chase]], [[Bahamut|Abyss Dive]], [[Nereid|Elica Sommersa]], [[Ping|Fractal Test Run]], and [[Kirin Station|Void Curba]].

## Raging Dust

Raging Dust is restricted to ground motor vehicles on planetary routes. Notable active drivers include [[Doutzen Doutzen Doutzen]], [[Hauke Siefken]], [[Turgut Özkan]], [[Brigitte Mohn]], and [[Amrita Bramwell]].
