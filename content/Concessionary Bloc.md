## Overview

The Concessionary Bloc is the political alignment formed by formally sovereign [[Space Union]] members whose governments operate under sustained [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] influence. Its members are the Republic of [[Procne]], the Federation of [[Barei System|Barei]], the [[Firan Confederation]], and the [[Deres Confederation]]. They retain their own governments and Union representation while sharing an economic and administrative order built around CCS concessions.

The Bloc is distinct from the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]]. The Consortium is the private association of corporations and the [[Universal Credit Bank]]; the Bloc comprises the governments and territories shaped by their concessions. The two overlap through personnel, finance, infrastructure, security, and policy, but they are not legally the same institution.

## Concessionary governance

CCS concessionary governance describes formally sovereign systems in which Collective Commercial Space institutions exercise sustained influence over public administration or policy. That influence can operate through extraction concessions, credit, infrastructure agreements, security contracts, and corporate participation in government.

The degree of concessionary dependence varies by member. CCS publications generally describe these relationships as commercial integration or partnership, while the [[Alliance of Worlds]] and other critics use terms including corporate capture and puppet rule. The agreements supporting the Bloc have no standing in Space Union law, leaving a continuing distinction between each member's recognized sovereignty and its practical dependence on Consortium institutions.

[[Irlan Mining System|Irlan]] is not a Bloc member. It has no civic government, and its extraction rights are divided eighty–twenty between the [[Universal Federation]] and the CCS Consortium.

## Shared administration

The Bloc's courts, commercial standards, and professional culture combine Imperial precedents with institutions developed in the [[Broken Isles]]. The growing use of the Bloc name presents this order as a modern partnership rather than an Imperial successor. [[Cofradia]] and other Deresian institutions complicate that claim: Deresian actors helped build the concessionary system and remain among both its beneficiaries and its most organized critics.

Standardized administration allows the member governments to coordinate without formally surrendering sovereignty, but it also favors officials trained inside CCS institutions. Local councils and community archives retain authority in principle while often depending on Bloc professionals to make their claims legible to courts and corporations.

## Political position

The Concessionary Bloc is part of the wider [[Established Order]]. Within the Space Union it frequently opposes the [[Old World Powers]], while the [[Republic of Cthel]] often holds the deciding vote. The Alliance of Worlds formed as the principal organized opponent of the Bloc and is excluded from the Established Order.
