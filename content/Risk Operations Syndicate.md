The Risk Operations Syndicate, usually called the Black Syndicate or the Blacks, is the [[Haulers Guild]] organization responsible for gray-market cargo. It controls thirty percent of the [[Guild Court]]'s seats. Unverified reports also connect some Black Ledger operatives with contracts for the [[Bugyn Orda]]'s [[Dragonbrood]].
