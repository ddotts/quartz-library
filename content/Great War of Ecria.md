The Great War of Ecria was fought in the 14th century AP between the Kingdom of [[Schil]] and the Dindessian Empire. Both contested their border and sought control of arcane artifacts. The Empire suffered devastating attacks launched from Schil before open war escalated. Imperial forces then advanced as far as Saira Da and the Himmelgruft, also known as Gisela's Spear.

## The Dissidence

The prewar attacks were orchestrated by the Dissidence, a coalition led by [[House Cane|Therion Cane]]. It united nobles on both sides of the border who sought to take power in their own countries. The coalition also drew support from forces associated with [[Kur]] and from Media Res, the terrorist group led by Archlich Xiran of [[Ecria|Elmier]].

## The Returned

During the war, the dead were coming back to life in Schil and along the imperial frontier. They became known as the Returned. Their return went unexplained at the time. The cause was an altered state of Avergwyn's [[Anima]] following the [[Devastation of Rham]].

The Dindessian Empire persecuted the Returned. Schil's houses differed in their response, and the kingdom attempted to reintegrate the Returned into ordinary life. [[House Rumasa]] and [[House Piesmorados]] came under Returned leadership and together spearheaded reintegration. Media Res fought for the rights of the Returned against persecution across [[Ecria]] while participating in the Dissidence's campaign.

## Saira Da and the peace talks

Multiple guilds took service with Schil's court houses during the war. [[House Orchidee]] employed the Spine of the World and the [[Storm Guild]]. At the Battle of Saira Da, the Himmelgruft was destroyed and the Orchidee guilds defeated the Dissidence. The battle ended the war and opened peace talks between King Morales Orchidee of Schil and the Warrior Empress Morrigan Din of Dindessia.

A wartime issue of the [[Verkünder von Schil]] reported that the Himmelgruft glowed crimson under Cathar watch at Andam on the southern coast of [[Ecria]]. The report places the lance near Andam during the war.
