Western [[Ecria]] reaches from the inlets of Liveria across the Cordillera del D’Ailadon. Salt spray reaches the cliffs of the Costa das Eiras, where old threshing floors occupy sheltered terraces and harbor lights mark breaks in the coast. The coastal name means roughly “Threshing Ground Coast” in Dindessian and recalls the threshing grounds that once lined the shore. The English and Spanish demonyms for the coast are _Eiran_ and _eirense_.

One road runs south from the western mountain country to the [[Maelor March]]. Paso Antiguo carries regular traffic across the Cordillera near Schobeldach.

## Elmier

Small villages occupy raised ground among the shifting channels of the Elmier swamps. The swamps remain after the old miasma passed.

## Valle de Y’malor

The valley’s independent elven court retains its rights over the wooded country. Pico Kath and Pico Daoiwe rise separately along its eastern high ground.

## Asserico

Asserico stands near the mouth of the Río de Sinnero. River landings receive inland traffic, while separate coastal wharves take boats from the sea.

## Schobeldach

Regular traffic crosses the Paso Antiguo near Schobeldach. The Dauerte family’s roots in masonry survive in the town’s association with the retaining walls and stonework of the pass road.
