# OSP3

## Overview

OSP3 was formerly [[Potemkin 1|Mothership Potemkin]] III; now Orbital Station Potemkin 3 (informal: "Ospe" / "Ospe Station"). Incapacitated in orbit of [[Ping]] by an Illithid parasite; an entire section was blown away and placed in stasis. Quarantine held AC 403–616; repurposed as trading station after quarantine broken.

Trading center of the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]]; bridges goods between [[Farspace]] and [[Transpace]]. OSP3 is controlled by CCS (post-AC 616). OSP3 is Potemkin 3 transformed from an expansion vessel into the trading bridge between Farspace and Transpace.

Its hull preserves several political eras at once: construction at the [[Rotator Stations]], the AC 209 voyage, the AC 403 parasite attack, more than two centuries of quarantine, and subsequent [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] control. The section blown away and placed in stasis is not simply wreckage; it is a separated part of the original incident whose legal, biological, and temporal status remains unresolved. As the principal CCS trading center, the station must serve corporations, [[Space Union]] members, and traffic from Nova even though control rests with the CCS.

Its origin alongside [[Potemkin 1]] and the [[Odyssey]] makes OSP3 a secular counterpart to the two surviving motherships: fixed, commercial, and radically repurposed.
