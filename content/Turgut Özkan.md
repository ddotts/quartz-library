# Turgut Özkan

Turgut Özkan is an Issiz-speaking Orc driver from a mobile desert community on [[Ping]], not a dome stable. Lean and sun-darkened, he races with a wrapped lower face and salt-pitted goggles. [[EZ]] sponsors him.
