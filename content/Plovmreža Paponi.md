# Plovmreža Paponi

Plovmreža Paponi is a [[Nereid]] Ironball club whose home venue moves among the planet's artificial islands. [[Newland–Yuriel]] supplies marine logistics while facing scrutiny over extraction concessions near aquatic settlements.

## Notable players

### Andro Zammir

Andro Zammir is a Prime League screen player raised in a Bloc-speaking Tortle family on a mobile island. Tall, with a scarred shell rim and waterproof grip wraps, he creates passing lanes through legal screens and strong lateral movement. Newland–Yuriel sponsors his travel and nutrition.
