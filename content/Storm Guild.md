The Storm Guild won its reputation during the Red Summer, suppressing an uprising in [[Schil]] in about half a year. Mihai Erner received land and a title for his service. Jhonna Weatherlight commanded the guild's vessel, the _Viento Tormenta_. During the [[Great War of Ecria]], [[House Orchidee]] employed the guild alongside the Spine of the World. Both served the house in the war that culminated at Saira Da.

## Campaign notes

### Avergwyn

- The crew included Rumi Gheta, Montblanc, Alamer Brauss, Einn Shivleft, and Mihai Erner.
- The _Viento Tormenta_ docked at [[Eigach]] for repairs and hosted the memorial at which Bladeward invoked the guild's service and honored Obern's defenders.
