## Overview

The Supervoid is a non-spinning black hole at the center of the aether nebula, not an empty region of space.

## Appearance

The Supervoid is a perfectly still circular absence framed by a thin ring of distorted starlight, suspended inside the luminous aether nebula like a hole cut through the universe.

## Astral stasis

It is one of the astral bodies held in pseudo-stasis by friction acting on its celestial anchor in the astral plane, so its slow drift cannot be explained only through material orbital mechanics. The merged fragments of parallel systems surround it, making the black hole both a physical hazard and the common reference point by which otherwise incompatible maps can be aligned. Its lack of spin is cosmologically unusual and may be evidence of the planar collapse rather than a feature that preceded it.

## Navigation hazards

Travel near the center must account for ordinary gravity, charged aether, and the displaced relationship between material bodies and astral anchors at the same time.
