# James Jonastile Group

James Jonastile Group is a [[Procne]] retail conglomerate founded in the late AC 800s. It owns [[Mori's]], [[Fergus']], [[BT Börger]], and [[New Heart Designs]] and is led by [[Jeremy J. Baseman]].

## Sports sponsorships

James Jonastile Group sponsors [[Huracan de Cornelia]], using the club as a shared public platform for its Procne retail brands. It also contributes to the CCS joint sponsorship of [[Portas#Los Cuatro Senderos|Los Cuatro Senderos]].
