**Demonyms** · cinzen / cinzes

Isole di Cinza is a young volcanic archipelago on [[Nereid]]. Nereid’s only natural land above water began to emerge less than two centuries ago. Swells cut black beaches and carry ash through the gaps. Plants and birds occupy older slopes while algae colonize cooled lava below the tide line.

The name means roughly “Ash Islands” in Bloc Deresian and refers to the ash that coats them after eruptions.

Volcanic activity began when the [[Tsuiseki]] solved Nereid’s part of the [[Universal Puzzle]].

Santa Nerina receives surface tenders at the outer edge of the [[Rema Thermí]] gyre.

## Santa Nerina

**Demonyms** · Nerinan / nerinense

Santa Nerina is a volcanic island port in Isole di Cinza. Homes and rainwater cisterns occupy a stable lava bench. Floating cargo pontoons serve a sheltered quay. Fish smokers line the road uphill.

It is named for a local saint.

Surface tenders from the [[Rema Thermí]] circuit call at its quay.
