# Red Feather Conflict-Zone Vow

## Overview

[[Red Feather]]'s conflict-zone vow commits the organization to continue medical operations in regions affected by war even when the responsible government does not fund the service. The vow explains its unpaid operations in the [[Broken Isles]] and its historical work in [[Space Commerce Federation]] and [[Imperium]] territory before those governments joined its service area.
