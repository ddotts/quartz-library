# Wiston Baltasar

## Overview

Wiston Baltasar is the alleged Pirate King associated with the surge in piracy during the [[Nova Civil War]]. He was captured after the AC 961 peace and is imprisoned at [[Tartarus]]. Available records describe his title as an allegation rather than an established office.
