## Overview

DAWAN was founded in AC 948 by [[William Bright]], who remains CEO. DAWAN supplies the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] and Nova with automata, vehicles, industrial and military equipment, reactors, energy weapons, and AI training. Alongside [[Weiß]], operates a complete public military-industrial portfolio.

## Relocation

DAWAN moved from [[Quamridge Mass]] to [[Dome Yüceil]] on [[Ping]] in AC 955, then to [[Dome Mostyn]] after the AC 970 [[Plane Shift Disaster]]. DAWAN developed [[Trained Awakened Intelligence]]: a prototype automaton is awakened, its temporary consciousness recorded, and that model replicated in later units. Automata-rights opposition led to the [[Prototype Protection Act]] of AC 975.

## Competition with Good Doctor

Entered aggressive competition with [[Good Doctor]] after [[Abel Hawkins]] murdered [[Emily Montenegro]] in AC 984.
