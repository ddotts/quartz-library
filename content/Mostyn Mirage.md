# Mostyn Mirage

Mostyn Mirage is an Ironball club based in [[Ping]]'s western dome corridor. Its Orc, Genasi, and Triton members are presented through their Ping communities rather than as corporate settlers. [[DAWAN]], headquartered in [[Dome Mostyn]], supplies arena automata, ward power, and emergency systems.

## Notable players

### Ceren Yalçın

Ceren Yalçın is an Issiz-speaking Orc and Elite League ward runner from a desert family. Tall and copper-brown, with salt-bleached braids and layered eye protection, she reads reflected spells as changes in arena rhythm and attacks gaps before sensors confirm them. DAWAN supplies her regulated armor under a contract retaining an independent desert-community adviser.
