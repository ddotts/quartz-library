## From Potemkin 2 to pilgrimage

Originally launched as Potemkin 2, the vessel was renamed after the [[Lighting of the Path]] in AC 119, when its researchers, mages, and humanitarian crews founded the [[Tsuiseki]] in the [[Broken Isles]]. It is both a capital ship and a traveling nation: the Chapel and its [[Tsuiseki Trinity|Trinity]] govern the pilgrimage, the three elite arms operate from it, and [[Tsuiseki Civilian Classes|Squires]], Pilgrims, and Protected live alongside them without sharing the same status or beliefs. Its route explains the political reach of the Tsuiseki. Every node left behind became a place where the [[Inquisition]], [[Cathars]], [[Hospitaller Knights|Hospitallers]], and civilian followers could maintain a presence long after the Odyssey departed.

## Ruby Abyss aboard

[[Ruby Abyss]] is officially headquartered on [[Quamridge Mass]] but unofficially operates from the Odyssey, making the ship a hidden center of manufacturing, navigation research, and counterintelligence. The Odyssey reached the spiral edge in AC 952 and entered the [[Nodal Trough]] in AC 979. Its present isolation limits ordinary oversight just as trough discoveries make the [[Cathars]]' research more important. The move into the trough has changed the balance inside the [[Tsuiseki Trinity|Trinity]]. The [[Hospitaller Knights|Hospitallers]] must sustain a traveling population beyond reliable aid, the [[Inquisition]] decides what can be transmitted back through [[Blue Coast|the Coast]], and the Cathars alone can interpret most new phenomena; practical decisions about survival now double as arguments about the [[Universal Puzzle]].
