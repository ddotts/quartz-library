**Demonyms** · Zalihan / zalicense

Zaliha is Demeter’s civilian capital in the [[Hollis Belt]]. Lodging houses face convoy yards and vehicle sheds occupy the freight edge. Insulated municipal stores supply crews stranded when the polar routes close.

The name means roughly “Reserve” in Belter Deresian. The name refers to the municipal stores that supply stranded crews when the polar routes close.

Zaliha’s convoy yards serve the northern supply road to [[Desponia]] and the southern road to [[Tartarus]].
