## Overview

deGraf's is a family-owned [[Schil]] company founded in 1432 AP by a line of surgeons and technomages. deGraf's produces civilian and transport vehicles, industrial equipment, appliances, medical devices, prosthetics, specialized components, and most weapon categories. It is officially headquartered on [[Typhon]] for logistical reasons, with its largest industrial presence on [[Tybalt]].

## Migration and expansion

deGraf's established workshops, clinics, and supply contracts on [[Sator]] during the Schil settlement of [[Aldan System|Aldan]]. Its later expansion into Cthel followed the Satorian diaspora, turning the company into a durable link among Schil communities on Avergwyn, Sator, and Typhon. Its modern corporate identity is much broader, but family ownership and institutional memory still connect it to that history.

## Engineering in unstable conditions

Its hydraulics and bare-metal systems are unusually resilient under [[Tybalt]]'s magnetic instability. deGraf's joined the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] through the postwar settlement without participating in the [[Corporate War]]. Dominates the veteran-prosthetics market and privately supplies unbranded prostheses to [[Red Feather]].

## Legacy machinery

Manufactures replacement components for Imperial and [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] machinery, making it a critical dependency for otherwise rival organizations. [[Walter de Graf]] has served as CEO since AC 971.
