# deGraf's

## Overview

deGraf's is a family-owned [[Schil]] company founded by Dona Graf in 1432 AP. deGraf's produces civilian and transport vehicles, industrial equipment, appliances, medical devices, prosthetics, specialized components, and most weapon categories. It is officially headquartered on [[Typhon]] for logistical reasons, with its largest industrial presence on [[Tybalt]].

## Family history

Records from the preceding century place the historical [[House Graf]] under Cane patronage. The family's public genealogy is incomplete.

## Migration and expansion

deGraf's established workshops, clinics, and supply contracts on [[Sator]] during the Schil settlement of [[Aldan System|Aldan]]. Its later expansion into Cthel followed the Satorian diaspora, turning the company into a durable link among Schil communities on Avergwyn, Sator, and Typhon. Its modern corporate identity is much broader, but family ownership and institutional memory still connect it to that history.

A smaller reverse migration later followed the company's contracts and family networks from [[Typhon]] to [[Tybalt]]. Those migrants entered Firan Creole society rather than recreating Cthelic institutions, making Tybalt an offshoot of the corridor rather than an equal historical center.

## Engineering in unstable conditions

Its hydraulics and bare-metal systems are unusually resilient under [[Tybalt]]'s magnetic instability. deGraf's joined the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] through the postwar settlement without participating in the [[Corporate War]]. Dominates the veteran-prosthetics market and privately supplies unbranded prostheses to [[Red Feather]].

## Legacy machinery

Manufactures replacement components for Imperial and [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] machinery, making it a critical dependency for otherwise rival organizations. [[Walter de Graf]] has served as CEO since AC 971.

## Sports sponsorships

deGraf's sponsors [[Estivan Glutwacht]], Raging Dust driver [[Hauke Siefken]], and Sator's [[Sator#Pyre Steig|Pyre Steig]]. The contracts emphasize resilient civilian vehicles, prosthetics, repairable machinery, and the company's historic relationship with Sator. deGraf's also contributes to [[Portas#Los Cuatro Senderos|Los Cuatro Senderos]].
