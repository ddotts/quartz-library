## Overview

Din is the modern name of the Dindessian Empire. It is a constituent of the [[Empire of Avergwyn]]. Din preserves the old imperial line, the central institutions of [[Her Church of Light]], and the political traditions historically associated with Dindessian rule.

## Political tradition

Din traditionally follows a policy of protection by and from power. Its institutions preserve authority as a defense against external domination while treating unchecked authority, rapid concentration, and exploitation as threats in their own right. This position places Din between Schil's strong secular institutions and the Melissian Federation's accelerationism.

The Dindessian imperial line supplies the emperor or empress of the Empire of Avergwyn. The title gives Avergwyn a unified external face, but it does not dissolve the autonomy of the Empire's constituent powers.
