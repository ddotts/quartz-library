The Sauer governed Traun and had longstanding ties to the Feywild. They first dealt with the spread of miasma swamps as a local matter, then sought cooperation among the territories affected.

The family figures mainly in Traun's boundary records and early accounts of the swamps. Its old duties over the affected territory passed to local government.
