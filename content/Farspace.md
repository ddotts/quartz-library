## Overview

Farspace's legally sovereign [[Space Union]] members form the core of the [[Concessionary Bloc]]. They became independent after the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] defeat in AC 855, after which [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] corporations acquired extensive extraction rights, financial claims, infrastructure contracts, and administrative influence within their governments. These systems retain Space Union membership and representation. The CCS agreements shaping their domestic economies have no standing in Union law, creating a continuing distinction between their recognized sovereignty and the practical constraints under which their governments operate.

## Centers of power

[[Procne]] concentrates CCS Consortium administration and finance, [[Barei System|Barei]] controls the main [[Transpace]]–Farspace trade bridge, and [[Firan System|Firan]] supplies industrial production; together their governments anchor the Concessionary Bloc. The [[Alliance of Worlds]] emerged in AC 904 as the principal organized opponent of this structure, drawing support from Deres, Barei, Firan, and [[Aldan System|Aldan]]. [[Tsuiseki]] aid infrastructure and informal political support give Farspace communities access to institutions outside the Bloc. [[Ruby Abyss]]'s simultaneous role as a CCS member and the Tsuiseki's de facto manufacturing arm complicates the independence of that channel.

## Routes beyond the Coast

Slower conventional routes remain valuable because they bypass Coast approaches monitored by Bloc governments and navigation systems supplied by [[Ruby Abyss]]. [[Weiß]] logistics, independent haulers, and old survey paths are consequently used by [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] institutions, local governments, and opposition groups alike.
