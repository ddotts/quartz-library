# Farspace

## Overview

Legally sovereign [[Space Union]] members operating under varying degrees of [[CCS Concessionary Governance]]. Farspace's systems became independent after the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] defeat in AC 855. [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] corporations subsequently acquired extensive extraction rights, financial claims, infrastructure contracts, and administrative influence within their governments. These systems retain Space Union membership and representation. The CCS agreements shaping their domestic economies have no standing in Union law, creating a continuing distinction between their recognized sovereignty and the practical constraints under which their governments operate.

[[Procne]] concentrates [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] administration and finance, [[Barei System|Barei]] controls the main [[Transpace]]–Farspace trade bridge, and [[Firan System|Firan]] supplies industrial production; the arrangement is distributed but mutually reinforcing. The [[Alliance of Worlds]] emerged in AC 904 as the principal organized opponent of this structure, drawing support from Deres, Barei, Firan, and [[Aldan System|Aldan]]. [[Tsuiseki]] aid infrastructure and informal political support give Farspace communities access to institutions outside the CCS. [[Ruby Abyss]]'s simultaneous role as a CCS member and the Tsuiseki's de facto manufacturing arm complicates the independence of that channel.

Slower conventional routes remain valuable because they bypass Coast approaches monitored by local governments and navigation systems supplied by [[Ruby Abyss]]. [[Weiß]] logistics, independent haulers, and old survey paths are consequently used by [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] institutions, local governments, and opposition groups alike.
