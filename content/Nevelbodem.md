**Demonyms** · Nevelian / nevelense

Nevelbodem is a haze basin on [[Quamridge Mass]]. Enclosing slopes trap dense haze above crowded neighborhoods. Roof intakes reach cleaner air above the basin. [[Roetkom]] lies below the last public lift stop.

The name means roughly “Haze Floor” in Firan Creole.

The basin is infamous for the poverty of its lowest neighborhoods, beneath Quamridge’s industrial haze.

The public lift from [[Hamerkom]] ends at the basin rim. Private lifts continue to [[Roetkom]], below the Breath Banks on the upper slope. Nevelput lies beyond the lowest streets.

## Nevelput

Nevelput is an unstable sinkhole in Nevelbodem. Haze collects in an unstable sinkhole whose fresh slumps erase survey marks.

The name means roughly “Mist Pit” in Firan Creole.

## Breath Banks

The Breath Banks are a filtration complex in Nevelbodem. Dust collects on exposed screens at the filtration works. Ducts branch toward different housing levels.

It is named for the habit of budgeting filtered air around the towers.
