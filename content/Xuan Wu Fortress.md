Xuan Wu is the [[North Sea]]'s principal fortress and a center of opposition activity, regional administration, and navigation during the [[Vanguard Corps|Vanguard]] campaign. Its greatest defensive asset is not only armor but continuity: a fixed installation with long observation records can distinguish real movement from the apparent drift caused by the aether field. The fortress also gives the Confederation a place to document Vanguard actions for the [[Space Union]], turning siege records into potential diplomatic evidence.
