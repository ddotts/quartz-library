# Nevelput Rising

Nevelput Rising is an Ironball club founded in the industrial and ventilation districts of [[Quamridge Mass]] rather than its floating spires. [[Good Doctor]] sponsors the club through its Quamridge roots and common-grade equipment.

## Notable players

### Fenna Kooistra

Fenna Kooistra is a Prime League pivot raised in a Firan Creole ventilation district. The broad-shouldered Tiefling has one shortened horn, industrial ear protection, and visible gravity-assist braces. She uses heavy-gravity balance to hold the center and publicly opposes ancestry-based medical classifications. [[Ruby Abyss]] supplies her bare-metal performance sensors.

### Jorrit Fenestra

Jorrit Fenestra is a retired Elite League captain who rose through Quamridge's lower leagues. The gray-skinned Tiefling has a damaged left ear and a permanently reinforced shoulder following repeated ward failures. Good Doctor supplied his armor and rehabilitation. He now campaigns through the [[Alliance of Worlds]] against concealed injury data and discriminatory stadium policy.
