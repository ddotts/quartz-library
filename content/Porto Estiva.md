**Demonyms** · Estivan / estivense

Porto Estiva is Sator’s capital and a freight port in [[Hafenkessel]]. Crew berths stand beside transfer sheds, while insulated waterfront warehouses shelter loads from ash on their way to the [[Rotator Stations]].

The name means roughly “Stowage Port” in Dindessian.

From the port, inland freight takes either the quarry road to the [[Greifen Shelves]] or the valley road to Nietental in [[Aschenhang]].
