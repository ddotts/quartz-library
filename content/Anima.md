An Anima is the semiconscious will developed by a sufficiently massive astral body. It creates the [[Leylines]] that cover its world, sustains magical life, and supplies the power borrowed by its [[Ascended Godhood|Ascended Gods]].

An Anima is the living embodiment of its astral body, not a mortal who achieved godhood. The surviving sources do not establish that every named world has an Anima.
