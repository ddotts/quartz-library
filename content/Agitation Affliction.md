The Agitation Affliction affected at least four towns on routes to the Twin Cities three days or more before the [[Viewport Gate]] opened. It began among a few children and spread. When affected children were brought together, they glowed and opened rifts into an unidentified plane, possibly the Nine Hells or the Abyssal plane; the settlements therefore kept them apart.

Skull-shaped artifacts distributed by an unidentified group caused the affliction. The same group is alleged to have carried out the [[Attack on the Aurora Foundation Ball]]. Known affected towns were Tabe and Roalon on [[Lisden]], and Disl and Kiast on [[Lyore]].
