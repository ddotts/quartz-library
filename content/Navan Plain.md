**Demonyms** · Navanian / navanés, -esa

Navan Plain is an industrial plain on [[Eden]]. Broad fields separate the industrial conurbations of central Eden.

The plain is known across the galaxy for precision magitech manufacturing.

Lines from Navanagar run west to the ferry coast of [[Samudratat]], south to the produce markets of [[Nadipradesh]], and northeast along the ridges to [[Vidyagiri]].

## Navanagar

**Demonyms** · Navanagari / navanagarense

Navanagar is an industrial metropolis in Navan Plain. Fabrication halls occupy whole blocks, and housing follows tram and freight lines. Independent workshops bid for [[Vineflare]] contracts. Apprenticeship charters govern entry to the trades that supply them.

The name means roughly “New City” in Naya Milessian.

Rail lines connect the city to Naukāghāt, Phalmandi, and [[Naya Sarai]].
