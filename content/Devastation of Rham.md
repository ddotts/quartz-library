The Devastation of Rham followed the Duchy of Rham's uprising against the [[Melissian Federation]]. The Federation answered the uprising with an arcane strike of such scale that the event became known by the name of the duchy it devastated. It marked a turning point in pre-space-age warfare on Avergwyn. An act of repression against a single duchy could disturb the source of magic across the world.

The effects extended beyond Rham. The strike altered the state of [[Avergwyn]]'s [[Anima]], and in Schil and along the Dindessian frontier the dead began to return. The link was unknown to those living through the first wave of returns. The persecution of the Returned and the struggle to reintegrate them became part of the political crisis that fueled the [[Great War of Ecria]].
