**Demonyms** · Gadmaidanite / gadmaidanense

Gād Maidan is a lower floodplain on [[Maguuma]]. Silt spreads below the levees and side channels shift after floods. Farmsteads occupy raised ground.

The name means roughly “Silt Plain” in Melissian.

The floodplain is the center of Maguuma’s plant biomass exports, including wood for manufacturing.

Barges from [[Khetvana]] join the main river above Indrapur. The clay seam at Labio Ocre lies on the next bend downstream of the city.

## Indrapur

**Demonyms** · Indrapuri / indrapurense

Indrapur is a river market city in Gād Maidan. Barge ramps face produce markets. Repair shops occupy the lower waterfront and high streets remain dry through seasonal floods.

The name means roughly “City of Indra” in Naya Milessian.

Labio Ocre lies on the next bend downstream of the barge landings.

## Labio Ocre

Labio Ocre is a clay bank in Gād Maidan. River water undercuts an ochre seam on a bend. Shallow scoops mark earlier digging.

The name means roughly “Ochre Lip” in Dindessian.
