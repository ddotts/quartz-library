# Lantern Officers

## Overview

Lantern Officers are civilian observers assigned to some CR2 and CR1 [[Nightwatch]] Companies. They are often retired [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] senators. A Lantern Officer cannot issue commands but may veto an order that violates the [[Nightwatch Charter]].
