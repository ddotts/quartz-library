Emily Montenegro was murdered by [[Good Doctor]] founder [[Abel Hawkins]] in AC 984. Her murder forced Hawkins from corporate leadership, brought [[Lee Brown]] to the position of CEO, reversed Good Doctor's gains against [[deGraf's]], and ended [[DAWAN]]'s friendly rivalry with the company. The surviving source does not yet identify Montenegro's own position or her relationship to Hawkins.
