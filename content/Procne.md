# Procne

- **Capital:** [[Portas]]
- **Population:** Approximately 740 million residents.
- **Species:** [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 76%; Genasi 5%; Dragonborn 5%; Lizardfolk 5%; Tortle 5%; Yuan-ti 4%.
- **Languages:** [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 88%; [[Deresian|Bloc Deresian]] 64%; [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 48%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 38%; [[Deresian|Islandic Deresian]] 15%; [[Planar Languages|Old Sylvan]] 5%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 11%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 17%; [[The Moiety]] 15%; local traditions 12%; atheism 45%.

## Overview

Procne has diverse biomes and a humid, windy climate.

The Republic is a [[Space Union]] member under strong [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] influence. [[Portas]] is home to the Consortium, the [[Universal Credit Bank]], [[Weiß]], [[Stinson's]], [[Silver Meridian]], the [[James Jonastile Group]], and [[Embrace Group]]. Procne supplies logistics, finance, atmospheric products, and luxury commissions. It can meet its basic needs locally and imports machinery and precision parts.

## Appearance

Procne is almost entirely concealed beneath the white and slate-gray spiral of its permanent hurricane; its stabilized eye forms a startling circle of blue calm with Portas glittering at its center.

## The permanent storm

Permanent never-ending storm covering the planet; a single gigantic hurricane dominates the atmosphere. Hurricane eye artificially stabilized near Portas, giving the capital stable weather. Seat of the Republic of Procne ([[Space Union]] member).

Fey inhabited Procne long before its colonization. Few remained when the colonists arrived. Today, a handful of small fey settlements survive in the planet’s harshest reaches, where Old Sylvan is still spoken.

## Minority settlement

Some Genasi, Lizardfolk, Tortle, and Yuan-ti communities descend from populations that lived beyond the stabilized eye before modern colonization, in storm belts, wetlands, reservoirs, and sheltered reaches. Genasi and Dragonborn also arrived through atmospheric harvesting, storm engineering, shipping, and later [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] institutions. The SCF's relocation after the [[Palade Cataclysm]] accelerated migration into Portas, while older populations remained outside the eye and retained claims predating corporate rule.

## The corporate capital

De facto corporate capital of the galaxy: the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] is headquartered here, and the Republic of Procne is a central member of the [[Concessionary Bloc]]. The [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] relocated here after the [[Palade Cataclysm]] (AC 799), and Procne inherited that status when the CCS formed (AC 855). Multiple corporations are headquartered here: [[Stinson's]], [[James Jonastile Group]], [[Embrace Group]], [[Silver Meridian]]; [[Weiß]] established its first official HQ here in AC 965; [[Universal Credit Bank]] has been headquartered here since its founding in 455 AC. Procne's permanent hurricane divides the planet into the stabilized political center inside the eye and the extraction economy operating in the storm beyond it.

## Portas

Portas contains the Republic's seat alongside the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]], [[Universal Credit Bank]], and several corporate headquarters, collapsing the distinction between civic and commercial capitals into one location. The [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]]'s relocation here after the [[Palade Cataclysm]] explains why so much corporate infrastructure survived into the CCS with little interruption. [[Weiß]]'s decision to establish its first official headquarters here in AC 965 further concentrated power shortly before the [[Third CCS Summit]] disaster.

## Atmospheric harvesting

Atmospheric harvesting gives the wider planet an economy not reducible to offices, but the risks and profits of that work are distributed very differently from power inside the eye.

## Regional geography

[[Portas]] stands at the center of the stabilized eye in [[Cornelia]]. At the southeastern rim, covered markets in Arcade serve traffic headed out into the storm. The Portas University of Arcane Arts occupies the sheltered approach to Arcade.

A covered freight road from Arcade reaches Os Peiraos in [[Cinto das Rachas]], an arc of storm-break settlements east of the eye. The terminal and Vila do Rebite sit together at the southern end of the belt. Farther along the arc, the scree shelter of O Colo de Pedra lies uphill from Sotavento.

South of Cornelia, runoff drains into [[As Brañas]]. A raised causeway from the eye reaches the reservoir ridge, and a high service road from Cinto das Rachas enters the catchments above the spillways. L’Orgue de Ferro carries overflow below the main reservoir. La Ramera’s gorge crossing opens briefly at low water.

## Physical characteristics

- **Diameter:** Approximately 9,170 km.
- **Surface gravity:** Approximately 0.80 g.
- **Mean density:** Approximately 6.13 g/cm³.

## Sports

[[Huracan de Cornelia]] is Procne's Ironball club. The world also hosts the **Tempest Chase**, an Aerial X circuit routed through a migrating storm belt whose legal course is declared only shortly before launch. [[BLACK LABEL]] sponsors the race, including the weather ships that mark its abort corridors.
