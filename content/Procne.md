## Overview

Procne has diverse biomes and a humid, windy climate.

## Appearance

Procne is almost entirely concealed beneath the white and slate-gray spiral of its permanent hurricane; its stabilized eye forms a startling circle of blue calm with Portas glittering at its center.

## The permanent storm

Permanent never-ending storm covering the planet; a single gigantic hurricane dominates the atmosphere. Hurricane eye artificially stabilized near Portas, giving the capital stable weather. Seat of the Republic of Procne ([[Space Union]] member).

## The corporate capital

De facto corporate capital of the galaxy: [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] (Collective Commercial Space) is headquartered here; [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] relocated here after the [[Palade Cataclysm]] (AC 799) and Procne inherited that status when the CCS formed (AC 855). Multiple corporations are headquartered here: [[Stinson's]], [[James Jonastile Group]], [[Embrace Group]], [[Silver Meridian]]; [[Weiß]] established its first official HQ here in AC 965; [[Universal Credit Bank]] has been headquartered here since its founding in 455 AC. Procne's permanent hurricane divides the planet into the stabilized political center inside the eye and the extraction economy operating in the storm beyond it.

## Portas

Portas contains the Republic's seat alongside the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]], [[Universal Credit Bank]], and several corporate headquarters, collapsing the distinction between civic and commercial capitals into one location. The [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]]'s relocation here after the [[Palade Cataclysm]] explains why so much corporate infrastructure survived into the CCS with little interruption. [[Weiß]]'s decision to establish its first official headquarters here in AC 965 further concentrated power shortly before the [[Third CCS Summit]] disaster.

## Atmospheric harvesting

Atmospheric harvesting gives the wider planet an economy not reducible to offices, but the risks and profits of that work are distributed very differently from power inside the eye.
