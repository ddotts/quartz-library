# Oomi

- **Population:** No atmospheric residents; approximately 12,000 associated orbital personnel.
- **Species:** Among associated personnel, [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 92%; Aarakocra 8%.
- **Languages:** Among orbital personnel, [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 62%; [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 60%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 48%; [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 15%.
- **Beliefs:** Among orbital personnel, [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 9%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 6%; [[The Moiety]] 10%; local traditions 12%; atheism 63%.

Oomi is a deep-violet gas giant in the [[Ertes System]], crossed by chalk-white atmospheric bands. The moon [[Aaaros]] follows a close, rapid orbit above its clouds. Its Aarakocra personnel belong to a wider gas-giant station chain descended largely from migrants trained on Eden, Procne, and earlier atmospheric facilities.

## Racing

Oomi hosts the **Gasline Deadshot**, an Aerial 0 circuit through the habitat's exposed industrial frame, where pilots slingshot around refinery spars and abandoned transfer pipes. [[Weiß]] sponsors the race and its emergency tug crews.
