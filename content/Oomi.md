- **Population:** No atmospheric residents; approximately 12,000 associated orbital personnel.
- **Languages:** Among orbital personnel, [[Common Language|Common (Union)]] 62%; [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 60%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 48%; [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 15%.

Oomi is a deep-violet gas giant in the [[Ertes System]], crossed by chalk-white atmospheric bands. The moon [[Aaaros]] follows a close, rapid orbit above its clouds.
