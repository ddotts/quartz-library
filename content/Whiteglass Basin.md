**Demonyms** · Whiteglass / whiteglassense

Whiteglass Basin is a glacial drainage basin on [[Demeter]]. Glacial channels join across a broad basin. Clear pools form where meltwater reaches the ice margin.

The glacial pools and channels are among Demeter’s natural landmarks and draw visitors from the inhabited belt.

The basin lies just south of the inhabited [[Hollis Belt]]. A spur from Zavetrie reaches the pools and channels at the ice margin.
