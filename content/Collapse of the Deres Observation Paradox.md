# Collapse of the Deres Observation Paradox

## Overview

The [[Broken Isles]] formed when at least fifty parallel versions of one system collapsed into the prime material plane. The collision produced the central non-spinning [[Supervoid]], an ionized aether nebula, planetary fragments, and astral bodies held in pseudo-stasis. Before 1800 AP, local time flowed at different and fluctuating rates, making a shared chronology nearly impossible.

Time began normalizing around 1800 AP and matched the prime material plane on average by AC 100. Rationalist theories argue that [[Potemkin 1]]'s observation of nearby [[Barei System|Barei]] caused the [[Broken Isles]] to become fully entangled with the prime plane, collapsing the paradox through observation and cognition.
