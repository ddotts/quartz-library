# Oriol Zubia

Oriol Zubia is a retired Avergwyn Fairy Aether champion from an old local community with Dindessian public education and family institutions. He has copper wings and close-cropped black hair. [[Boxe–Cane–Kaste]] sponsored his career. He now serves as a public magical-safety commissioner for festivals, demonstrations, and amateur duelling.
