The Excelsior Research Wing is a research body aboard [[The Odyssey]]. Cathar mage and information theorist [[Rho Lovelize]] worked there before presenting the theory that established [[Impressionism]] at the AC 276 [[Inauguration of Portas University of Arcane Arts]].
