Arcane Teleportation Jumps are scaled and modified castings of the Teleport spell. An [[Arcane Engine]] uses them to teleport an entire container—usually a vehicle—rather than a small group of individuals. Routes are charted through the [[Astral Sea]] with an [[Astral Charter]].
