The Entresuelo is a network of old mines, industrial passages, and black-market spaces beneath [[Valgrath]]. Its principal sections include an old commercial district, a resonance plant, abandoned transit rails, and infrastructure connected to the [[Viewport Gate]].

Pirates serving [[Marc Davos]] occupied the district after violence in its market escalated into a massacre. The death or displacement of much of the cell ended the immediate occupation, but securing the wider mine network was expected to take months.
