## Overview

The [[Vanguard Corps|Vanguard]] ([[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] private security apparatus, formed from the remains of the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] military) is currently running campaigns in the North Sea. [[Cofradia]] (first and major corporation from the [[Broken Isles]]; key Vanguard coordinator in this region) oversees the Vanguard's North Sea operations. North Sea is currently an active theater of Vanguard campaigns coordinated by Cofradia, so descriptions of it as merely remote or austere understate its political importance.

## An outward frontier

It is the least charted of the four Seas and faces outward toward unexplored [[Farspace]]. [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] accounts describe its campaigns as efforts to extend settlement and eliminate hostile entities encountered beyond the established routes. Frontier troops known as the [[Protectors of the North]] secure established settlements and routes. Some of their personnel are subsequently recruited by the [[Vanguard Corps]]. [[Xuan Wu Fortress]] anchors Confederation presence in a region where CCS private security operates with the equipment and institutional lineage of the defeated [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] military.

## Security and sovereignty

The [[Vanguard Corps|Vanguard]] describes its actions as security operations conducted under Confederation and [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] authority. The [[Alliance of Worlds]] and affiliated Deresian groups describe the same campaign as the armed enforcement of the concessionary order. Fragmented terrain and unstable astral positions favor armed groups with local charts, while the Vanguard brings capital ships, corporate logistics, and inherited [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] doctrine. [[Red Feather]]'s [[Red Feather Conflict-Zone Vow|conflict-zone vow]] means it continues operating here although the Confederation does not remit the normal service fee. Available accounts indicate that Hospitaller and Coast infrastructure supports this work, but the precise funding arrangement is not public.
