The [[Firan System|Firan]] Confederation represents both [[Tybalt]] and [[Quamridge Mass]] in the [[Space Union]] and belongs to the [[Concessionary Bloc]]. Its political seat is on Tybalt, while much of its industrial wealth comes from Quamridge. [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] extraction rights across both worlds make that imbalance a source of structural dependence.
