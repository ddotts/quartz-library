**Demonyms** · Vandermere / vandermereño, -a

The Vandermere Lowlands are a district of lakes, rivers, and flood meadows on [[Lisden]]. Channels link farms across the low ground. Ferries use the higher banks. [[Prealis]] stands at the cliff edge above [[Moonlake]].

Vandermere is roughly “From the Lake” in Sylvan.

The Lowlands are Nova’s leading tourist destination. Ancient groves survive from the Eladrin Kingdoms, and [[Prealis]] preserves Lisden’s most extensive pre-spaceflight old town.

The monorail crosses from [[Bellecour Weald]] at [[Vile]] and serves [[Prealis]] and Vletdam. It connects the lowlands to [[Eikenbroek Canopy]] to the northeast and the [[Lavaadil Backwaters]] to the south.

## Vletdam

**Demonyms** · Vletdamer / vletdamense

Vletdam is a lowland boat city in Vandermere Lowlands. Boatyards and quays occupy a web of channels. Ferries link neighborhoods separated by water.

The name means roughly “Barge Dam” in Sylvan.

The monorail connects Vletdam to Eikesteg in [[Eikenbroek Canopy]], [[Lavaadil]] to the south, and [[Prealis]] on the cliff edge.
