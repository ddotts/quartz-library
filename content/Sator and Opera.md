## Overview

[[Sator]] and [[Opera]] form a binary pair: one habitable but volcanic, the other airless and rich in minerals. The [[Rotator Stations]] between them built all three Potemkin-class motherships.

## Appearance

**Sator.** A charcoal-and-rust world wrapped in ash bands, with volcanic chains glowing along its nightside and settlements clustered on cooler highlands.

**Opera.** A metallic-gray globe streaked with exposed copper, cobalt, and iridescent mineral strata.

## A paired industrial system

The binary arrangement places habitable but volcanic [[Sator]] beside resource-rich, uninhabitable [[Opera]], allowing the [[Rotator Stations]] to draw labor and material from the same tightly coupled environment. The Rotator Stations are the largest shipyard in known space and the birthplace of all three Potemkin motherships. Their importance predates both the [[Universal Federation]] and the [[Space Union]]. [[Vineflare]] was headquartered on Sator from AC 97 to AC 700, overlapping the Potemkin program and tying its later generation-ship division directly to [[Aldan System|Aldan]]'s industrial base.

## The end of generation ships

The winding down of [[Vineflare]]'s generation-ship division after [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]] departed in AC 209 marks the point at which [[Blue Coast|the Coast]], rather than another mothership program, became the assumed future of expansion.
