# Bogdan Koen

Bogdan Koen is a Firan Creole Tiefling and active Mixed War Arts fighter from a segregated lower district of [[Quamridge Mass]]. Maroon-skinned, with swept horns cut flat at the tips, he wears compact armor and a visibly repaired casting gauntlet. He transitions between grappling, short blades, and low-power spellwork. [[BLACK LABEL]] sponsors him as the sport's most extreme current star.
