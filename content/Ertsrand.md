**Demonyms** · Ertsrander / ertsrandense

Ertsrand is a mineral escarpment on [[Quamridge Mass]]. Exposed mineral seams stripe a steep scarp cut into working terraces. Haul roads make wide turns beneath the rock.

The name means roughly “Ore Rim” in Firan Creole.

The scarp contains some of the most abundant and profitable jewel mines in the galaxy.

Service lifts reach the escarpment from the [[Aders Highlands]]. Below the working terraces, Burdenrails carry ore down to Wisselwerf in [[Hamerkom]].

## Milhojas Bluffs

Milhojas Bluffs is a layered mineral bluff in Ertsrand. Folded mineral bands change color from bed to bed across the weathered bluff.

Milhojas means “a thousand layers” in Dindessian and refers to the folded mineral bands.

## Burdenrails

Burdenrails is an assisted freight line in Ertsrand. Counterweighted loading cradles assist heavy freight along the scarp.

Haulers named the site for the rails that carry their heaviest loads.

The rails carry ore from the working terraces down to Wisselwerf in [[Hamerkom]].
