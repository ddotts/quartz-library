The Inauguration of [[Portas University of Arcane Arts]] took place in [[Higys System|Higys]] in AC 276, eight years after [[The Odyssey]] reached the system. During the ceremony, Cathar mage and information theorist [[Rho Lovelize]] applied the findings of [[Psionics|psionics]] back to magic and presented the argument that established [[Impressionism]].
