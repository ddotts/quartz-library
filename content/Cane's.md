Cane's is a formalwear, office, and legal-services retailer in [[Vile]] owned by [[Boxe–Cane–Kaste]]. It supplies court and office goods, magical accessories, and specialist legal services. Its regular manufacturers are [[Weiß]], [[Stinson's]], [[DAWAN]], [[Cofradia]], and [[Vineflare]].
