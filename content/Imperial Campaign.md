# Imperial Campaign

## Overview

The [[Imperium]] invaded neighboring systems, beginning with [[Irlan Mining System|Irlan]] and later contesting [[Kilnt System|Kilnt]] and [[Godon System|Godon]]. The [[Universal Federation]]'s superior shipbuilding capacity proved decisive in the [[Transpace]] approaches. Defeat in AC 697 dissolved the Imperium, produced the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] from its corporate interests, and prompted creation of the [[Space Union]].
