Lavaadil is a settlement on [[Lisden]] and a major symbol of the [[Nova Civil War]]. An [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] attack on a [[Nightwatch]] patrol outside the settlement triggered the war in AC 939. Near the conflict's end, [[Artemis Araval]] and [[Amanda Asgae]] jointly defeated an extremist Orda attempt to seize Lavaadil and hold [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] diplomats hostage.
