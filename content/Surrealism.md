## Response to expressionism

Surrealism arose by taking [[Expressionism]] beyond the composition of personal rituals. Expressionism allowed practitioners to choose and combine methods; surrealism argued that the method itself is part of reality and can therefore be changed. No actuator is fixed, and reality—including the chosen method—is the medium.

## The Eden formulation

The school received its first stable formulation during the AC 889 [[Eden Summit of Universal Academics]]. [[Anais Lamar]], a researcher at the [[Eden University of the Universe]], designed a containment working for the conflicting material and astral measurements observed near the [[Supervoid]]. During the test, the working altered the rule by which its own stages were sequenced rather than merely changing the contained phenomenon. Lamar's account treated this not as a malfunction but as evidence that actuators, media, and rules are all conventions within reality.

## Status

Surrealist explanations remain difficult to test, but many consider them a natural conclusion of [[Rho Lovelize|Lovelize's]] [[Impressionism]].
