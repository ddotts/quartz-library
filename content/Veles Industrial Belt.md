**Demonyms** · Velesian / velesiano, -a

Veles Industrial Belt is an interior industrial belt on [[Typhon]]. Rails, power trunks, and service lanes wind around structural ribs. Trusses carry cranes and freight between districts that cast, machine, and test heavy assemblies. Older haul lines end at abandoned shelves, and newer tracks double back around later construction. Disused lines still give crews access to power junctions and gravity regulators. Closing a freight span can delay production in several districts.

It is named for the broad arc of industrial districts that follows Typhon’s inner shell.

The belt exports specialized machinery and structural assemblies. Its workshops concentrate on mechanical engineering and construction.

The short branch from [[Brücker Pforten]] leads to Caer Gof. Perlenfäden hangs below a sheltered span of the Rib, and an inner-shell freight span crosses to the Hanging Acres in [[Orșovei Reach]].

## Caer Gof

**Demonyms** · Gofan / gofense

Caer Gof is a shellward workshop town in Veles Industrial Belt. Workshops line an inner ridge outside the main interchange. Repair courts open beneath braces, and tool stores share walls with housing.

The name means roughly “Smith's Town” in Schil.

## The Rib

The Rib is a fractured ridge in the Veles Industrial Belt, named for the way it projects into the Hollow. Loose material gathers against the intact braces below its dark fissure. The Cthelic name Spaltgrat means roughly “Split Ridge,” and the Islandic Deresian Raspuklo Rebro means roughly “Broken Rib.”

The ridge juts into the Hollow like an exposed rib.

## Perlenfäden

Perlenfäden is a suspended mineral drizzle in Veles Industrial Belt. Amber droplets hang in fine strands. Mineral beads thicken at the tips and leave flecks on nearby surfaces.

The name means roughly “Strings of Beads” in Cthelic.
