Prototype Protection Act was passed in response to opposition to [[DAWAN]]'s [[Trained Awakened Intelligence]] process. Established protections for prototype automata whose temporary awakened consciousness is recorded to train later units. Automata-rights movements drove the legislation.

The Act sharpened legal disputes around [[Source Language|Source]]. A copied instruction may remain property while a trained model or expressive statement may receive protection, making translation evidence of agency and consent rather than a purely technical operation.
