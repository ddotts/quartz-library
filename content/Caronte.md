Caronte is a living Astral Dreadnought and the nomadic geographical center of the [[Astral Sea]]. The center travels with Caronte. Navigators projecting into the Astral Sea can use the plane to chart routes for [[ATP Jumps]] while retaining control of their material bodies. Those traveling physically through the plane must contend with its non-Euclidean currents and a geography whose center moves. Caronte is one of the reasons direct passage is exceptionally dangerous.

## Campaign notes

### Avergwyn

- The Caronte accounts describe a prison and a command protocol that bound a soul to the dreadnought.
- Caronte was associated with Civitas Animarum, where soul crystals allowed the living to contact the dead and potentially restore them.
- The Reigar Elise and Korise offered different ways to restore control of Caronte. The eventual command succession is unresolved.
