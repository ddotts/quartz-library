# Portas

Portas is the capital of [[Procne]], in [[Cornelia]] beneath the stabilized eye. The Republic and the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] sit here alongside the headquarters of the [[Universal Credit Bank]], [[Weiß]], [[Stinson's]], [[Silver Meridian]], [[James Jonastile Group]], and [[Embrace Group]]. Central skyscrapers rise toward the middle of the eye, with covered squares below and low freight halls near the edges.

## Sports

Portas hosts **Los Cuatro Senderos**, a rotating singular-combat festival with separate brackets for Iron Fist, Steel League, Aether Duels, and Mixed War Arts before a noncompetitive closing exhibition. The event is jointly sponsored by the members of the [[Collective Commercial Space]].
