The Harts of Obern built their fortune through gold mining and became bankers to the Schil court. Atel Hart appears in recovered correspondence from the siege of the city.

The family is remembered in Obern as an example of the old connection between mining wealth and royal finance. Its court banking office lapsed with the old crown's financial arrangements.
