## Overview

The Eden Summit of Universal Academics is a recurring academic gathering hosted by the [[Eden University of the Universe]]. Two of its meetings became decisive events in the history of [[Magic's Schools of Thought|magical theory]].

## AC 436 summit

At the AC 436 summit, [[Godel Soret]] reconciled [[Impressionism]] with the inherited ritual systems of [[Classicism]] and established [[Romanticism]]. Eden's connection to the [[Blue Coast]] allowed the university to compare Core archives with traditions carried through [[Transpace]].

## AC 889 summit

At the AC 889 summit, [[Anais Lamar]] presented a self-modifying containment working developed for conflicting measurements near the [[Supervoid]]. Her interpretation of its behavior gave [[Surrealism]] its first stable formulation.
