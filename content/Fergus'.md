Fergus' is a [[Valgrath]] gym, forge, and retail chain owned by [[James Jonastile Group]]. It provides bodybuilding services, traditional smithing, heavy armor, and close-combat equipment, particularly for monks, fighters, and barbarians. Its regular manufacturers are [[Good Doctor]] and [[deGraf's]].
