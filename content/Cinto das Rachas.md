**Demonyms** · Rachalian / rachalense

Cinto das Rachas is a storm belt on [[Procne]]. Crosswinds sweep a corridor of raised breaks and sheltered freight berths. Crews use vanes and flags to judge outdoor routes.

The name means roughly “Gust Belt” in Dindessian.

The corridor offers the most reliable living conditions beyond the center of Cornelia’s stabilized eye. Organizations outside the CCS settle here when they cannot afford space in [[Portas]].

The covered road from Arcade in [[Cornelia]] reaches Os Peiraos beside Vila do Rebite at the southern end of the belt. A high service road continues toward [[As Brañas]], above its spillways. O Colo de Pedra lies uphill from Sotavento.

## Vila do Rebite

**Demonyms** · Rebitan / rebitense

Vila do Rebite is an industrial freight city in Cinto das Rachas. Reinforced yards surround fabrication sheds. Covered lanes connect shops with freight bays.

The name means roughly “Rivet Town” in Dindessian.

## Sotavento

**Demonyms** · Sotaventan / sotaventense

Sotavento is a storm-break settlement in Cinto das Rachas. A heavy wall with few openings on its exposed face shields the low housing and landing lanes to leeward.

The name means roughly “Wind Shelter” in Dindessian.

## O Colo de Pedra

O Colo de Pedra is a sheltered scree fan in Cinto das Rachas. Large boulders in a scree fan give work crews space to wait out the wind.

The name means roughly “Stone Lap” in Dindessian. The boulders form a pocket deep enough for a whole crew to crouch out of the wind.

## Os Peiraos

Os Peiraos is a weatherproof freight terminal in Cinto das Rachas. Reinforced walls and drainage trenches protect its recessed loading bays. Silver Meridian contractors and independent carriers share handling capacity.

The name means roughly “The Quays” in Dindessian. Haulers use the old word for quays for the sheltered loading aprons.

The terminal sits beside Vila do Rebite at the belt’s southern end and receives traffic from Arcade in [[Cornelia]].
