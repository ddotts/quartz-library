**Demonyms** · Cornelian / corneliano, -a

Cornelia surrounds the artificially stabilized eye above [[Portas]]. Calm extends beyond the city before giving way to rain-dark roads, raised shelters, and weather stations.

[[Portas]] is the seat of the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] and houses many of its members’ headquarters. The [[Space Commerce Federation]] relocated here after the [[Palade Cataclysm]], and the CCS inherited that seat.

From Arcade on the southeastern rim, a covered freight road reaches Os Peiraos in [[Cinto das Rachas]]. A raised causeway leads south from the eye to the reservoir ridge in [[As Brañas]].

## Arcade

**Demonyms** · Arcadan / arcadense

Arcade is a sheltered urban center in Cornelia. Covered markets keep freight moving through storms. Civil offices coordinate housing for evacuees.

It is named for the covered walkways linking the city’s markets.

Arcade sits at the southeastern rim of the eye, where the covered freight road leads to Os Peiraos.
