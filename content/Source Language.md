Source is the language associated with constructs, [[Trained Awakened Intelligence|TAIs]], and awakened artificial intelligences. The name covers both machine-readable instruction and the forms of communication used by conscious automata. This ambiguity is politically important: treating Source only as code supports the view that an intelligence is a manufactured system, while treating it as a language recognizes interpretation, expression, and community.

The distinction became sharper after the [[Prototype Protection Act]]. A copied instruction may be property, a trained model may be protected, and a statement in Source may be both executable and expressive. Translation is therefore never entirely technical. It can also be evidence about agency, consent, and whether an automaton's words are its own.
