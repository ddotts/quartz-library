## Overview

The Melissian Federation is an Avergwyn polity with dual membership in the [[Empire of Avergwyn]] and the [[Universal Federation]]. It retains its own institutions while participating in both larger political systems.

Its accelerationist politics favor rapid expansion, experimentation, and institutional change. The old Dindessian imperial line presents the [[Empire of Avergwyn]] as a safeguard against those policies, while Melissian leaders often regard imperial restraint as an obstacle to necessary development.

## Dual membership

The Melissian Federation gives the Universal Federation a direct institutional presence on [[Avergwyn]], although the Empire of Avergwyn itself does not belong to the Universal Federation. Disputes that begin within Avergwyn can therefore continue through the [[Confederation of Avergwyn and Aldan]], the Universal Federation, and the [[Space Union]].

The two larger powers nevertheless remain partners within the [[Old World Powers]] and cooperate more closely with each other than either does with the [[Concessionary Bloc]].
