# Dome Mostyn

## Overview

Dome Mostyn is [[DAWAN]]'s headquarters on [[Ping]] after the corporation abandoned [[Dome Yüceil]] following the AC 970 [[Plane Shift Disaster]]. The relocation preserved DAWAN's presence on the planet while making dome design, evacuation, and planar monitoring major corporate-security concerns.
