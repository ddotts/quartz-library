The Corporate War was fought from AC 727 to AC 855 between the [[Space Commerce Federation]] and a coalition including the [[Space Union]], [[NOX ULTRA]], and the [[Bugyn Orda]]. After AC 800 it became operationally inseparable from the [[Nova Independence War]]. The SCF's defeat dissolved the federation, returned [[Ertes System|Ertes]] to the [[Universal Federation]], and enabled the formation of [[Collective Commercial Space]].
