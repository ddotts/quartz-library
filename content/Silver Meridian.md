# Silver Meridian

Silver Meridian is a [[Procne]] finance, legal, transportation, and logistics group founded in the late AC 900s by former [[Collective Commercial Space]] administrators. It invests heavily in Nova and is led by [[Virion Isalor]].

## Sports sponsorships

Silver Meridian sponsors [[Night Safīna]] and Aerial 1 pilot [[Leonor Bellver]], supporting transport, legal administration, and interworld logistics around their appearances. It also contributes to [[Portas#Los Cuatro Senderos|Los Cuatro Senderos]].
