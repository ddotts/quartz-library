# Silver Meridian

## Overview

Silver Meridian is a [[Procne]] finance, legal, transportation, and logistics group founded in the late AC 900s by former [[Collective Commercial Space]] administrators. It invests heavily in Nova and is led by [[Virion Isalor]].
