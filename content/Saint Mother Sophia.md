Saint Mother Sophia heads the [[Cathars]] and serves on the [[Tsuiseki Trinity]] aboard [[The Odyssey]]. She oversees the order's scientific, magical, and investigative work, including research into the [[Universal Puzzle]], ancient artifacts, and extraplanar phenomena.
