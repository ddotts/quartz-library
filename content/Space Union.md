# Space Union

## Overview

Space Union is a galactic alliance for collective security, diplomacy, and trade. Space Union was created in AC 697 after the [[Imperium]]'s defeat to prevent any one power—particularly the [[Universal Federation]]—from dominating known space. Members:

- [[Universal Federation]].
- [[Federation of Avergwyn and Aldan]].
- [[Republic of Cthel]].
- [[Deres Confederation]].
- [[Firan Confederation]].
- Federation of [[Barei System|Barei]].
- Republic of [[Procne]].

The former [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] was expelled and dissolved after its AC 855 defeat. The nine standing seats include two for the [[Universal Federation]], two for the [[Federation of Avergwyn and Aldan]], and one for each remaining member. Five votes form a majority. [[Cthel System|Cthel]] often becomes the deciding member when [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]]-aligned systems and the old federations split.

No member may hold more than thirty percent of standing committees. Its capital and seat of government is the [[Omnicron]], following the [[Tsuiseki]] precedent of mobile sovereignty. The Union fleet is larger than any single power's fleet but smaller than the combined [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] fleets and combined [[Universal Federation]] forces.

Space Union maintains cooperative anti-piracy relations with [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] and increasingly hostile formal relations with the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]]. Space Union issues [[Seals of Legitimacy]], the standard physical galactic currency. The [[Omnicron]] follows the [[Nonary Rotation]], visiting the systems represented by the nine seats over a nine-year cycle. The route has been interrupted only once, in AC 854 during the final year of the [[Corporate War]].
