# Open Channel 9

## Overview

Open Channel 9 is an encrypted advocacy and coordination stream operated by the [[Alliance of Worlds]] over [[Collective Commercial Space]] trade frequencies. Authorized receivers extract voice and data while other listeners hear ordinary commerce or static. The Alliance treats it as a public-information service; the CCS classifies it as propaganda and prioritizes identifying its listeners.
