## Programme

The Aether Programme was a joint research effort conducted by [[Vineflare University]] and the newly founded [[Sator Polytechnic]] from AP 1698 to AP 1705. Its researchers established the aether plane as a plane of potential rather than another domain within [[Classicism|classical]] magic.

## Potemkin 2 and realism

[[The Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] entered development in AP 1700 while the programme was underway, allowing revisions of aether theory to be tested against an active generation-ship project. The programme supplied the experimental basis for [[May Farday]]'s AP 1711 synthesis of [[Realism]].
