# Inquisition

## Overview

The militant and political arm of the [[Tsuiseki]]. Composed primarily of nuns and monks, although it recruits warriors and adventurers from outside those traditions. Inquisition conducts military operations, black operations, and counterintelligence in support of Tsuiseki policy.

Its Counter Intelligence unit also monitors the other [[Tsuiseki]] arms. Inquisition holds limited extradition rights across [[Space Union]] systems. The scope of those rights remains contested after disputes at the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] Summits of AC 904, 933, and 970. [[Ruby Abyss]] supplies its military laser weapons under an exclusive contract. The weapons share an optics platform with the company's civilian and mining products.

Some Inquisitors reportedly carry [[Vineflare Heirloom Series|Vineflare Heirloom Series weapons]], which are produced in limited numbers and bonded to one owner through an arcane registry.
