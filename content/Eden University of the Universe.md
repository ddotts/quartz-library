The Eden University of the Universe is a university on [[Eden]] and the host of the recurring [[Eden Summit of Universal Academics]]. [[Godel Soret]] established [[Romanticism]] there in AC 436, and university researcher [[Anais Lamar]] gave [[Surrealism]] its first stable formulation there in AC 889.
