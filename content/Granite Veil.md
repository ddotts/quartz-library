# Granite Veil

Granite Veil is an [[Eden]] construction and extraction company founded in the early AC 900s. It invests heavily in Nova and is led by [[Miller Grey]].

## Sports sponsorships

Granite Veil sponsors [[Éclats de rail]] and Lyore's [[Lyore#Kirstal Trove|Kirstal Trove]]. Both partnerships connect its construction and extraction business to reinforced venues, surveyed routes, and mineral-working communities. Granite Veil also contributes to [[Portas#Los Cuatro Senderos|Los Cuatro Senderos]].
