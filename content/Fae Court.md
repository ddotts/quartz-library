The Fae Court is the political institution formed by the victorious Houses of the [[Fae Alliance]] after the [[Lisden Civil War]]. It retained the [[Bugyn Orda]] as [[Lisden]]'s Protectors of Peace but granted the Orda no throne, regency, or governing seat. The Court later entered [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] politics, ending its planetary rule while preserving the strong Orda loyalties of many Houses.
