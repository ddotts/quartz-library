## Overview

The Empire of Avergwyn is the principal political structure of [[Avergwyn]] and a constituent of the [[Confederation of Avergwyn and Aldan]]. Despite its name, it operates as a confederation among [[Din]], [[Schil]], the [[Melissian Federation]], Liveria, Filran, Y'malor, and Kasian. Each retains its own institutions and political identity.

## The imperial line

The old Dindessian imperial line traditionally rules the Empire and represents Avergwyn to external powers. The imperial title presents a single center of authority to potential rivals and provides a safeguard against the accelerationism of the Melissian Federation. In practice, the throne governs through agreements among the constituent powers rather than direct administration of the whole planet.

The Dindessian political tradition treats power as something society must be protected by and protected from. The imperial line is expected to preserve enough authority to resist external domination while restraining the exploitation or uncontrolled concentration of that authority at home.

## Internal balance

The [[Melissian Federation]] also belongs to the [[Universal Federation]], while the Empire of Avergwyn does not. That dual membership gives the Universal Federation a direct institutional presence inside the Empire and produces recurring conflicts of interest. The Empire's other members provide separate centers of continuity, making its unity a negotiated political arrangement rather than a single national identity.
