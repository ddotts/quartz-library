Shellside is the surface region and shaft approaches on [[Typhon]]. **Demonyms** · shellie / corticense

The rocky exterior and its older pressure stations meet the inhabited interior along the access shafts of [[Brücker Pforten]] and other Shaft regions.

Surface stations decide which vessels can reach the shafts. Refusing entry can be fatal to a vessel that needs shelter or assistance.

Oberwacht handles passengers and cargo for the shaft down to Tiefenbrück in [[Brücker Pforten]].

## Oberwacht

Oberwacht is a surface transfer station in Shellside. A sealed concourse connects shuttle pads to an upper lift terminal. Older pressure modules house crews, while covered bays stage cargo and shelter stranded arrivals when the shaft closes.

The name means roughly “Upper Watch” in Cthelic. The name refers to the crews who keep watch over arrivals and shaft traffic.
