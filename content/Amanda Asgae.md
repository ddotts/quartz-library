Amanda Asgae was an Akhmad of the [[Bugyn Orda]] during the [[Nova Civil War]]. Her clan joined [[Artemis Araval]]'s [[Nightwatch]] Company to defeat the extremist seizure of [[Lavaadil]]. She was subsequently authorized to speak for [[Burkhan Luu Buga]] in the peace negotiations.
