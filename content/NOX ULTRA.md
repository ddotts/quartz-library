# NOX ULTRA

## Overview

NOX ULTRA began as an alliance of mercenaries, merchants, and workers who arrived in Nova as representatives of old-world powers. NOX ULTRA declared independence and developed into a mobile, democratic space nation. NOX ULTRA signed its founding charter in AC 731, four years into the [[Corporate War]]. Representatives of thirteen old-world labor and trade cohorts were the original signatories.

NOX ULTRA fought beside the [[Bugyn Orda]] against the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] during the [[Corporate War and Nova Independence War]]. NOX ULTRA fought the Orda over [[Nough System|Nough]] during the [[Nova Civil War]], then reached a neutral peace in AC 961. Since AC 961, governs the entire [[Nova Sector]], including [[Lisden]]. The [[Fae Court]] remains part of NOX politics but no longer governs the planet.

NOX ULTRA recognizes the independent [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] as [[Lisden]]'s Protectors of Peace without placing it inside the NOX chain of command. [[Nightwatch]] and Orda forces cooperate in space control and anti-piracy operations. The capital is the ship [[Zizek]]. NOX deliberately avoided establishing a planetary capital to preserve mobile government.

NOX ULTRA uses competitive multiparty elections and proportional representation in the Senate. Parties are normally organized around regions, trade guilds, or cultural communities rather than broad ideological platforms. Coalitions therefore exist in several layers. This fragmented system reflects a population in which roughly half of the residents are semi-nomadic and live aboard mobile settlements or vessels.

Unlike the [[Universal Federation]], corporations hold no Senate seats; cultural and regional parties do. The Chancellor may serve no more than three five-year terms. NOX holds [[Space Union]] observer status but refuses membership on sovereignty grounds.

Its tax base depends on [[Haulers Guild]] tithes and salvage rights over [[Corporate War]] wreckage.
