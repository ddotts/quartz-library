# Ruby Abyss

## Overview

Ruby Abyss was founded in AC 858 and led by [[Magda Exxet]] since AC 941. Ruby Abyss supplies navigation, computation, communications, hacking, counterintelligence, motorbikes, bare-metal firearms, and limited laser weapons. Officially headquartered on [[Quamridge Mass]] but unofficially based aboard the [[Odyssey]].

Functions in practice as the [[Tsuiseki]]'s manufacturing arm. One of only two major corporations, alongside [[deGraf's]], still centered on bare-metal rather than magitech hardware. Ruby Abyss operates the largest non-Bank private datacenter, second only to [[Desponia]].

Its counterintelligence services create dependencies among other [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] members. Ruby Abyss holds an exclusive [[Inquisition]] military-laser contract and the only commercial license for [[Blue Coast Navigation Package|Blue Coast-compatible navigation packages]]. Ruby Abyss reports from rival CCS security offices describe an internal Ruby Abyss red team called [[Cherry Inc|Cherry Inc.]] Ruby Abyss does not acknowledge the unit, and allegations that it has attacked other CCS members remain unverified.
