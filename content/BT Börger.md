BT Börger is a [[Valgrath]] performance retailer and events service owned by [[James Jonastile Group]]. It supplies musical instruments, performance magic, costumes, and special effects, and organizes parties. Its regular manufacturers are [[Stinson's]], [[DAWAN]], [[Ruby Abyss]], and [[deGraf's]].
