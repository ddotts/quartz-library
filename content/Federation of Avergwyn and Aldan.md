# Federation of Avergwyn and Aldan

## Overview

The Federation of [[Avergwyn]] and [[Aldan System|Aldan]] is a [[Space Union]] member representing the Core and Aldan systems. It holds two of the Union's nine standing seats. Its separate federal identity complicates the [[Universal Federation]]'s historical claims over the older Melissian expansion and Potemkin legacy.
