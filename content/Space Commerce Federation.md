# Space Commerce Federation

## Overview

Space Commerce Federation is a corporate federation created from the [[Imperium]]'s surviving commercial interests in AC 697. Initially based in [[Ertes System|Ertes]], [[Cthel System|Cthel]], and [[Higys System|Higys]]. Attempted to control [[Farspace]] and the [[Nova Sector]].

Space Commerce Federation fought the [[Space Union]], [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]], and the [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] during the [[Corporate War and Nova Independence War]]. [[Palade]] served as its headquarters until the AC 799 Cataclysm made the planet uninhabitable. The SCF then relocated to [[Procne]]. Defeat in AC 855 dissolved the federation, secured Nova and [[Farspace]] independence, returned [[Ertes System|Ertes]] to the [[Universal Federation]], and produced the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]].

Member corporations retained legal personhood, allowing the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] to become a practical continuity vehicle within months. Captured SCF capital frames later became the [[Khan]] and [[Zizek]]. The [[Treaty of Independence of Farspace]] prohibited the restoration of SCF governmental authority. Whether later CCS concessions reproduce the form of control prohibited by the treaty remains a continuing legal and political dispute.
