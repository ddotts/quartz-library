The Hollow is the habitable interior on [[Typhon]]. **Demonyms** · Hollower / huequense

Typhon’s inhabited inner shell surrounds the nano-sun. Routes from [[Brücker Pforten]] lead toward the [[Veles Industrial Belt]] and [[Orșovei Reach]].

The Hollow’s trade and cultural life center on its constituent regions and their links to the surface.
