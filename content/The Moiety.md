The Moiety, also called the Church of the Anima, is a religion that treats care for a planet and its [[Anima]] as the highest duty.

Its clerics preserve the moral teachings and social conduct believed to maintain the health of a planet's soul. Its druids protect the physical environment and therefore the planet's body. These are complementary institutions rather than competing explanations of the faith.

## Demographics

The Moiety has approximately **950 million members** across the surveyed resident and associated populations around AC 998. This estimate excludes people aboard major ships and artificial habitats that were not assigned local religious shares, and it does not count [[Eoccenon]]'s last-known or [[Palade]]'s historical population as current members.

Its largest estimated memberships are on [[Avergwyn]] at about 305 million, [[Eden]] at about 165 million, and [[Procne]] at about 111 million. Its highest current local shares are on Avergwyn at 21%, Procne at 15%, and [[Lisden]] and the [[Supervoid]] area at 12% each.
