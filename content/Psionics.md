## Overview

Psionics is the practical discipline produced when [[Rationalism|rationalist]] principles were tested without conventional ritual. Its institutional origin lies aboard [[The Odyssey]], whose researchers could compare spellcasting near planetary leylines with the weakened conditions of open space.

## Hopper's experiments

In AC 231, Cathar [[Nadia Hopper]] isolated practitioners from ritual implements and conventional magical cues during the passage from [[Firan System|Firan]] to [[Higys System|Higys]]. The trials expanded existing psionic powers and supported the claim that sufficiently disciplined understanding could shape reality directly. They also exposed a problem for rationalism: successful psionic effects did not always behave as if aether were their only medium. The trials were conducted through the [[Mǔ shī Technical Institute]] and supplied the findings later developed into [[Impressionism]].
