**Demonyms** · Aderian / aderense, -esa

The Aders Highlands are a district of mineral ridges and elevated spires on [[Quamridge Mass]]. Service roads cling to the slopes below the private towers and connect work yards on narrow ledges. [[Wolkennaald]] rises above the haze.

The spires house Quamridge’s wealthiest residents and its most lucrative commerce. Most chipmakers and jewelers have their headquarters there.

Service lifts descend from ridge landings beneath the floating spires to the working terraces of [[Ertsrand]].
