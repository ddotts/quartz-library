Godel Soret was the scholar who established [[Romanticism]] at the AC 436 [[Eden Summit of Universal Academics]]. Soret reconciled [[Impressionism|impressionist]] accounts of mutable reality with the inherited rituals preserved by [[Classicism]], treating those rituals as contextual models rather than isolated laws.
