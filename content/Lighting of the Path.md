## Overview

The founding event of the [[Tsuiseki]]. Scientists, mages, researchers, and humanitarian workers aboard [[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] underwent a religious conversion after weeks assisting people endangered by the [[Broken Isles]]' planar collapse. Details are preserved only in recordings treated as sacred texts by the Chapel.

## The Odyssey

[[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] was renamed the Odyssey after the event.
