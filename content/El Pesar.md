El Pesar was a secret intelligence service of the king of [[Schil]], reconstructed from private court records and campaign accounts. Lilith Rowen acted as its liaison to King Morales Orchidee. Rumor identified its leader, known only as the White, as one of the [[Fomori]]. The record is too fragmentary to establish the leader's nature or the service's full reach.

Togail Bhreugan, the astral figure associated with deception, was directly connected to El Pesar. Accounts of Bhreugan's mask describe it passing information and tools to agents who usually acted independently. When Bhreugan appointed an avatar, the avatar coordinated their work. The exact relationship between the mask network and the king's service remains obscure.

El Pesar disappears from the record as an identifiable service after the [[Great War of Ecria]]. It survives in studies of the king's private intelligence and the political use of esoteric patronage.
