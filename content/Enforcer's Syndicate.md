# Enforcer's Syndicate

## Overview

The Enforcer's Syndicate is the [[Haulers Guild]] organization responsible for recovering delinquent debts and missing cargo. Its accountants, surveyors, and breakers carry a stamped chit rather than a uniform or badge. Every Guild dock is required to cooperate with the bearer of such a chit.
