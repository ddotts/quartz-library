# Blue Coast Node

## Overview

A [[Blue Coast]] node is a several-kilometre-wide teleportation circle that connects a star system to the Blue Coast network. Each node is anchored to asteroids joined into an artificial body large enough to sustain ley lines, the circle's arcane drain, and its shipyards. Local governments control the approaches, the [[Tsuiseki]] claim authority over the node itself, and [[Ruby Abyss]] supplies the licensed navigation layer required for commercial travel.
