# Atlas Group

Atlas Group is an [[Eden]] passenger-travel and tourism company founded in the early AC 900s. It operates throughout [[Transpace]] and at selected [[Farspace]] destinations and is led by [[Troy Wellington]].

## Sports sponsorships

Atlas Group sponsors [[Sarai Soar Sabha]], Aerial 1 pilot [[Nadita Solanki]], Raging Dust driver [[Amrita Bramwell]], and Eden's [[Eden#Zenith Marg|Zenith Marg]]. The program ties passenger travel and tourism to events across Eden's landscapes while limiting individual contracts to two athletes. Atlas Group also contributes to [[Portas#Los Cuatro Senderos|Los Cuatro Senderos]].
