# Nereid

## Overview

Fully oceanic planet.

> **Appearance:** Nereid is a nearly seamless cobalt sphere covered by immense spiral storms; its mobile artificial islands appear on the nightside as constellations that subtly change position.

Multiple artificial islands, many of them mobile. One of the largest organic matter farms and oil extraction operations in the galaxy. With no natural land, every settlement is also infrastructure. Mobile islands can follow biological harvests, avoid depleted extraction zones, or reposition around weather and trade routes.

[[Newland–Yuriel]] is based here and specializes in marine farms and offshore extraction platforms, linking local settlement design directly to one of the galaxy's largest preserved-food supply chains. Organic-matter farming and oil extraction compete for the same ocean space; movement of an island can therefore be an economic decision, a political protest, or both. Dependence on constructed land gives shipwrights, platform engineers, and food producers unusual influence over daily governance.
