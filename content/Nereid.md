# Nereid

- **Capital:** [[Plovmreža]]
- **Population:** Approximately 330 million residents.
- **Species:** [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 79%; Lizardfolk 7%; Tortle 7%; Triton 7%.
- **Languages:** [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 76%; [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 62%; [[Deresian|Bloc Deresian]] 48%; [[Planar Languages|Primordial]] 32%; [[Planar Languages|Deep Speech]] 20%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 9%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 6%; [[The Moiety]] 10%; local traditions 21%; atheism 54%.

## Overview

Nereid is an ocean world. Its only natural land above water is a small volcanic archipelago that began to emerge less than 200 years ago.

[[Newland–Yuriel]] operates marine farms and offshore platforms alongside independent fleets. Nereid exports preserved food, biomass, oil, and shellwork. Its artificial islands and offshore platforms rely on imported structural metals and machinery.

## Appearance

Nereid is a nearly seamless cobalt sphere covered by immense spiral storms; its mobile artificial islands appear on the nightside as constellations that subtly change position.

## Mobile islands and industry

Multiple artificial islands, many of them mobile. One of the largest organic matter farms and oil extraction operations in the galaxy. Most settlements are built on floating or fixed marine infrastructure. Mobile islands can follow biological harvests, avoid depleted extraction zones, or reposition around weather and trade routes.

## Aquatic settlement

Lizardfolk, Tortle, and Triton migrants formed the first large communities able to live beyond Nereid's artificial surface islands. Tritons established deep settlements, Tortle communities organized around mobile islands and seasonal circuits, and Lizardfolk settlements bridged shallow-water infrastructure with surface platforms. Successive crews sponsored relatives and constructed permanent habitats. Their communities remain because Nereid permits aquatic civic life independent of surface governments, although extraction concessions continue to contest control of that space.

## Newland–Yuriel

[[Newland–Yuriel]] is based here and specializes in marine farms and offshore extraction platforms, linking local settlement design directly to one of the galaxy's largest preserved-food supply chains. Organic-matter farming and oil extraction compete for the same ocean space; movement of an island can therefore be an economic decision, a political protest, or both. Dependence on constructed land gives shipwrights, platform engineers, and food producers unusual influence over daily governance.

## Regional geography

The warm surface gyre of [[Rema Thermí]] carries [[Plovmreža]] along its seasonal circuit, including calls over the submerged shelf of [[Banco delle Reti]]. Surface tenders follow the gyre out to [[Isole di Cinza]], where Santa Nerina’s harbor sits at the current’s outer edge. At Banco’s outer shelf break, traffic changes depth. Submersibles descend the basin wall into [[Vathi Misthosi]], stopping at Sottoporto on its ledge. Dnomera Station surveys the seafloor below.

## Physical characteristics

- **Diameter:** Approximately 8,030 km.
- **Surface gravity:** Approximately 0.70 g.
- **Mean density:** Approximately 6.13 g/cm³.

Lower gravity reduces structural loads on its artificial islands.

## Sports

[[Plovmreža Paponi]] represents Nereid in Ironball. [[Newland–Yuriel]] sponsors the team and both of the world's major racing circuits.

### Regata Sopraonda

The **Regata Sopraonda** is a Raging Dust race for amphibious ground vehicles across floating causeways, wave-battered platforms, and temporary bridges. Its route is rebuilt around the season's currents.

### Elica Sommersa

The **Elica Sommersa** is an Aerial X circuit held beneath the sea. Racers use pressure-rated craft to follow illuminated gates through kelp farms, flooded infrastructure, and deep-water turbine fields.
