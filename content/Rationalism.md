## Response to realism

Rationalism arose in response to [[Realism]] after [[Applied Magic|applied magic]] became technically mature. Realism located magical potential in the aether and explained how rituals shaped it, but left the ritual itself as an unexplained interface. Rationalists asked why understanding a ritual mattered and whether comprehension could replace performance.

## Cajal's theory

At the AC 138 [[Core System Convention of Arcane Arts]], [[Sevrin Cajal]] of the [[Piesmorados University of Magic and Philosophy]] argued that thought is the actuator and aether the medium. Under this model, understanding potential allows the mind to shape it through disciplined intention and the mental representation of an effect.

## Reception and confirmation

The theory was initially criticized for describing trained intuition in philosophical language without producing a practice separable from ritual, because its practical demonstrations relied on a wide breadth of rituals that produced the same results. Cajal's theories were not widely accepted until [[Nadia Hopper]]'s experiments in [[Psionics|psionics]] confirmed its claims about the actuator.
