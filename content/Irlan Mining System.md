## Overview

- **Blue Coast node:** AC 479 ([[The Odyssey]]).

Irlan Mining System's extraction rights are shared between the [[Universal Federation]] and the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] on an 80–20 split in favor of the Federation.

## Appearance

From a distance, Irlan resembles a loose constellation of stripped asteroids, dust plumes, work lights, and the orange glow of processing furnaces rather than a conventional inhabited system.

## Extraction and conquest

Irlan's defining fact is political: the [[Imperium]] invaded it at the start of the [[Imperial Campaign]] for its resources, and its extraction rights are now divided between the Universal Federation and CCS Consortium. The Federation holds eighty percent and the Consortium twenty percent. [[The Odyssey]] created its Coast node in AC 479, placing the system on the network before the [[First Galactic War]] but long before the CCS existed. Modern extraction can therefore rely on infrastructure that predates the current arrangement and on claims inherited through Imperium and [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] corporations.

## Power in the stations

With no inhabited world or civic government, power is concentrated in stations, work crews, and the Federation and Consortium institutions that control access to the mined material.
