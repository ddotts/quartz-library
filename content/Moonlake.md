Moonlake lies below the cliffs of [[Prealis]] on [[Lisden]].

Reed beds fringe the lake beneath [[Prealis]], at the cliff edge of the [[Vandermere Lowlands]]. Moss-dark stairs descend to stone landings in sheltered coves. Fey named the lake when passage to Lisden’s Feywild was common and an aquamarine moon shone above that plane’s Lisden.
