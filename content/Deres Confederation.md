The Deres Confederation provides the shared civic structure of the [[Broken Isles]] and represents the system in the [[Space Union]]. Its authority is complicated by strong local Sea identities, incompatible historical chronologies, and [[Collective Commercial Space]] [[CCS Concessionary Governance|concessionary governance]]. Critics in the [[Alliance of Worlds]] argue that extensive CCS participation in its administration limits its representativeness.
