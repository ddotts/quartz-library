The Deres Confederation provides the shared civic structure of the [[Broken Isles]], represents the system in the [[Space Union]], and belongs to the [[Concessionary Bloc]]. Its authority is complicated by strong local Sea identities, incompatible historical chronologies, and [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] concessionary governance. Critics in the [[Alliance of Worlds]] argue that extensive CCS participation in its administration limits its representativeness.
