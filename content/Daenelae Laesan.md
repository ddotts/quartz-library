Daenelae Laesan was chief executive of [[Cofradia]] during the [[Third CCS Summit]]. Laesan disappeared during the [[Plane Shift Disaster]] at [[Dome Yüceil]] in AC 970. The available records do not establish whether Laesan died or remained within the shifted dome.
