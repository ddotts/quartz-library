# Nova Civil War

## Overview

[[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] and the [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] fought over [[Nough System|Nough]] amid [[Nightwatch]] formalization, patronage corruption, piracy, and conflicting loyalties inside the incorporated [[Fae Court]]. The immediate trigger was an AC 939 Orda attack on a Nightwatch patrol outside [[Lavaadil]]. Both sides contained pro-war and pro-settlement factions. Nightwatch bureaucracy and decentralized Orda leadership allowed extremists to escalate.

Most fighting concerned control of space. Both main forces limited surface warfare, although semi-independent factions continued ground attacks. Nova Civil War is an extremist [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] clan attempted to seize [[Lavaadil]] and hold [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] diplomats hostage to prevent peace. [[Artemis Araval|Captain Artemis Araval]]'s Company and [[Amanda Asgae|Akhmad Amanda Asgae]]'s clan defeated the takeover together.

[[Amanda Asgae|Asgae]] was then authorized to speak for [[Burkhan Luu Buga|the Burkhan]] during negotiations. The Burkhan neither endorsed nor denounced the war and kept the [[Khan]] out of major engagements. The AC 961 settlement left [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] governing all Nova, preserved the [[Fae Court]] as a political institution, and recognized the independent [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] as [[Lisden]]'s non-governing Protectors.

Pirate activity rose during the conflict under alleged [[Wiston Baltasar|Pirate King Wiston Baltasar]]. He was captured after the peace and is held at [[Tartarus]].
