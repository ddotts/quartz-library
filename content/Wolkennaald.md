**Demonyms** · Wolkennaaldan / wolkennaaldense

Wolkennaald is the capital of [[Quamridge Mass]], on an elevated spire in the [[Aders Highlands]]. Exterior service lifts pass viewing rooms that overlook the industrial haze.

The name means roughly “Cloud Spire” in Firan Creole.

The spire rises above the [[Aders Highlands]], where ridge landings give access to the service lifts down to [[Ertsrand]].
