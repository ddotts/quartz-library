Reybar Pharma is a [[Procne]] pharmaceutical company founded in the early AC 700s. It is the only pharmaceutical organization that rivals [[Red Feather]] and is a major supplier across [[Transpace]], [[Farspace]], and the [[Broken Isles]]. [[Robert Reybar]] leads the company.
