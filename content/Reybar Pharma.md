# Reybar Pharma

Reybar Pharma is a [[Procne]] pharmaceutical company founded in the early AC 700s. It is the only pharmaceutical organization that rivals [[Red Feather]] and is a major supplier across [[Transpace]], [[Farspace]], and the [[Broken Isles]]. [[Robert Reybar]] leads the company.

## Sports sponsorships

Reybar Pharma sponsors Iron Fist competitor [[Jacob Leer]], supporting regulated recovery care and anti-doping research around his bouts. It also contributes to the CCS joint sponsorship of [[Portas#Los Cuatro Senderos|Los Cuatro Senderos]].
