# Tartarus

## Overview

Tartarus is high-security prison; located at the south pole of [[Demeter]]. Tartarus is the highest-security prison in known space and currently holds [[Wiston Baltasar]], the alleged Pirate King captured after the [[Nova Civil War]] peace. Its polar location supplies natural isolation while the [[Kilnt System|Kilnt]] Coast node makes controlled transfers possible from across known space.

The prison's population is politically heterogeneous: Federation enemies, war criminals from earlier conflicts, and detainees transferred by other [[Space Union]] powers may share the same institution without sharing a legal regime.
