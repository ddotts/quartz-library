The Abyssal Maw, commonly called the Abyss, is a fissure through [[Eoccenon]]'s obsidian surface and mantle of [[Infernal Iron]] toward its uncharted core. Its depth and origin are unknown. Nothing has been observed emerging from it, and no expedition sent into it has returned.

Its name reflects a common confusion between demons and devils, their respective planes, and Eoccenon's Infernal Iron. It should not be confused with the Abyssal plane.
