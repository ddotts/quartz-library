**Demonyms** · Nadipradeshan / Nadipradeshense

Nadipradesh is a farming and river freight district on [[Eden]], with towpaths along its navigable river. Yards occupy high bends and civic centers stand at road crossings.

The name means roughly “River Country” in Melissian.

Nadipradesh supplies most of Eden’s food and exports fruit and other delicacies offworld. Its markets, orchards, and river scenery make it a destination for culinary tourism.

Phalmandi sends produce north by rail to Navanagar in [[Navan Plain]] and west by barge to the [[Samudratat]] coast.

## Phalmandi

**Demonyms** · Phalmandi / phalmandí

Phalmandi is a wholesale market city in Nadipradesh. Wholesale stalls open before dawn. Cold rooms behind the produce sheds hold public reserves for use when ordinary supplies are interrupted.

The name means roughly “Fruit Exchange” in Melissian.

Produce leaves north by rail for Navanagar or west by barge for [[Samudratat]].

## Purānī Chhāyā

Purānī Chhāyā is a ravine fruit grove in Nadipradesh. The old fruit grove fills a sheltered ravine outside Phalmandi’s commercial orchards. Its canopy shades the path and keeps the ground damp.

The name means roughly “Old Shade” in Melissian.
