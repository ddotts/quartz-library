Mach is a bay city in [[Schil]] and a center of [[Her Church of Light]]. Quayside cellars serve the wine trade, and a processional route climbs from the waterfront toward the churches.

The [[House Rumasa|Rumasa]] oversaw churches and wine, while the Barroco became associated with oil. During the [[Great War of Ecria]], the Rumasa and [[House Piesmorados|Piesmorados]] led efforts to reintegrate the Returned.

The ferry from Mach crosses the bay to Wiednausen. The road toward Elumere passes the campus of the [[Piesmorados University of Magic and Philosophy]] outside town.
