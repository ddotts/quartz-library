## Overview

The Standard Cascade Circuit is the AP 1764 component standard created by [[Iria Solavel]] at [[Sator Polytechnic]]. It expresses high-level spells as programmed chains of lower-level evocations and transmutations using shared notation and testing procedures.

## Industrial impact

Its components could be manufactured, inspected, and replaced independently, turning the theory of [[Realism]] into mature [[Applied Magic|applied magical]] engineering. The standard drove magic's second industrial revolution and was mature by the time [[The Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] departed in AC 0.
