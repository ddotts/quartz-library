Obern is the industrial heart of [[Schil]]. By the 14th century AP, its gold mines had made the Harts wealthy enough to finance the crown. Catacombs ran beneath the Casco Viejo, with approaches through the old library, church, mining museum, post office, and the former printworks of the [[Verkünder von Schil]]. Recovered letters and press fragments indicate that these routes mattered to the defense and evacuation of the old town during the [[Great War of Ecria]].

Obern remains a working industrial city. The surviving mine works and Casco Viejo are the clearest traces of its early economy. The old shafts and catacombs help explain how the city grew and why its oldest districts were difficult to defend.

## Industry and access

All of Obern’s historical gold workings are inactive. Modern freight follows the pass roads to the city’s industrial yards. The Casco Viejo still has traces of the shafts and catacombs that once ran beneath its streets.

The Paso de Obern road descends to [[Eigach]], and a market road runs from Obern to the saddle markets of the [[Maelor March]].

## Campaign notes

### Avergwyn

- At Obern’s castle, Captain Bladeward and Catar Olivia received separate reports as defenders sealed the underground approaches to the Casco Viejo.
- Atel Hart appealed to Reynold Rumasa after aberrations emerged from the catacombs. The defenders sealed underground entrances into the old town and sheltered civilians.
- Captain Bladeward and Catar Olivia reported spontaneous animal transformations, failed messenger service, and distress near the north barricade.
- The western castaways and the paladin Blod were credited with rescuing printworks staff and helping relieve the siege. The press fragment breaks into warnings about a thinning veil.
- Lewis Philips identified Pons as Ocibum's cherub and accused golden-masked figures of mistreating it. Underground scenes record a dispute between Xiran and Celia and machinery cycling a black substance. The source of the siege remains unresolved.
- Separate correspondence records a full booking at the Hotel Breavermi and an unusual order for mining supplies to be sent to Eigach.
