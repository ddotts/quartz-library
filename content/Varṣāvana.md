**Demonyms** · Varshavanite / varshavanense

Varṣāvana is an upland forest on [[Maguuma]]. Tall rainforest crowns close over steep watersheds. Footpaths follow the divides toward [[Vanlekh]].

The name means roughly “Rain Forest” in Melissian.

The uplands are Maguuma’s cultural center and export some of the galaxy’s most sought-after woods.

The Mud Harp stands on the outlet toward [[Khetvana]]. Portage tracks carry traffic around the falls into the basin below.

## The Mud Harp

The Mud Harp is a stepped waterfall in Varṣāvana, where falling water sounds across broad ledges streaked with silt. Portage tracks climb past the spray.

It is named for the sound of falling water and the silt it carries.

The portage runs past the falls and down into [[Khetvana]].
