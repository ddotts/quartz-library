**Demonyms** · Retarian / retariense

Banco delle Reti is a submerged shelf on [[Nereid]]. Fast tidal streams cross a submerged shelf of limestone ridges, shell gravel, and eelgrass. Fish spawn in sheltered shallows and juvenile rays shelter in the grass. [[Plovmreža]] calls here during its seasonal circuit.

The name means roughly “Net Bank” in Bloc Deresian.

The shelf accounts for around three quarters of Nereid’s biomass exports, supplying food markets across settled space.

Below [[Plovmreža]]’s seasonal stop, submersibles cross the outer shelf break and descend to Sottoporto in [[Vathi Misthosi]].
