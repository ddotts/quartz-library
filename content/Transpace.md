## Overview

Majority controlled by the [[Universal Federation]]. [[Cthel System]] is an exception — independent Republic. Transpace contains most Universal Federation territory but also independent Cthel and [[Irlan Mining System|Irlan]], whose extraction rights are divided eighty–twenty between the Federation and the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]]. It is a political patchwork rather than a uniform Federation interior. [[Ertes System|Ertes]], Cthel, and [[Higys System|Higys]] formed the [[Imperium]] and later the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]]. [[Godon System|Godon]] remained continuously within the Universal Federation but became a principal target during the [[Imperial Campaign]]. Their present borders preserve the outcomes of both the Imperial Campaign and [[Corporate War]].

## Political and strategic centers

[[Eden]] is the [[Universal Federation]]'s capital and the main political center, while [[Kilnt System|Kilnt]] supplies critical shared infrastructure through [[Tartarus]] and [[Desponia]]. The region is where the [[Old World Powers]], [[Space Union]] law, and [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] interests overlap most directly. Transpace's central conflict is institutional rather than territorial: the Universal Federation controls most systems, the Space Union supplies shared legitimacy, and CCS companies remain embedded through contracts and legacy assets. A crisis can therefore be fought through procurement, data access, and shipping long before fleets move.
