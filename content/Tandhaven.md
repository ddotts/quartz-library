**Demonyms** · Tandhavenite / tandhavenense

Tandhaven is Tybalt’s capital and mechanical freight port in the [[Veen Reach]]. Work berths and tool counters face the cargo yards, while repair sheds carry cranes over the water. The [[Firan Confederation]] is seated here.

The name means roughly “Tooth Harbor” in Firan Creole.

From the freight port, cargo can move downstream to [[Geelwaard]] or onto the eastern tributary toward [[Kronen Belt]].
