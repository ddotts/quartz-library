# ATP Jump Drive

## Overview

An ATP jump drive is the technology on which the [[Blue Coast]]'s teleportation system is based. A [[Blue Coast Node]] scales the same principles to a several-kilometre-wide circle supported by an artificial asteroid anchor and its ley lines.
