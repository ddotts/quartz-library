## Overview

The sworn members of the [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]]: anyone personally pledged to an Akhmad or [[Burkhan Luu Buga|the Burkhan]]. Members receive substantial financial support and protection, extended to their families for one generation. Distinct from the flock, whose members receive Orda protection through residence or contract without swearing allegiance.

## Other affiliations

Some [[Haulers Guild]] Black Ledger operatives reportedly serve both organizations.
