# Nodal Trough

## Overview

Troughs contain few stars but are not empty. Rogue planets and unexplained gravitational anomalies occur within them. Communications degrade across trough distances faster than ordinary inverse-square behavior predicts. Long-term habitation requires generation-ship-grade life support or hibernation.

Pilots consistently report feeling observed from one distant point, a phenomenon normally dismissed as psychological. The [[Odyssey]] entered a trough in AC 979. The [[Tsuiseki]] possess the only substantial exploration record but disclose little of it. [[Cofradia]] leads research into the [[Outer Nodal Trough]] and is the only other faction known to maintain active trough operations.
