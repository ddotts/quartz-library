# Imperium

## Overview

Imperium is an alliance of [[Ertes System|Ertes]], [[Cthel System|Cthel]], and [[Higys System|Higys]] under [[Redmond Daeson|Lord Redmond Daeson]]. Imperium was formed after Ertes, Cthel, and Higys won independence from the [[Universal Federation]] in AC 546. Daeson was crowned on [[Palade]], which became the Imperial capital.

Expanded into neighboring systems during the [[Imperial Campaign]] of AC 600–697, particularly targeting resource-rich [[Irlan Mining System|Irlan]] and [[Kilnt System|Kilnt]] and contesting [[Godon System|Godon]] with the [[Universal Federation]]. The Imperial line ended nominally when [[Redmond Daeson|Daeson]]'s grandson died near the end of the campaign. The Imperium was defeated and dissolved in AC 697. Its corporate beneficiaries formed the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]].

Used an inverted-Y emblem representing its founding systems. The mark remains visible on Imperial-era structures. Codified its law in the [[Daeson Directive]], portions of which survived through [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] corporations and remain persuasive precedent in [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] courts. [[Godon System|Godon]] remained under [[Universal Federation]] control, [[Cthel System|Cthel]] and [[Higys System|Higys]] remained independent, and [[Ertes System|Ertes]] retained the industrial complex that seeded the SCF.

Imperium is a few dozen Imperial-loyalist families still preserve the [[Redmond Daeson|Daeson]] genealogy and meet annually inside [[Typhon]]. Intelligence agencies consider them harmless, but [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] legal departments employ some as historical archivists.
