# Battle of Aaaros

## Overview

The Battle of [[Aaaros]] was fought in AC 532 during the [[First Galactic War]] on and around the moon Aaaros. [[Redmond Daeson]]'s performance there established him as the leading commander of the independence war and prepared the way for his later coronation as ruler of the [[Imperium]].
