# Weiß Demiplane Storage

## Overview

[[Weiß]] demiplane storage is a proprietary system combining remote delivery with storage outside ordinary material space. It is particularly valuable in the [[Broken Isles]], where destinations may be physically close but separated by unstable planar relationships.
