Leylines are paths of energy produced by an astral body's [[Anima]]. They sustain a world's magical life and supply its [[Ascended Godhood|Ascended Gods]]. Their absence in open space greatly weakens communication, teleportation, recovery, and other magic when travelers are not on an astral body capable of supporting them.
