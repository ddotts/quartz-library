# Broken Isles

## Overview

[[Space Union]] member operating under [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] extraction concessions and extensive commercial influence.

- **First arrival:** [[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]], AC 105 (node).
- **Blue Coast node:** AC 105 ([[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]]).

The Broken isles consists of a constellation of fragments of planets and planes of existence, split and scattered, floating in a nebula of air and ionised aether. In the center of the nebula it's [[Supervoid|the supervoid]], a non-spinning black hole. Orbiting around the nebula its an F-type sun.

Within the nebula, astral bodies including [[Supervoid|the supervoid]], stay in a pseudo-stasis, drifting at a very reduced rate, understood to be caused by the friction in space-time created by the charged aether nebula, which affects not the material plane but the celestial anchors in the astral plane of each of the astral bodies. The broken isles are the result of a massive planar collapse, resulting in the merge of the star system located at that point on at least 50 parallel material planes, onto the prime material plane. Time tracking prior to 1800 is virtually impossible since the time dilation crated by the planar collapse made time flow at a different and fluctuating rate in comparison with the rest of the the prime material plane.

Starting in 1800, the time dilation started to match the rest of the plane, reaching average rates by 100 AC. This is known as the [[Collapse of the Deres Observation Paradox]], and lead to rationalist theories proposing that the Broken Isles entangled upon the material plane once it was observed and congnised when the [[Potemkin 1]] arrived on the nearby system of [[Barei System|Barei]]. The Broken Isles are not the remains of one destroyed system but the superimposed fragments of at least fifty parallel versions of it, suspended in an ionized aether nebula around [[Supervoid|the Supervoid]]. The central non-spinning black hole and the F-type sun orbiting the nebula do not behave like an ordinary binary. Their astral anchors are slowed by the charged aether even though matter still moves on the material plane.

North, South, East, and [[West Sea]] are practical names for regions of a three-dimensional field of planetary and planar fragments, not literal oceans or simple quadrants. The [[Deres Confederation]] supplies a common civic structure, but long isolation and historically uneven time flow make local Sea identities and chronologies unusually strong. The Confederation is a [[Space Union]] member operating under [[CCS Concessionary Governance]], while the [[Red Feather]] continues to provide services without payment because the Broken Isles are treated as an active conflict zone.

[[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]]'s humanitarian work here led directly to the [[Lighting of the Path]] in AC 119, making Deres foundational to the [[Tsuiseki]] despite the extensive influence [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] institutions exercise through concessions, finance, and administrative relationships. Calendar reconciliation remains politically charged even after average time flow matched the prime material plane. Dates before AC 100 can be locally valid but mutually incompatible, allowing ancient [[Cofradia]] claims and modern CCS extraction contracts to rely on different chronologies. The four Seas differ considerably in accessibility and political conditions. The South contains the principal routes to [[Transpace]], the North borders unexplored [[Farspace]], the East is the main theater of armed opposition to CCS concessions, and the West receives little CCS investment.

Communities descended from different collapsed planes may share a navigational region without sharing an origin or historical chronology. Several Deresian members of the [[Alliance of Worlds]] dispute the representativeness of the Confederation because [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] personnel occupy much of its administration.
