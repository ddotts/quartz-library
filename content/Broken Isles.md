## Overview

The Broken Isles belong to the [[Space Union]], though [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] extraction concessions give corporations extensive influence there.

- **First arrival:** [[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]], AC 105 (node).
- **Blue Coast node:** AC 105 ([[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]]).

## The fragmented system

The Broken Isles are fragments of planets and planes suspended in a nebula of air and ionized aether. [[Supervoid|The Supervoid]], a non-spinning black hole, lies at its center; an F-type sun orbits the nebula.

## The Observation Paradox

The Isles formed when at least fifty parallel versions of one star system collapsed into the prime material plane. Charged aether appears to slow the celestial bodies through their astral anchors, leaving them in a kind of pseudo-stasis. Time flowed unevenly after the collapse, making dates before 1800 AP difficult to reconcile with the rest of the prime material plane.

Time began to normalize around 1800 AP and matched the prime material plane on average by AC 100. This change is known as the [[Collapse of the Deres Observation Paradox]]. One rationalist theory links it to [[Potemkin 1]] reaching the nearby [[Barei System|Barei]] system: observation may have anchored the Isles more firmly to the material plane. The central black hole and orbiting F-type sun still do not behave like an ordinary binary.

## The four Seas

North, South, East, and [[West Sea]] are practical names for regions of a three-dimensional field of planetary and planar fragments, not literal oceans or simple quadrants. The [[Deres Confederation]] supplies a common civic structure, but long isolation and historically uneven time flow make local Sea identities and chronologies unusually strong. The Confederation is a [[Space Union]] member operating under [[CCS Concessionary Governance]], while the [[Red Feather]] continues to provide services without payment because the Broken Isles are treated as an active conflict zone.

## Pilgrimage and politics

[[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]]'s humanitarian work here led directly to the [[Lighting of the Path]] in AC 119, making Deres foundational to the [[Tsuiseki]] despite the extensive influence [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] institutions exercise through concessions, finance, and administrative relationships. Calendar reconciliation remains politically charged even after average time flow matched the prime material plane. Dates before AC 100 can be locally valid but mutually incompatible, allowing ancient [[Cofradia]] claims and modern CCS extraction contracts to rely on different chronologies. The four Seas differ considerably in accessibility and political conditions. The South contains the principal routes to [[Transpace]], the North borders unexplored [[Farspace]], the East is the main theater of armed opposition to CCS concessions, and the West receives little CCS investment.

Communities descended from different collapsed planes may share a navigational region without sharing an origin or historical chronology. Several Deresian members of the [[Alliance of Worlds]] dispute the representativeness of the Confederation because [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] personnel occupy much of its administration.
