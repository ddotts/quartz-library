Portas University of Arcane Arts is a university in [[Higys System|Higys]], inaugurated in AC 276, eight years after [[The Odyssey]] reached the system. At its [[Inauguration of Portas University of Arcane Arts|inauguration]], [[Rho Lovelize]] presented the argument that established [[Impressionism]].
