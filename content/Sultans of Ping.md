The Sultans of Ping are the political power claiming [[Ping]] within the [[Alliance of Worlds]].

## The Aspects of Man

The Sultans rule Ping's desert cities and are the local equivalents of [[Ascended Godhood|Ascended Gods]] for the Aspects of Man. Around AC 997, Rio Atorix held the Blue title in the north.

Caleb's _The Sultans of Ping_ lists six hereditary titles. He associates Crimson with the southern tribes, Gray with a construct, Black with the Ancient Ones, Blue with the northern nation, White with Grua'knoith, and Yellow with the stars. He also describes five rulers of Nila Aureum and a Chamber whose guardians required claimants to demonstrate knowledge of their traditions. The city itself remains disputed.
