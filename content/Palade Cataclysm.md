[[Palade]] Cataclysm is an arcane catastrophe of unknown cause devastated Palade, rendered its surface uninhabitable, and forced the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] headquarters to [[Procne]]. The SCF accused the [[Universal Federation]], which denied involvement but refused humanitarian aid. Postwar investigations dropped the accusation for lack of evidence without identifying the true cause.
