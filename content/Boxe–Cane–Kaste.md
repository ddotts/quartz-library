# Boxe–Cane–Kaste

Boxe–Cane–Kaste is an [[Eden]] finance and legal firm founded in the early AC 700s by several families, including the Canes, whose histories reach the 1000s AP. It maintains ties to demonology and the divine, owns [[Cane's]], and is led by [[Theodore Cane]].

## Cane family history

The historical [[House Cane]] of Elumere was known for diabolism and the manipulation of patron contracts, interests also found in the firm's history. Public records do not preserve the intervening Cane genealogy.

## Sports sponsorships

Boxe–Cane–Kaste sponsors Aether duelists [[Adela Morante]] and retired champion [[Oriol Zubia]], as well as Ping's [[Ping#Ping Spelljammer|Ping Spelljammer]]. Its legal and financial staff specialize in magical liability, warding contracts, and patronage rights. The firm also contributes to [[Portas#Los Cuatro Senderos|Los Cuatro Senderos]].
