[[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] concessionary governance describes formally sovereign systems in which Collective Commercial Space institutions exercise sustained influence over public administration or policy. That influence can operate through extraction concessions, credit, infrastructure agreements, security contracts, and corporate participation in government. CCS publications prefer terms such as commercial integration or partnership, while critics use corporate capture or puppet rule.
