The Hecate Network is an [[Umbra]] creation resembling a network of interconnected demiplanes, although it lies entirely on the material plane. Fractal spatial distortion makes it effectively unchartable, and few expeditions return from its deeper levels. The [[Nox]] created it to escape the [[Lux]].

## Campaign notes (Astronaufragos)

One of the entrances to the Hecate Network is within the Charon Chains. [[Laudna Bolgause]] is currently lost within the network after an alleged attack involving the same skulls that started the [[Agitation Affliction]] attacks.
