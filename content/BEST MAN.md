# BEST MAN

BEST MAN is a Cthelic Construct and Aerial X pilot recognized as a resident through Hollow institutions rather than owned by a racing stable. Their matte ceramic frame has an asymmetrical optic cluster and a visible manual release between body and cockpit. They excel at [[Typhon#Sonnenkehre|Sonnenkehre]] while rejecting claims that Constructs are naturally suited to risk. [[Ruby Abyss]] sponsors them.
