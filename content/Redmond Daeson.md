Redmond Daeson was the founder and first ruler of the [[Imperium]]. He rose to prominence at the [[Battle of Aaaros]] in AC 532 during the [[First Galactic War]] and was crowned on [[Palade]] after the AC 546 peace. The [[Daeson Directive]] codified Imperial law under his rule and continues to influence [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] courts.
