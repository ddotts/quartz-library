# Mechthild Kranz

Mechthild Kranz is a retired Sator Dwarf Steel League champion from a Schil-rooted enamelworking town. Stocky, white-bearded, and missing her left eye, she retains a ceremonial blade that is never used in modern demonstrations. [[Stinson's]] sponsored her career. She now curates a public arms-and-labor museum and presents historical safety demonstrations.
