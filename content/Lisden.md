## Overview

Lisden is a damp, overgrown, moonless world rich in flora and fauna.

Lisden supplies Nova with timber and preserved food and exports fibers and joinery. It relies on Lyore for metals and machinery and uses Lyorean resonant stone for power.

## Appearance

Lisden's visible hemisphere is deep green and indigo beneath tangled cloud systems, while its entire dayside disappears beneath the dark, opaque surface of [[The Medium]].

## The Medium and the civil war

Its dayside is submerged in [[The Medium]], producing diffuse pearlescent daylight.

Elven, Eladrin, and Fairy kingdoms fought for control of Lisden during the [[Lisden Civil War]]. The [[Fae Alliance]] and [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] won the war in AC 876. The Alliance's Houses then formed the [[Fae Court]] and retained the Orda as Protectors of Peace. The Court later entered [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] politics, ending its planetary rule without severing most Houses' allegiance to the Orda.

## Legacy feyfolk

Centaurs, Firbolgs, Harengon, Satyrs, Fairies, and Eladrin belong to Lisden's pre-spaceflight history but never constituted one fey civilization. Eladrin and Fairy dynasties ruled some of the kingdoms that competed for the planet, while Centaur, Firbolg, Harengon, and Satyr communities were prominent among the mixed kingdoms. Allegiance crossed species lines, and the victorious Fae Alliance incorporated Houses from several peoples. Their descendants remain attached to old settlements, territories, and Sylvan-speaking institutions under [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]].

## Government since AC 961

Since AC 961, [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] governs the planet. The [[Fae Court]] represents its constituent Houses, while the independent [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] remains a non-governing protective force. Native life evolved under weak direct sunlight. Several large semi-aquatic species move between the planetary surface and [[The Medium]]. [[Prealis]], the capital, stands on cliffs above [[Moonlake]] and preserves the most extensive pre-spaceflight old town.

## Vile and Lavaadil

[[Vile]] is growing through the [[Viewport Gate]] connection to [[Valgrath]] on [[Lyore]]. [[Lavaadil]] became a symbol of the [[Nova Civil War]] through both the initiating patrol attack and the later joint defeat of an extremist [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] takeover.

## Regional geography

[[Bellecour Weald]] lies west of the [[Vandermere Lowlands]]. Lisden’s cities share a monorail line, which crosses from the Weald into the lowlands at [[Vile]] on their shared border. Vile houses the [[Quartz Library]] and the [[Viewport Gate]] to Valgrath on Lyore.

In Vandermere, [[Prealis]] stands on the cliff edge above [[Moonlake]]. From the lowlands, the monorail serves Eikesteg in [[Eikenbroek Canopy]] to the northeast and [[Lavaadil]] in the [[Lavaadil Backwaters]] to the south. Boats unload their catch beneath Lavaadil’s raised market walks.

## Physical characteristics

- **Diameter:** Approximately 8,600 km.
- **Surface gravity:** Approximately 0.90 g.
- **Mean density:** Approximately 7.35 g/cm³.

A large metallic core accounts for its high density.
