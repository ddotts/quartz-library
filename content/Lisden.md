## Overview

Lisden is a damp, overgrown, moonless world rich in flora and fauna.

## Appearance

Lisden's visible hemisphere is deep green and indigo beneath tangled cloud systems, while its entire dayside disappears beneath the dark, opaque surface of [[The Medium]].

## The Medium and the civil war

Its dayside is submerged in [[The Medium]], producing diffuse pearlescent daylight. The [[Fae Alliance]] and [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] won the [[Lisden Civil War]] in AC 876. The Alliance's Houses then formed the [[Fae Court]] and retained the Orda as Protectors of Peace. The Court later entered [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] politics, ending its planetary rule without severing most Houses' allegiance to the Orda.

## Government since AC 961

Since AC 961, [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] governs the planet. The [[Fae Court]] represents its constituent Houses, while the independent [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] remains a non-governing protective force. Native life evolved under weak direct sunlight. Several large semi-aquatic species move between the planetary surface and [[The Medium]]. [[Prealis]], the capital, stands on cliffs above [[Moonlake]] and preserves the most extensive pre-spaceflight old town.

## Vile and Lavaadil

[[Vile]] is growing through the [[Viewport Gate]] connection to [[Valgrath]] on [[Lyore]]. [[Lavaadil]] became a symbol of the [[Nova Civil War]] through both the initiating patrol attack and the later joint defeat of an extremist [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]] takeover.
