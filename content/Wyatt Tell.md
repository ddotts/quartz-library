Wyatt Tell was an engineer at [[Vineflare]] in the [[Federation of Avergwyn and Aldan|Melissian Federation]] who led the [[First Cascade Circuit Demonstration]] in AP 1448. His linked low-level sigils proved that higher-order spells could be decomposed into reproducible sequences. Descendants of his design became the [[Tell Circuits]] used aboard [[Potemkin 1]], making Tell a foundational figure in [[Proto-realism]] and magic's first industrial revolution.
