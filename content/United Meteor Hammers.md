# United Meteor Hammers

United Meteor Hammers is a [[Schil]] Ironball club owned through municipal associations, technical schools, and supporter cooperatives. [[Vineflare]] sponsors both squads through its Avergwyn apprentice-school network.

## Notable players

### Martí Cervera

Martí Cervera is an Elite League Minotaur from an old Avergwyn community who trained through a Schil technical school. Short-horned and rangy, he combines precise changes of direction, restrained spellwork, and a narrow casting blade instead of relying on size. Vineflare also sponsors his registered equipment.
