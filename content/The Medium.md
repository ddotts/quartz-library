# The Medium

## Overview

The Medium is a planet-scale orbital ring of air and aether with properties created by an unusual interaction with the planar weave.

> **Appearance:** From outside, the Medium is dark and opaque like an immense ring of oil, with slow pearlescent streaks moving across its surface.

Breathable and comparable in density to thick sea-level fog. Bends light like water; vessels can cross it without modification but suffer poor visibility. Its aether normally phases through matter as though occupying an ethereal layer between planes, but it sometimes interacts with organisms and magic.

The Medium spans several million kilometers and covers roughly one side of both [[Lyore]] and [[Lisden]] near their dawn–dusk terminators. The same refraction produces Lisden's pearlescent days and Lyore's pearlescent shines at night. No native organisms have been formally catalogued, although dangerous encounters have occurred.

The Medium predates known settlement. Its origin remains unexplained.
