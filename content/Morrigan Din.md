Morrigan Din was empress of [[Avergwyn]] and a vocal supporter of the [[Alliance of Worlds]] during the [[Third CCS Summit]]. Din disappeared during the [[Plane Shift Disaster]] at [[Dome Yüceil]] in AC 970. The available records do not establish whether Din died or remained within the shifted dome.
