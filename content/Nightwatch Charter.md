# Nightwatch Charter

## Overview

The [[Nightwatch]] Charter supplies the common rules accepted by the otherwise autonomous Companies of Nightwatch. It defines their shared communications and command structure. [[Lantern Officers]] assigned to senior Companies may veto orders that violate it, although they cannot otherwise issue commands.
