House Piesmorados once shared the government of Mach with the Rumasa and Barroco. It lost influence after the Red Summer and regained its standing during the [[Great War of Ecria]], when Bobadil Piesmorados came back as one of the Returned and took the lead of the house. Under Returned leadership, the Piesmorados allied with [[House Rumasa]] and helped lead Schil’s efforts to reintegrate the Returned into ordinary life.

Bobadil was a prodigy in temporal magic and played a key role in the war as a member of the Spine of the World, one of the guilds employed by [[House Orchidee]]. The [[Piesmorados University of Magic and Philosophy]] was named in his honor.
