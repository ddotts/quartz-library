# Tsuiseki Civilian Classes

## Overview

[[Tsuiseki]] society recognizes three civilian classes whose members live alongside the order's elite arms aboard the [[Odyssey]] without sharing the same duties, status, or beliefs.

## Classes

- **Squires:** civilians employed directly by the [[Tsuiseki]].
- **Pilgrims:** believers who participate in the pilgrimage.
- **Protected:** non-believers who travel with or collaborate with the order.
