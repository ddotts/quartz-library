# West Sea

## Overview

[[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] corporations have withdrawn from most of the West Sea because surveys have identified few resources able to offset the costs of navigation, security, and infrastructure. The withdrawal reduced access to services administered through concession agreements. Inhabited settlements consequently rely more heavily on local authorities, independent traders, and non-commercial institutions. CCS and Confederation presence is lower than in the other three Seas. Some migrants seeking to avoid corporate or Confederation oversight have settled in the region.

Records of the West Sea remain incomplete because few organizations maintain regular routes through it.
