**Demonyms** · Aschenhanger / aschenhangense

Aschenhang is a district on [[Sator]]’s downwind volcanic slopes, where ash collects above inhabited valleys.

The name means roughly “Ash Slope” in Schil.

Views of [[Opera]], volcanic slopes, and hot springs draw visitors seeking the landscapes around the shipyards that built the later Potemkin motherships.

Nietental receives freight by the valley road from [[Hafenkessel]]. The high route from the [[Greifen Shelves]] comes out above town, and Glashufen’s growing houses stand uphill.

## Nietental

**Demonyms** · Nietentali / nietentalí

Nietental is a fabrication city in Aschenhang. Fabrication sheds occupy broad valley floors. Crane bays are visible from the freight approaches. Worker housing follows service roads leading to crane bays and freight approaches.

The name means roughly “Rivet Valley” in Schil.

The valley freight road climbs from [[Hafenkessel]]. The Hinterjoch back route enters the slopes above town.

## Bad Rotsein

Bad Rotsein is a volcanic spring in Aschenhang. A secluded spa gives its name to a volcanic spring in a basin of red-stained rock.

The name means roughly “Rotsein Spa” in Schil. The spring takes the name of its secluded spa, named for the red-stained rock around the basin.

## Glashufen

Glashufen is a complex of geothermal growing houses in Aschenhang. Pipes heat stepped growing beds beneath glass roofs. Water meters stand at each inlet.

The name means roughly “Glass Plots” in Schil.

The growing houses stand uphill from Nietental.
