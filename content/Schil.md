## Overview

Schil is a cultural and political constituent of the [[Empire of Avergwyn]]. It retains its own communal institutions and identity alongside the Empire's other members.

Schil society has long included secular movements, technical associations, mutual aid societies, and traditions of local government. These institutions allow it to participate in the wider Empire without relying on the Dindessian throne for its internal identity.

## Cities

Eigach is the capital of Schil. Mach is the heart of [[Her Church of Light]], Elumere is the ancestral home of the deGraf and Cane families, and Obern is Schil's industrial heart.

## Diaspora

Large numbers of Schil workers, engineers, families, and institutions migrated to [[Sator]] during the settlement of [[Aldan System|Aldan]]. Sator became a second cultural center rather than a simple colonial extension of Avergwyn. Its industrial conditions strengthened technical schools, labor associations, mutual aid societies, and secular organizations.

A later migration from Sator into [[Cthel System|Cthel]] created a diaspora of a diaspora. Communities on [[Typhon]] and elsewhere in Cthel inherited traditions already reshaped by Satorian industry and settlement. Their connection to Schil therefore runs through Sator as much as through Avergwyn.

## deGraf's

[[deGraf's]] was founded in Schil in 1432 AP. The family company followed Schil settlement to Sator and later established its official headquarters on Typhon. Its route from Avergwyn through Aldan to Cthel parallels the wider diaspora, making it one of the few institutions with continuous roots across all three centers.

The company's later membership in the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] complicates that legacy. It remains family held and benefits from old community ties, but it now operates inside a concessionary order that many descendants of the same migrations distrust.

## Modern identity

Schil identity does not imply a single political allegiance. Communities on Avergwyn participate in the Empire of Avergwyn and the [[Confederation of Avergwyn and Aldan]]; Satorian institutions often claim an independent authority over their shared heritage; and Cthel's descendants belong to a republic shaped by Imperial and corporate rule. Their common history creates durable ties without making any one government their uncontested representative.
