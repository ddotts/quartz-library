## Overview

Schil is a cultural and political constituent of the [[Empire of Avergwyn]]. It retains its own communal institutions and identity alongside the Empire's other members.

Schil society has long included secular movements, technical associations, mutual aid societies, and traditions of local government. These institutions allow it to participate in the wider Empire without relying on the Dindessian throne for its internal identity.

## Cities

[[Eigach]] is the capital of Schil. [[Mach]] is a center of [[Her Church of Light]], [[Schil#Elumere|Elumere]] is the ancestral home of the deGraf and Cane families, and [[Obern]] is Schil’s industrial heart.

## Geography

The Daslich range runs along the southwestern peninsula. The Paso de Obern climbs near [[Obern]], with passing places on the steeper grades before the road descends toward [[Eigach]] and the eastern coast. The [[Maelor March]] occupies frontier hills farther along Schil’s boundary.

A market road from [[Obern]] crosses the [[Maelor March]] toward the Ecrian mainland.

### Traun

Traun lies west of [[Mach]]. The Sauer once governed it and eventually sought cooperation as the miasma swamps spread. Raised roads cross wetlands that still shape the settlement after the miasma of the Returned passed.

### Elumere

Elumere lies northwest of Mach. The former Cane and Graf estates now house museums devoted to their families. Estate roads converge on the old service yards around which the town grew.

### Wiednausen

A ferry links Wiednausen’s sheltered market to Mach across the bay. The former Fischer estate houses a renovated gallery with few works from the lost original collection.

### Ofen

Ofen’s harbor lies below an exposed point near the peninsula’s southwestern tip. The Etzenberger took control of the town after the Becker household was destroyed.

### Veekz Islands

Goblin tribes inhabit all the Veekz Islands southwest of the peninsula. They joined Schil as the territorial houses lost power and retain a distinct island culture. Crossings run between sheltered landings, with exposed approaches harder to use in rough water.

## Historical geography and government

In the 14th century AP, the Kingdom of Schil occupied a coastal peninsula along the Daslich range. Mach stood on its bay, [[Eigach]] on the eastern coast, and [[Obern]] inland near its pass. The Veekz islands lay offshore to the southwest. Schil bordered the Dindessian Empire, the polity now known as [[Din]].

The surviving record names King Morales Orchidee. The crown governed through territorial houses and cities with their own institutions. The Schwarz administered Eigach, [[House Cane]] supplied the court with arcane expertise from Elumere, and the Rumasa and Barroco held power in Mach. The [[Storm Guild]] suppressed the Red Summer uprising. During the [[Great War of Ecria]], court houses employed multiple guilds. [[House Orchidee]] retained the Spine of the World and the [[Storm Guild]]. The [[Verkünder von Schil]] is among the recovered sources for the war. Schil attempted to reintegrate the Returned, with [[House Rumasa]] and [[House Piesmorados]] taking leading roles.

## Diaspora

Large numbers of Schil workers, engineers, families, and institutions migrated to [[Sator]] during the settlement of [[Aldan System|Aldan]]. Sator became a second cultural center rather than a simple colonial extension of Avergwyn. Its industrial conditions strengthened technical schools, labor associations, mutual aid societies, and secular organizations.

Dwarves, Gnomes, Bugbears, Goblins, Hobgoblins, Goliaths, Dragonborn, and Kobolds participated in this working-class migration. Goblin migrants included families from the Veekz Islands. Constructs joined through the later industrial and shipyard institutions that recognized them as workers and residents.

A later migration from Sator into [[Cthel System|Cthel]] created a diaspora of a diaspora. Communities on [[Typhon]] and elsewhere in Cthel inherited traditions already reshaped by Satorian industry and settlement. Their connection to Schil therefore runs through Sator as much as through Avergwyn.

## deGraf's

[[deGraf's]] was founded in Schil in 1432 AP. The family company followed Schil settlement to Sator and later established its official headquarters on Typhon. Its route from Avergwyn through Aldan to Cthel parallels the wider diaspora, making it one of the few institutions with continuous roots across all three centers.

The company's later membership in the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]] complicates that legacy. It remains family held and benefits from old community ties, but it now operates inside a concessionary order that many descendants of the same migrations distrust.

## Modern identity

Schil identity does not imply a single political allegiance. Communities on Avergwyn participate in the Empire of Avergwyn and the [[Confederation of Avergwyn and Aldan]]; Satorian institutions often claim an independent authority over their shared heritage; and Cthel's descendants belong to a republic shaped by Imperial and corporate rule. Their common history creates durable ties without making any one government their uncontested representative.
