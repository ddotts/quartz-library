The Astral Lighthouse was an order dedicated to protecting the material universe from extraplanar threats. Scientists, mages, and former members of the Lighthouse helped found the [[Tsuiseki]] aboard [[The Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] after the [[Lighting of the Path]]. The Tsuiseki continued the older order's protective mission.
