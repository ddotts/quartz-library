**Demonyms** · Brañan / brañense

As Brañas is a district of connected drainage catchments on [[Procne]]. Runoff channels converge on reservoirs below raised ground. Spillways divide the settled catchments.

The name means roughly “Wet Meadows” in Dindessian. The name refers to the wet lowlands below the reservoir ridges.

The catchments contain the oldest known fey ruins and artifacts in settled space. Many sites remain undiscovered in the storm, which has made the region the subject of fascination far beyond Procne.

A raised causeway reaches the reservoir ridge from [[Cornelia]], and a high service road arrives from [[Cinto das Rachas]] above the spillways. L’Orgue de Ferro handles overflow below the main reservoir.

## La Ramera

La Ramera is a rain-cut gorge in As Brañas. Floods leave branches caught on ledges above a narrow gorge. A shallow crossing opens briefly at low water.

The name means roughly “The Branch Catcher” in Dindessian. It takes its name from the branches that floodwater leaves snagged above the crossing.

## L’Orgue de Ferro

L’Orgue de Ferro is a public stormwater overflow complex in As Brañas. Stepped outlets carry runoff away from settled ground. Iron grates sound at different pitches as stormwater and debris strike them.

The name means roughly “Iron Organ” in Dindessian. The name refers to the iron grates that ring at different pitches as stormwater and debris strike them.
