Lavaadil Backwaters is a wetland region on [[Lisden]] centered on [[Lavaadil]]. Slow side channels run below the raised waterfront of [[Lavaadil]]. Approaches from firm ground reach its civic square above the fish landings.

Fortifications survive from the Mixed Kingdoms. Lavaadil’s smokehouses supply both everyday food and high-end preserves across Nova. The attack on a [[Nightwatch]] patrol outside [[Lavaadil]] triggered the [[Nova Civil War]].

The monorail from the [[Vandermere Lowlands]] reaches [[Lavaadil]] from the north.
