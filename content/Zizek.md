# Zizek

## Overview

Zizek is the mobile capital of [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]]. Zizek was built from an [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] capital frame captured near the end of the [[Corporate War]]. Zizek houses both civilian government and military functions.
