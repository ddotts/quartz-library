# Newland–Yuriel

Newland–Yuriel is a [[Nereid]] food and biomass producer founded in the late AC 700s. It specializes in marine farms and offshore platforms, is a leading supplier of preserved food, and is led by [[Peter Cullen]].

## Sports sponsorships

Newland–Yuriel sponsors [[Plovmreža Paponi]], Aerial X pilot [[Tullio Sastre]], and Nereid's [[Nereid#Regata Sopraonda|Regata Sopraonda]] and [[Nereid#Elica Sommersa|Elica Sommersa]]. Its marine farms and offshore platforms supply venues, provisions, and technical crews suited to both surface and submerged competition. It also contributes to [[Portas#Los Cuatro Senderos|Los Cuatro Senderos]].
