Shannon Claude was a traveling lecturer from [[Avergwyn]] who established [[Expressionism]] during the AC 711 [[Open Forms Debate]] aboard the [[Omnicron]]. Claude argued that practitioners could compose personal rituals from parts of several inherited systems, making personal expression part of a working's method rather than an ornament added to it.
