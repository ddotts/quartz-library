# Saint Mother Cassandra

## Overview

Saint Mother Cassandra heads the [[Inquisition]] and serves on the [[Tsuiseki Trinity]] aboard the [[Odyssey]]. Her office directs the order's military, political, and counterintelligence operations. The Odyssey's entry into the [[Nodal Trough]] has also placed the Inquisition at the center of decisions about what discoveries may be transmitted back through the [[Blue Coast]].
