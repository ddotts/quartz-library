## Overview

The Plane Shift Disaster occurred during the [[Third CCS Summit]] in AC 970, when the whole of [[Dome Yüceil]] shifted into the Shadowfell. Hundreds died, the dome was devastated, and [[Collective Commercial Space]] entered a political crisis. Investigators ruled out a natural cause, and [[Daim Arus|Dr. Daim Arus]] compared the event with the historical shifts of [[Kur]] into and out of [[Avergwyn]]. [[DAWAN]] abandoned Yüceil after the disaster and relocated its headquarters to [[Dome Mostyn]].
