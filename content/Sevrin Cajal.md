Sevrin Cajal was a theorist at the [[Piesmorados University of Magic and Philosophy]]. At the AC 138 [[Core System Convention of Arcane Arts]], he argued that thought rather than ritual is the actuator of magic while aether remains its medium, establishing [[Rationalism]]. His theory gained broad acceptance only after [[Nadia Hopper]]'s experiments in [[Psionics|psionics]] confirmed its account of the actuator.
