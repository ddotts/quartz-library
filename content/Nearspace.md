## Overview

The origin of our spacefaring civilization. Nearspace is the oldest zone of the spacefaring expansion: [[Potemkin 1]] reached [[Aldan System|Aldan]] centuries before the Astral Calendar and later returned to Core in AC 310. Its politics cannot be reduced to the [[Universal Federation]]. [[Avergwyn]] and Aldan retain their own federal identity and two [[Space Union]] seats, while the Universal Federation's historical claim runs through the older Melissian relationship and its custody of Potemkin 1.

## The first infrastructure

The region contains the [[Rotator Stations]], the origin of all three Potemkin-class motherships, and the first [[Blue Coast Node]]. Its strategic importance comes as much from shipbuilding and historical legitimacy as from population. Nearspace's institutions predate the [[Imperium]], the [[Space Union]], and the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]], so disputes here often turn on competing continuities rather than newly written charters.
