The _Verkünder von Schil_ was a newspaper printed in [[Obern]] in the 14th century AP. Recovered issues give historians a contemporary view of the [[Great War of Ecria]]. Fragments of its Obern coverage place the printworks on the route used during relief of the old town. Another issue records a crimson light at the Himmelgruft near Andam and reports fighting around Saira Da. These fragments establish what the newspaper circulated during the crisis.

The historical newspaper ceased publication. Surviving issues and proofs are consulted as archival material on late royal Schil. The wartime collection is incomplete.
