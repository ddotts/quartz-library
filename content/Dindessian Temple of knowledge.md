The Dindessian Temple of knowledge is an [[Avergwyn]] institution and one of the earliest known centers of [[Classicism|classical]] magical study. Its records date to around 500 AP, placing it alongside the [[Melissian University of Arcane Practices]] in the earliest documented period of formal magical research.
