Ecria is a continent on [[Avergwyn]] with a mountainous western interior, a long southwestern peninsula, and a broad eastern mainland. [[Mesia]] lies across the Great Ocean to the east.

## Geography

- **Southwestern peninsula.** [[Schil#Geography|Schil]] occupies much of the peninsula. The Daslich range separates the country around [[Mach]] from its eastern coast at [[Eigach]].
- **Western mountains and valleys.** [[Western Ecria]] extends inland from the Liverian coast through the Cordillera del D’Ailadon to the Elmier swamplands and the Valle de Y’malor.
- **Eastern interior and southern frontier.** Beyond the western mountains lie the Corcel Negro Desert and Largo Pesar. Andam was a coastal town east of the core Schil cities. Farah lies nearby offshore, while Saira Da is remembered for the final battle of the [[Great War of Ecria]]. Gelianum stands farther east on Ecria’s southern coast, in [[Din]].

The [[Valdoura Basin]] lies on the Dindessian side of Ecria.

The south-coast route past Andam leads east to Gelianum. From the Liverian side, Paso Antiguo crosses the western mountains near Schobeldach. The Corcel Negro Desert and Largo Pesar lie beyond the range.

## History

In the 14th century AP, the Kingdom of [[Schil]] occupied the southwest, the Liveria Union held the northwestern coast, and Y’malor lay in the western interior. The Filran Hordes ranged across much of the north and east. The Dindessian Empire held much of the eastern mainland and southern coast. Its frontier with Schil became the theater of the [[Great War of Ecria]], which ended at Saira Da.

By AC 998, Schil, Liveria, Y’malor, Filran, and [[Din]], the modern Dindessian polity, are constituent powers of the [[Empire of Avergwyn]]. They retain their own institutions within the confederation.
