## Overview

Demeter is a habitable but sparsely populated planet with a severe cold climate.

## Appearance

Demeter is a blue-white world of glaciers and dark frozen seas, with only a narrow, storm-battered belt of exposed land interrupting the ice.

## Two isolated poles

Demeter's sparse population and severe climate make it suitable for two facilities that require isolation: [[Tartarus]] at the south pole and [[Desponia]] at the north. The planet's ordinary settlements exist in the shadow of those institutions even though neither prison administration nor datacenter operations amount to a planetary government. The same cold environment that limits habitation provides passive security for Tartarus and efficient cooling for Desponia.

## Travel and access

Traffic to the two poles is functionally specialized: guarded transfers move south, while technical staff, data couriers, and infrastructure supplies move north. Demeter is a failure at either pole becomes a system-wide security event. This makes Demeter's small civilian ports and transport crews disproportionately important: they are the only neutral logistical layer shared by the prison and datacenter without belonging to either institution.
