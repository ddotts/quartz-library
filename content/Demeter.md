# Demeter

- **Civilian capital:** [[Zaliha]]
- **Population:** Approximately 25 million residents.
- **Languages:** [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 66%; [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 54%; [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 45%; [[Deresian|High Deresian]] 10%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 8%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 7%; [[The Moiety]] 9%; local traditions 10%; atheism 66%.

## Overview

Demeter is a habitable but sparsely populated planet with a severe cold climate.

Civilian charters operate the inhabited belt and carry staff and freight between the supply routes to [[Desponia]] and [[Tartarus]]. Demeter exports data and cold-weather services. Its settlements and polar facilities rely on imports of food and machinery.

## Appearance

Demeter is a blue-white world of glaciers and dark frozen seas, with only a narrow, storm-battered belt of exposed land interrupting the ice.

## Two isolated poles

Demeter's sparse population and severe climate make it suitable for two facilities that require isolation: [[Tartarus]] at the south pole and [[Desponia]] at the north. The planet's ordinary settlements exist in the shadow of those institutions even though neither prison administration nor datacenter operations amount to a planetary government. The same cold environment that limits habitation provides passive security for Tartarus and efficient cooling for Desponia.

## Travel and access

Traffic to the two poles is functionally specialized: guarded transfers move south, while technical staff, data couriers, and infrastructure supplies move north. Demeter is a failure at either pole becomes a system-wide security event. This makes Demeter's small civilian ports and transport crews disproportionately important: they are the only neutral logistical layer shared by the prison and datacenter without belonging to either institution.

## Regional geography

The civilian charters occupy the sheltered equatorial [[Hollis Belt]], where [[Zaliha]] organizes freight for both polar facilities. Seasonal roads lead north to [[Desponia]] and south to [[Tartarus]]. From the lee-side relay at Zavetrie, a shorter spur branches south to the pools and meltwater channels of [[Whiteglass Basin]], just beyond the inhabited strip at the ice margin.

## Physical characteristics

- **Mean density:** Approximately 6.13 g/cm³.

## Racing

### Icicle Fall

The **Icicle Fall** is a Raging Dust circuit across glacial freight roads and seasonal ice shelves. Crews race against cracking surfaces, whiteout conditions, and steep meltwater channels rather than an entirely fixed route. [[BLACK LABEL]] sponsors the event and its heavy recovery vehicles.
