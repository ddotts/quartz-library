Sator Polytechnic grew from the technical schools and professional associations created during the [[Schil]] settlement of [[Sator]]. It was newly founded when it joined [[Vineflare University]] in the AP 1698–1705 [[Aether Programme]]. It became the institutional base of [[May Farday]], whose synthesis established [[Realism]], and [[Iria Solavel]], whose [[Standard Cascade Circuit]] converted realist theory into mature [[Applied Magic|applied magical]] engineering.
