# Universal Federation

## Overview

Universal Federation is the successor to the Melissian Federation and the largest territorial power in [[Transpace]]. The new identity was adopted after the colonization of [[Aldan System|Aldan]] as a deliberate break with the pre-stellar Melissian past. The Melissian Federation nevertheless functions as an affiliate of the Universal Federation, creating friction with the [[Federation of Avergwyn and Aldan]].

Universal Federation lost control of [[Ertes System|Ertes]], [[Cthel System|Cthel]], and [[Higys System|Higys]] during the [[First Galactic War]]. Those three systems then formed the [[Imperium]], which the Federation defeated during the AC 600–697 [[Imperial Campaign]]. [[Godon System|Godon]] remained continuously within the Universal Federation and became a principal target of Imperial expansion. Currently controls Godon, most of [[Transpace]], Ertes, and [[Potemkin 1]], its only territory in [[Nearspace]]. Universal Federation is governed from [[Eden]] by a syndicalist congress and an elected Chancellor.

Corporate and general political parties sit in the Senate; regional parties form the lower house. Political culture is strongly [[Eden]]-centered. Off-world citizens often identify simply as “Federation.”
