## Overview

Universal Federation is the largest territorial power in [[Transpace]]. Its identity developed from the expansion of the [[Melissian Federation]] after the colonization of [[Aldan System|Aldan]], but the original Avergwyn polity continues to exist as a member of both the Universal Federation and the [[Empire of Avergwyn]].

The Melissian Federation's dual membership creates friction with the [[Confederation of Avergwyn and Aldan]] inside the [[Space Union]]. Despite these conflicts of interest, the Universal Federation and Confederation form the [[Old World Powers]] within the wider [[Established Order]] and remain closer allies to each other than either is to the [[Concessionary Bloc]].

## Wars and losses

Universal Federation lost control of [[Ertes System|Ertes]], [[Cthel System|Cthel]], and [[Higys System|Higys]] during the [[First Galactic War]]. Those three systems then formed the [[Imperium]], which the Federation defeated during the AC 600–697 [[Imperial Campaign]]. [[Godon System|Godon]] remained continuously within the Universal Federation and became a principal target of Imperial expansion. The Federation currently controls Godon, most of [[Transpace]], Ertes, and [[Potemkin 1]], its only territory in [[Nearspace]], and holds eighty percent of the extraction rights in [[Irlan Mining System|Irlan]]. Universal Federation is governed from [[Eden]] by a syndicalist congress and an elected Chancellor.

## Government

Corporate and general political parties sit in the Senate; regional parties form the lower house. Political culture is strongly [[Eden]]-centered. Off-world citizens often identify simply as “Federation.”

Eden's schools, universities, civil service, and interplanetary professions give the Federation a shared public culture. Its prestige rests on the state's age and its claim of continuity with the earliest spacefaring civilization. Off-world communities often depend on these institutions while disputing Eden's authority to decide which customs, histories, and qualifications count as universal.
