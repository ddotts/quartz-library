# Leonor Bellver

Leonor Bellver is a Procne Dragonborn Aerial 1 pilot descended from atmospheric-harvesting migrants. Blue-scaled, with a scarred snout ridge and a storm-pressure suit covered in manual tabs, she competes on [[Procne#Tempest Chase|Tempest Chase]] with sponsorship from [[Silver Meridian]].
