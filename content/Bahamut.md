# Bahamut

## Overview

Bahamut is an uninhabitable terrestrial planet averaging roughly 450°C at the surface under more than ninety atmospheres of pressure.

> **Appearance:** Bahamut is a swollen amber-white sphere whose surface is permanently hidden beneath boiling sulfur clouds, with lightning illuminating the deeper layers like veins beneath skin.

Its atmosphere is dominated by carbon dioxide and sulfuric vapor; descent probes survive only minutes. Radar and orbital lidar provide most mapping because visual surveys are impractical. Unexplained dayside heat signatures may be volcanic or artificial.

[[Nightwatch]] classifies the planet as a scientific reserve and restricts orbital flyovers. Its moon is [[Eoccenon]].
