# Lagal System

## Overview

Lagal System joined the [[Blue Coast]] in AC 639. Lagal System contains several gas giants and no habitable terrestrial worlds.

> **Appearance — Unzan:** Unzan is a deep, saturated red gas giant, its darker storm bands resembling currents moving beneath a vast crimson sea.

> **Appearance — Soplo:** [[Soplo]] is a pale-gold giant with soft, widely spaced cloud bands and a faint dusty ring.

> **Appearance — Festo:** [[Festo]] is a compact rust-red gas giant mottled by dark vortices, giving it the appearance of a banked coal.

[[Unzan Station]] operates around the largest giant; smaller extraction sites serve [[Soplo]] and [[Festo]]. Lagal System is a cool primary star permits extraction infrastructure to orbit relatively close without extreme thermal stress. Most workers rotate in from [[Lyore]] and Santa Maria; few residents remain permanently.

The [[Long Shift]] assigns six months on station followed by three months of leave anywhere in Nova.
