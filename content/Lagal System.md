## Overview

Lagal System joined the [[Blue Coast]] in AC 639. Lagal System contains several gas giants and no habitable terrestrial worlds.

## Appearance

**Unzan.** Unzan is a deep, saturated red gas giant, its darker storm bands resembling currents moving beneath a vast crimson sea.

**Soplo.** [[Soplo]] is a pale-gold giant with soft, widely spaced cloud bands and a faint dusty ring.

**Festo.** [[Festo]] is a compact rust-red gas giant mottled by dark vortices, giving it the appearance of a banked coal.

## Stations and extraction

[[Unzan Station]] operates around the largest giant; smaller extraction sites serve [[Soplo]] and [[Festo]]. Lagal’s cool primary star allows extraction infrastructure to orbit relatively close without extreme thermal stress. Most workers rotate in from [[Lyore]] and Santa Maria; few residents remain permanently.

## The Long Shift

The [[Long Shift]] assigns six months on station followed by three months of leave anywhere in Nova.
