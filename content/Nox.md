The Nox are an [[Umbra]] faction created by the ancient philosophical schism. They built the [[Hecate Network]] to escape the [[Lux]]. [[Clovis Bolgause]] named [[NOX ULTRA]] after them because both fled the tyranny of a sun toward the edge of existence.
