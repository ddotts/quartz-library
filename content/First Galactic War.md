## Overview

[[Ertes System|Ertes]], [[Cthel System|Cthel]], and [[Higys System|Higys]] fought the [[Universal Federation]] for independence. [[Godon System|Godon]] remained part of the Universal Federation. Grievances centered on Federation taxation and trade policies favoring old [[Avergwyn]] enterprises. Cthel's black-hole geometry favored the rebels in several major engagements.

## Aaaros and the peace

[[Redmond Daeson]] rose to prominence at the [[Battle of Aaaros]] in AC 532. The [[Eden]] peace of AC 546 recognized the independence of [[Ertes System|Ertes]], [[Cthel System|Cthel]], and [[Higys System|Higys]]. Those three systems then formed the [[Imperium]] under Daeson.
