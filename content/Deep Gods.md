Deep Gods, also called Elder Gods, originate beyond the known universe. Some traditions describe them as aspects of a single plane-encompassing being, or as a conscious plane divided into distinct entities, in a relationship loosely comparable to an [[Anima]] and its world.

The [[Fomori]] fought the Deep Gods, partially sealing them at the cost of the Fomori's near-extinction. Remnants of both were encountered as late as 1400 BP. Deep-God cultists are often driven mad through communion, while other servants attempt to summon their patrons into the material plane.

Deep Gods are distinct from planetary [[Anima]] and mortal [[Ascended Godhood|Ascended Gods]].
