## Response to impressionism

Romanticism arose by applying [[Impressionism]] back to [[Classicism]]. Impressionism described reality as readable and changeable from within, while classicism preserved ritual systems that had remained useful for centuries. Romanticism joined the two: ritual is the actuator and reality the medium, but inherited systems are models for addressing an interconnected reality rather than isolated laws.

## The Eden Summit

[[Godel Soret]] established the school during the AC 436 [[Eden Summit of Universal Academics]] at the [[Eden University of the Universe]]. [[Eden]] had been reached in AC 253 and connected to the [[Blue Coast]] in AC 419, allowing the university to compare Core archives with magical traditions carried through [[Transpace]] before the [[First Galactic War]] began in AC 500.

## Practice

Romantic practice values lineage, symbolism, and context. A ritual works not only because its steps are valid but because those steps locate the practitioner within a relationship to place, community, or tradition. The school restored academic prestige to classical archives while rejecting the claim that their categories were final explanations.
