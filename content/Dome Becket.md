Dome Becket stands at the edge of [[Su Defteri]] and is the departure point for the [[Nila Pilgrimage Corridor]]. Under the steel-ribbed roof, a desert gate opens onto an outfitting court. Segregation, boycotts, and excessive force strengthened organized crime groups that now contest movement through the dome’s checkpoints and informal yards.
