The Guild Court resolves internal disputes within the [[Haulers Guild]] and operates outside [[Nightwatch]] jurisdiction. The [[Risk Operations Syndicate]] controls thirty percent of its seats, giving the Guild's gray-market wing formal influence over commercial adjudication.
