# Second CCS Summit

## Overview

The Second [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] Summit was held in AC 933. It was one of the summits at which governments and the [[Tsuiseki]] contested the scope of [[Inquisition]] extradition rights across [[Space Union]] systems.
