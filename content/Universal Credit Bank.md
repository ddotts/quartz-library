# Universal Credit Bank

## Overview

Universal Credit Bank is the galaxy's principal financial institution, founded in AC 455. Financed much of the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] but avoided most postwar sanctions through agreements with the [[Space Union]]. Universal Credit Bank operates branches in every major system and has done so since the late AC 500s.

Universal Credit Bank is headquartered in [[Procne]], with assets legally declared in the Errant City of [[Otoño]] since the late AC 800s for security and tax purposes. Universal Credit Bank issues the Collective Commerce Credit, the standard digital galactic currency. [[Salva Milay]] has served as CEO since AC 960.
