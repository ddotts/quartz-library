Mori's is a [[Valgrath]] retail chain owned by [[James Jonastile Group]]. It specializes in gothic clothing, cursed objects, venoms, rogue and warlock equipment, and chimera services. Its regular manufacturers are [[Vineflare]], [[Ruby Abyss]], and [[deGraf's]].
