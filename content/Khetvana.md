**Demonyms** · Khetvanite / khetvanense

Khetvana is a forest basin on [[Maguuma]] broken up by cultivated plots. Hedges follow small waterways and processing sheds sit in clearings.

The name means roughly “Forest Plots” in Melissian.

Khetvana produces Maguuma’s animal and insect biomass exports for mass food markets across the galaxy.

The portage from [[Varṣāvana]] enters the basin below the Mud Harp. Tributary barges leave the basin for [[Gād Maidan]]. The Grafting Loom stands at the forest edge, and the Bees Knees is near Mūl Chauk’s packing sheds.

## Mūl Chauk

**Demonyms** · Mulchauki / mulchaukí

Mūl Chauk is a basin processing town in Khetvana. Packing sheds back onto farm lanes around the growers’ square. Root crops fill shaded loading bays.

The name means roughly “Root Square” in Melissian.

## The Grafting Loom

The Grafting Loom is a living graft works in Khetvana. Jointed frames and soft bindings guide living saplings into curves as they grow.

Jointed frames hold saplings in place like threads on a loom.

## The Bees Knees

The Bees Knees is an insect breeding and processing plant in Khetvana. [[Malli]] breeds insects in raised, ventilated sheds and dries the meal in a separate hall. Surplus fruit and milling scraps become low-cost protein for offworld food manufacturers.

The crew coined the nickname for the bent legs of the raised sheds.
