Deresian originated in the [[Broken Isles]], where communities from many planes and histories were forced into contact after the collapse that created the region. It is best understood as a mixed language family shaped by isolation, migration, and the need to reconcile incompatible vocabularies and chronologies. The [[Collapse of the Deres Observation Paradox]] normalized the passage of time but did not erase the linguistic differences it produced.

- **Islandic Deresian** is the broad family of local varieties spoken across the Broken Isles. Communities separated by unstable routes or different planar origins may preserve words and grammatical forms unknown in neighboring fragments.
- **Belter Deresian** is the working language of station crews and mobile labor across known space. It spread beyond Deres through docks, extraction sites, maintenance crews, and shipping networks. Its practical vocabulary makes it especially useful where workers do not share a single [[Common Language|Common]] variety.
- **Union Deresian** is a co-official language of the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]]. Its status reflects both the commercial importance of Deresian institutions such as [[Cofradia]] and the CCS's extensive concessions in the Broken Isles. Critics of [[CCS Concessionary Governance]] dispute whether the standardized form represents Deresian communities or the institutions that administer them.
- **Pilgrim Deresian** developed through [[The Odyssey]] and the [[Tsuiseki]]. It carries language from the humanitarian mission in the Broken Isles into a mobile religious society. Because the Odyssey left communities at successive [[Blue Coast Node|Coast nodes]], Pilgrim Deresian is encountered far beyond Deres and often marks a connection to Tsuiseki service, scholarship, or pilgrimage.

The Deresian varieties overlap rather than form clean borders. A station worker from the Isles may move among Islandic, Belter, and Union Deresian in the same day. This code-switching can signal intimacy, profession, or resistance to official authority.
