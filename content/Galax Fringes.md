The Galax Fringes are planet-wide arcane puzzles that contain hints for the wider [[Universal Puzzle]]. It is unknown if there are common patterns on these puzzles and the the [[Tsuiseki]] maintains the highest secrecy on the topic, however some information has been made public from the solved puzzles thus far, like the fact that they usually involves the local [[Ascended]], and tend to have extraordinary effects on the planet, like the beginning of the volcanic activity in [[Nereid]] leading to the start of its terraformation.
