**Demonyms** · Carvazan / carvaceño, -a

Carvaz Springlands is an isolated spring country on [[Lyore]]. Springs surface in scattered alkaline basins. Most springs sink into the flats or end in a white crust. Saltbush and wiry rushes hug the short outflows, while bare clay and gravel stretch between pools. Some pools shelter fish or snails found nowhere else, and burrowing lizards hunt insects along wet margins after sundown. The few drinkable springs guide seasonal routes. Diverting a seep can remove an entire habitat.

The Carvaz name covers the scattered spring basins and the barren country between them.

The springs support Lyore’s most diverse ecosystem. Their scattered habitats sustain much of the planet’s flora and fauna.

The turnoff from the A75 reaches Juniper Wells at the basin edge. Farther in, caravan routes meet at Rāhband, where the desert crossing to the [[Valley of Gods]] begins. Yādgār-e Āb stands beside a dry pool across the spring country.

## Juniper Wells

**Demonyms** · Juniperian / juniperense

Juniper Wells is a spring-country service town in Carvaz Springlands. The service town sits on a gravel rise at the spring country’s edge. Tankers fill covered cisterns and outfitters sell cans and tires to travelers.

It is named for a lone juniper near the old wells, a landmark for drivers approaching across the flats.

The turnoff from the A75 reaches town from the direction of Buena Brisa.

## Rāhband

**Demonyms** · Rahbandi / rahbandí

Rāhband is an asimar caravan settlement in Carvaz Springlands. Asimar brokers grant access to a caravan market where routes converge. Awnings shade water vessels and the lanes leave space for animals.

The name means roughly “road binding” in Celestial. Several caravan routes meet at the market.

Caravans cross the desert from the market toward the [[Valley of Gods]].

## Yādgār-e Āb

Yādgār-e Āb is an abandoned tower in Carvaz Springlands. Offerings gather seasonally at an abandoned tower whose inscriptions preserve older obligations over drawing water.

The name means roughly “Water Memorial” in Celestial.
