## Overview

The charitable and humanitarian arm of the [[Tsuiseki]] and the only elite arm led by a man. Maintain chapter-houses at every [[Blue Coast Node]]. Work closely with the [[Red Feather]], using those chapter-houses to extend medical services and supplies across the network.

## Medical supplies

Distribute some of the unbranded prosthetics supplied to the [[Red Feather]] by [[deGraf's]].
