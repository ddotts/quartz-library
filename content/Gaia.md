Gaia is the Deresian name of one of the less common shards of the broken isles. A mix of coastal and urban environments survived the collapse, and small spheres of population.

One notable gaian was the Lich Fraga, who tried to unite the remnants of the costal settlements of Gaia but was met with resistance and was exiled, current location unknown.

Gaia was its chosen native name, since unlike its other native names "Terra", "Tierra" and "Earth" were too close to the Deresian and Common for earth.
