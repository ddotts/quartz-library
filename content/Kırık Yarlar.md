**Demonyms** · Yarli / yarlense

Kırık Yarlar is a canyon region in southern [[Ping]]. Pink Salt collects at bends in its tributary ravines, giving echoes misleading bearings. Rockfall and flash floods leave little stable ground for a dome.

The name means roughly “Broken Ravines” in Issiz. The name refers to the broken cliffs and branching ravines.

The canyons form the heart of Ping’s southern desert communities.

The desert trail from the direction of [[Su Defteri]] passes below Listening Needles on the northern approach to the ravines. Desert dwellers use it for caravan crossings, with rare crossings by armored hover vehicle.

## Nila Sepideh

**Demonyms** · Sepidehi / sepidehí

Nila Sepideh is a seasonal cave settlement in Kırık Yarlar. Households move seasonally between cave camps and seeps. Rope ladders connect occupied ledges, and dry recesses hold tools and jars for later arrivals.

The name means roughly “Dawn Settlement” in Issiz. The name refers to an east-facing cave where the first light reaches the camp.

## Sūzan-e Sorkh

Sūzan-e Sorkh is a group of slender pink mineral spires on an outlying salt ridge in Kırık Yarlar, with broken crystals at their bases.

The name means roughly “Red Needles” in Issiz.

## Listening Needles

Listening Needles is an interference monitoring array in Kırık Yarlar. A line of shielded instruments on firm ground overlooks the main canyon approach and records signal interference away from heavy traffic.

Survey crews named the slender instruments for the echoes they pick up.

The desert trail from the direction of [[Su Defteri]] passes below the instruments.
