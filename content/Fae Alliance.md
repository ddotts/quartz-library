The Fae Alliance began during the [[Lisden Civil War]] as the Alliance of Mixed Kingdoms. Its kingdoms accepted [[Bugyn Orda]] protection while retaining political leadership. After their victory in AC 876, the Alliance's Houses formed the [[Fae Court]] and kept the Orda as [[Lisden]]'s non-governing Protectors of Peace.
