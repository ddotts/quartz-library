Lisden's mixed kingdoms formed the Alliance of Mixed Kingdoms, later called the Fae Alliance, during the [[Lisden Civil War]] against the Elven, Eladrin, and Fairy kingdoms vying for control of the planet. Centaur, Firbolg, Harengon, and Satyr communities were prominent among the mixed kingdoms, although allegiance crossed species lines and some Eladrin and Fairy Houses joined the Alliance. The allied kingdoms accepted [[Bugyn Orda]] protection while retaining political leadership. After their victory in AC 876, the Alliance's Houses formed the [[Fae Court]] and kept the Orda as [[Lisden]]'s non-governing Protectors of Peace.
