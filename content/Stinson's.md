# Stinson's

## Overview

Stinson's is a luxury artisan manufacturer founded on [[Palade]] in AC 462 and expanded under Imperial and [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] patronage. Stinson's produces furniture, clothing, art, private vehicles, and luxury weapons and armor. Stinson's lost substantial assets to postwar sanctions.

Stinson's is headquartered in [[Procne]], with workshops on [[Quamridge Mass]], [[Maguuma]], and [[OSP3]]. Stinson's holds a [[Universal Federation]] concession for Maguuma's premium wood. Its weapons are low-volume, highly profitable, and often used as diplomatic gifts.

Traditionally abstains from [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] industrial-policy votes. The Quamridge atelier produces [[Stinson's Provenance]], whose pieces record owners through embedded arcane sigils. [[Benedict Stinson]] has served as CEO since AC 971.
