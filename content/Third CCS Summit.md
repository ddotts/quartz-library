The Third [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] Summit was held at [[Dome Yüceil]] in AC 970. It ended in the [[Plane Shift Disaster]] when the entire dome moved into the Shadowfell, killing hundreds, leaving several prominent figures missing, and forcing [[DAWAN]] to relocate to [[Dome Mostyn]].

## Delegations

Political delegates included CCS president [[Maximilian Godfield]], [[Space Union]] prime minister [[Hieronymous Roen]], [[Empire of Avergwyn]] empress [[Morrigan Din]], [[Universal Federation]] president [[Scarlet Jones]], [[Alliance of Worlds]] representatives [[Uriel Prago]] and [[Arkel Thach]], [[NOX ULTRA]] chancellor [[Alexa Arasta]], Orda Lulong representative [[Gal Ardu]], and [[Inquisition]] representative [[Echaid Daemos]].

Corporate representatives included [[Martha Vineflare]] for [[Vineflare]], [[William Bright]] for [[DAWAN]], [[Joe Smith]] for [[Weiß]], [[Abel Hawkins]] for [[Good Doctor]], [[Bernard Stinson]] for [[Stinson's]], [[Magda Exxet]] for [[Ruby Abyss]], [[Wallace de Graf]] for [[deGraf's]], [[Daenelae Laesan]] for [[Cofradia]], and [[Salva Milay]] for the [[Universal Credit Bank]]. Other documented attendees included [[Mistral]], [[Titania Pentecostes]], and [[Laeda Sigil]].

## Aftermath

[[Gal Ardu]], [[Scarlet Jones]], and [[Uriel Prago]] died during the summit's [[Plane Shift Disaster]]. One public report also lists [[Bernard Stinson]] as dead, while other records list him as missing. [[Maximilian Godfield]], [[Daenelae Laesan]], [[Morrigan Din]], and [[Karuna]] remained missing or of unknown status. The other named representatives survived.
