The Third [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] Summit was held at [[Dome Yüceil]] in AC 970. It ended in the [[Plane Shift Disaster]] when the entire dome moved into the Shadowfell, killing hundreds, leaving several prominent figures missing, and forcing [[DAWAN]] to relocate to [[Dome Mostyn]].
