# Abel Hawkins

## Overview

Abel Hawkins founded [[Good Doctor]] and served as its chief executive until AC 984. His [[Murder of Emily Montenegro]] forced his removal and triggered a corporate scandal. [[Lee Brown]] replaced him, while [[DAWAN]] abandoned its friendly rivalry with Good Doctor and began targeting its markets aggressively.
