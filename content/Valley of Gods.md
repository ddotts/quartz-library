The Valley of Gods is the largest surviving legacy Aasimar settlement on [[Lyore]] and the only sacred water-tower community that has remained continuously active.

The valley remains the cultural heart of Lyore’s legacy Aasimar communities.

## The valley

A long stretch of bare desert surrounds the valley. From the approaches, broken ridgelines conceal the green floor until the last descent. Inside, cold freshwater springs feed streams that run the length of the valley. Dense groves shade the banks, orchards grow on higher ground, and fish gather in the deep pools. The sacred water tower remains the center of the settlement, with homes and cultivated ground spread along the streams.

## The approaches

The long crossing begins at Rāhband in the [[Carvaz Springlands]]. Aasimar patrol the final approaches and guide invited travelers into the valley.

Legacy Aasimar tribes guard the approaches together. Lookouts watch the ridgelines, and patrols meet arrivals in the outer desert before they reach the descent. The tribes decide who may enter and guide invited visitors in. Families traveling between desert camps return to the valley to replenish their water and gather at the tower.
