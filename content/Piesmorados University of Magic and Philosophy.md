The Piesmorados University of Magic and Philosophy was the institution of [[Sevrin Cajal]] when he presented [[Rationalism]] at the AC 138 [[Core System Convention of Arcane Arts]]. Its combination of magical and philosophical study provided the institutional setting for Cajal's attempt to explain how thought interfaces with aether.
