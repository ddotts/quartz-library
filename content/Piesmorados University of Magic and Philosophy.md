The Piesmorados University of Magic and Philosophy was the institution of [[Sevrin Cajal]] when he presented [[Rationalism]] at the AC 138 [[Core System Convention of Arcane Arts]]. Its combination of magical and philosophical study provided the institutional setting for Cajal's attempt to explain how thought interfaces with aether.

The campus reading hall has carved timber galleries and paired bays for philosophers and spell practitioners. Shuttered windows overlook enclosed rooms used for aether demonstrations. The campus stands outside [[Mach]] on the road toward Elumere.

## Namesake

The university was named in honor of Bobadil Piesmorados, a prodigy in temporal magic and a member of the Spine of the World during the [[Great War of Ecria]]. His return as one of the Returned revived [[House Piesmorados]] after its decline following the Red Summer.
