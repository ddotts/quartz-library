# Potemkin 1

## Overview

Potemkin 1 is mothership of the [[Universal Federation]].

- **Current status:** nomadic trajectory in [[Nearspace]] (since AC 310).

Potemkin 1 is the first Potemkin-class vessel and the ship that carried the original expansion from [[Aldan System|Aldan]] through [[Barei System|Barei]], [[Cthel System|Cthel]], [[Ertes System|Ertes]], and [[Godon System|Godon]] before returning to Core in AC 310. Unlike [[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]], it never became part of the [[Tsuiseki]] pilgrimage; unlike [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]], it was never converted into a station. Its survival gives the [[Universal Federation]] a direct material link to the earliest era of spaceflight. Potemkin 1 created the Core node after the main Coast had already begun. That exceptional authorship complicates any simple claim that the [[Blue Coast]] belongs solely to the Odyssey or the Tsuiseki.
