The Old World Powers are the enduring collaboration between the [[Universal Federation]] and the [[Confederation of Avergwyn and Aldan]]. Each power holds two of the [[Space Union]]'s nine standing seats, giving them four votes when they act together. Their collaboration does not erase their disputes over the [[Melissian Federation]], Aldan, or the Potemkin legacy.

The Melissian Federation belongs to both the Universal Federation and the [[Empire of Avergwyn]], carrying internal Avergwyn conflicts into relations between the two Old World powers. They nevertheless cooperate more closely with each other than either does with the [[Concessionary Bloc]]. Both belong to the wider [[Established Order]], whose peace has contained their rivalry since AC 855.
