**Demonyms** · Eikenbroeker / eikenbroekense

Eikenbroek Canopy is an oak-marsh canopy on [[Lisden]]. Oak crowns cover standing marsh water. Raised walks reach dry ground, and nurseries occupy openings in the canopy.

Eikenbroek is roughly “Oak Marsh” in Sylvan.

Tree villages survive from the Elven Kingdoms. The Canopy is Nova’s principal source of wood and supports specialized marsh farming.

The monorail approaches from Vletdam in the [[Vandermere Lowlands]] to the southwest and stops at Eikesteg.

## Eikesteg

**Demonyms** · Eikestegan / eikestegense

Eikesteg is a raised oak settlement in Eikenbroek Canopy. Homes sit on dry knolls and among thick branches. Elevated footways cross flooded gaps between them.

The name means roughly “Oak Walkway” in Sylvan.

The monorail from Vletdam approaches from the southwest, and raised walks connect its stop to the settlement.
