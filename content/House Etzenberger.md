Otto Etzenberger took control of Ofen after the destruction of the Becker house. His administration was shaped by the same criminal interests that had brought down his predecessors.

The Etzenberger are remembered as Ofen's successor house. Their later influence is best traced through municipal history, with no surviving hereditary claim proposed.
