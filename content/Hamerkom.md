**Demonyms** · Hamerkoman / hamerkomense

Hamerkom is a heavy industry basin on [[Quamridge Mass]]. Smelter stacks rise over a low basin. Runoff channels divide the factory grounds and freight concentrates on level terrain.

The name means roughly “Hammer Basin” in Firan Creole.

Hamerkom concentrates the planet’s industrial and artisan jobs, including much of the work open to middle-income households and to people trying to leave the lowest industrial levels. Aspiring chipmakers and jewelers establish workshops here.

Wisselwerf receives ore from the [[Ertsrand]] terraces at the foot of Burdenrails. A public lift reaches the rim of [[Nevelbodem]].

## Ketelbrug

**Demonyms** · Ketelbruger / ketelbrugense

Ketelbrug is a basin industrial city in Hamerkom. Foundries stand beside industrial waterworks and heavy lifting frames. Housing occupies the higher streets.

The name means roughly “Boiler Bridge” in Firan Creole.

## Wisselwerf

**Demonyms** · Wisselwerfer / wisselwerfense

Wisselwerf is a transfer settlement in Hamerkom. Transfer platforms meet at several levels. Enclosed sidings hold waiting loads, and screened entrances lead to clean berths.

The name means roughly “Transfer Yard” in Firan Creole.

The transfer yards receive ore from Burdenrails, which descend from the terraces of [[Ertsrand]].
