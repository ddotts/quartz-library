# Huracan de Cornelia

Huracan de Cornelia represents settlements beyond [[Procne]]'s stabilized eye rather than corporate [[Portas]] alone. [[James Jonastile Group]] sponsors the club as part of its expansion beyond the capital.

## Notable players

### Alessa Borrell

Alessa Borrell is a retired Prime League captain born in a storm-belt community. The water Genasi has translucent blue-gray skin, cropped white hair, and old pressure scars along both arms. After organizing players during a disputed evacuation, she became chair of the intersystem professional players' association. Her former sponsor, the [[Universal Credit Bank]], now regularly faces her across contract negotiations.
