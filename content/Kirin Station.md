# Kirin Station

## Overview

Kirin Station cannot occupy [[Supervoid|the Supervoid]]'s exact center because that center is the black hole. It is better understood as an observation and relay station maintaining a controlled orbit near the inner edge of the inhabited fragments. Its primary purpose is to compare material trajectories with the motion of astral anchors, providing the reference data used to navigate the pseudo-stasis field. The station's clocks must be continuously reconciled with external standards because local historical experience prevents any single Deresian time source from serving as an uncontested scientific or legal standard.

[[Tsuiseki]] researchers maintain a significant presence through the [[Cathars]] and the order's origin in Deres, but the station remains subject to Confederation sovereignty and [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] concessionary influence rather than neutral extraterritorial law. Emergency transmissions from Kirin are especially valuable because normal communication can be distorted by the charged aether and by disagreements between planar frames.
