# Haulers Guild

## Overview

[[NOX ULTRA|NOX]]'s principal commerce and logistics network and the backbone of trade throughout Nova. Haulers Guild carries roughly sixty percent of Nova's civilian shipping by tonnage. Independent, [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]], and [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]]-flagged vessels carry the remainder. Also conducts trade between Nova and the rest of the galaxy.

Internal disputes are decided by a [[Guild Court]] outside [[Nightwatch]] jurisdiction. The [[Risk Operations Syndicate]]—usually called the Black Syndicate or the Blacks—handles gray-market cargo and holds thirty percent of the Guild Court's seats. The charter permits “discretionary cargo,” including illegal or stolen goods, subject to additional tariffs.

Haulers Guild maintains private docks at Santa Maria and Unzan stations. The [[Enforcer's Syndicate]] uses accountants, surveyors, and breakers to recover delinquent debts and missing cargo. Its agents carry a stamped chit rather than a uniform or badge; every Guild dock is required to cooperate with its bearer. Unverified reports have linked some Black Ledger operatives to contracts with the [[Bugyn Orda|Orda]]'s [[Dragonbrood]].
