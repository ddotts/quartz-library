The Burtiru administered Morgenland on behalf of Schil. The eastern territories maintained their own court and institutions despite repeated changes of frontier. The house's ties to alternative cults helped it operate in a region with varied devotional traditions.

The house's legacy is the precedent of eastern local autonomy. Morgenland's own institutions outlasted the Burtiru's appointment by the crown.
