The Rotator Stations are the largest shipyard complex in known space, positioned between [[Sator and Opera]] in the [[Aldan System]]. They draw labor from habitable [[Sator]] and raw material from resource-rich [[Opera]]. [[The Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] and [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]] were built there after [[Potemkin 1]], built on [[Avergwyn]], reached Aldan. The two later motherships give the shipyards a central place in the transition from generation-ship expansion to the [[Blue Coast]].
