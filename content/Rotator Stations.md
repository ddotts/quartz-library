# Rotator Stations

## Overview

The Rotator Stations are the largest shipyard complex in known space, positioned between [[Sator and Opera]] in the [[Aldan System]]. They draw labor from habitable [[Sator]] and raw material from resource-rich [[Opera]]. All three Potemkin-class motherships were built there, giving the shipyards a central place in the transition from generation-ship expansion to the [[Blue Coast]].
