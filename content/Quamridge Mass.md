## Overview

Quamridge Mass is a high-gravity industrial world, informally known as a “Jewel” planet.

## Appearance

Quamridge is a dark, heavy-looking globe smothered in brown industrial haze, with illuminated floating spires rising above it like jeweled needles.

## Life above and below the haze

Wealthy families live in floating spires, while impoverished communities are concentrated in the smog-choked industrial and ventilation levels below. Upper-class society uses hereditary-styled titles such as lord and duke, but those titles primarily denote ownership, corporate position, and social rank rather than feudal obligations. Species and ancestry strongly affect social and economic status. Wealth is concentrated among groups favored by the established social order, while tieflings are generally assigned its lowest status.

## Corporate roots

Several major [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] corporations are based here or have roots here:

- [[Ruby Abyss]] (navigation, computation, counterintelligence): officially headquartered here; many senior engineers recruited from the underclass.
- [[DAWAN]] (automata, combat/industrial equipment): originally headquartered here until AC 955, when it moved to the [[Dome Yüceil]] in [[Ping]].
- [[Good Doctor]] (combat/industrial equipment, land vehicles): originally headquartered here before negotiating a potential move to [[Lyore]] ([[Nova Sector]]).
- [[Stinson's]] (luxury goods, artisanal armaments): maintains a workshop here; the second-founded atelier runs the [[Stinson's Provenance]] sub-line.

## Technical careers

[[Ruby Abyss]] recruits engineers from lower-income communities, providing one path into professional and upper-level technical work while also transferring skilled personnel to a corporation operationally centered on the distant [[The Odyssey]]. [[DAWAN]] and [[Good Doctor]] both began here before seeking safer or more strategic headquarters, leaving their supply chains and rivalries embedded in the planet even after executive departure.
