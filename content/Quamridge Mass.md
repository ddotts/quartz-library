# Quamridge Mass

## Overview

High-gravity industrial planet; informally called a "Jewel" planet.

> **Appearance:** Quamridge is a dark, heavy-looking globe smothered in brown industrial haze, with illuminated floating spires rising above it like jeweled needles.

Wealthy families live in floating spires, while impoverished communities are concentrated in the smog-choked industrial and ventilation levels below. Upper-class society uses hereditary-styled titles such as lord and duke, but those titles primarily denote ownership, corporate position, and social rank rather than feudal obligations. Species and ancestry strongly affect social and economic status. Wealth is concentrated among groups favored by the established social order, while tieflings are generally assigned its lowest status.

Several major [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] corporations are based here or have roots here:

- [[Ruby Abyss]] (navigation, computation, counterintelligence): officially headquartered here; many senior engineers recruited from the underclass.
- [[DAWAN]] (automata, combat/industrial equipment): originally headquartered here until AC 955, when it moved to the [[Dome Yüceil]] in [[Ping]].
- [[Good Doctor]] (combat/industrial equipment, land vehicles): originally headquartered here before negotiating a potential move to [[Lyore]] ([[Nova Sector]]).
- [[Stinson's]] (luxury goods, artisanal armaments): maintains a workshop here; the second-founded atelier runs the [[Stinson's Provenance]] sub-line.

Quamridge's high gravity, smog-filled industrial depths, and floating noble spires reproduce class divisions in the physical organization of its settlements. Aristocratic titles describe ownership and corporate standing rather than feudal duty: the status of lords and dukes depends substantially on the industries operating below the spires. Ancestry-based hierarchy reinforces the economic division, with lower class communities concentrated in scavenging and industrial labor and tieflings assigned the lowest recognized social position.

[[Ruby Abyss]] recruits engineers from lower-income communities, providing one path into professional and upper-level technical work while also transferring skilled personnel to a corporation operationally centered on the distant [[Odyssey]]. [[DAWAN]] and [[Good Doctor]] both began here before seeking safer or more strategic headquarters, leaving their supply chains and rivalries embedded in the planet even after executive departure.
