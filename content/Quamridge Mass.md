# Quamridge Mass

- **Capital:** [[Wolkennaald]]
- **Population:** Approximately 230 million residents.
- **Species:** [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 73%; Ratfolk 7%; additional Tiefling concentration 8%; Tabaxi 4%; Kobold 4%; Yuan-ti 4%.
- **Languages:** [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 80%; [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 70%; [[Deresian|Bloc Deresian]] 52%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 40%; [[Common Language#Regional varieties and descendants|Firan Creole]] 60%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 6%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 7%; [[The Moiety]] 5%; local traditions 11%; atheism 71%.

## Overview

Quamridge Mass is a high-gravity industrial world, informally known as a “Jewel” planet.

The [[Firan Confederation]] represents Quamridge Mass alongside [[Tybalt]]. The planet hosts [[Ruby Abyss]]’s official headquarters, the original headquarters of [[DAWAN]] and [[Good Doctor]], and the atelier that makes [[Stinson's Provenance]]. Quamridge Mass exports assemblies, minerals, and luxury craft. It relies on imported food and clean consumables for daily needs. Wealth determines who can afford spire services.

## Appearance

Quamridge is a dark, heavy-looking globe smothered in brown industrial haze, with illuminated floating spires rising above it like jeweled needles.

## Life above and below the haze

Wealthy families live in floating spires, while impoverished communities are concentrated in the smog-choked industrial and ventilation levels below. Upper-class society uses hereditary-styled titles such as lord and duke, but those titles primarily denote ownership, corporate position, and social rank rather than feudal obligations. Species and ancestry strongly affect social and economic status. Wealth is concentrated among groups favored by the established social order, while tieflings are generally assigned its lowest status.

Firan Creole is a working-class variety of [[Common Language|Bloc]] spoken on Quamridge Mass and [[Tybalt]].

## Migration before segregation

Ratfolk, Tiefling, Tabaxi, Kobold, and Yuan-ti settlement preceded the modern racial order. Competing corporations recruited entire communities, financing transport, housing, schools, and workshops in exchange for industrial settlement. Collective recruitment initially gave migrant associations bargaining power and helped create the neighborhoods and working culture from which Firan Creole emerged.

## The failed industrial uprising

Segregation hardened only after a failed cross-species industrial uprising against unsafe work, polluted living levels, restricted gravity compensation, and growing corporate control. Authorities recast the uprising as proof that the migrant populations were dangerous. Emergency controls divided districts, restricted movement and employment, and created ancestry-based risk classifications. These measures outlived the crisis and became the foundation of Quamridge's racial hierarchy. Tieflings were eventually assigned its lowest general status, while limited access to the spires for cooperative elites prevented the order from becoming absolute segregation.

## Corporate roots

Several major [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] corporations are based here or have roots here:

- [[Ruby Abyss]] (navigation, computation, counterintelligence): officially headquartered here; many senior engineers recruited from the underclass.
- [[DAWAN]] (automata, combat/industrial equipment): originally headquartered here until AC 955, when it moved to the [[Dome Yüceil]] in [[Ping]].
- [[Good Doctor]] (combat/industrial equipment, land vehicles): originally headquartered here before negotiating a potential move to [[Lyore]] ([[Nova Sector]]).
- [[Stinson's]] (luxury goods, artisanal armaments): maintains a workshop here; the second-founded atelier runs the [[Stinson's Provenance]] sub-line.

## Technical careers

[[Ruby Abyss]] recruits engineers from lower-income communities, providing one path into professional and upper-level technical work while also transferring skilled personnel to a corporation operationally centered on the distant [[The Odyssey]]. [[DAWAN]] and [[Good Doctor]] both began here before seeking safer or more strategic headquarters, leaving their supply chains and rivalries embedded in the planet even after executive departure.

## Regional geography

Service lifts descend from the ridge landings of the [[Aders Highlands]], beneath floating spires such as [[Wolkennaald]], to the working terraces of the [[Ertsrand]] escarpment. Below the terraces, Burdenrails carry ore down to Wisselwerf in [[Hamerkom]]. The transfer yards feed the smelters around Ketelbrug and its industrial waterworks.

A public lift from Hamerkom reaches the rim of [[Nevelbodem]], a deeper neighboring basin. Private lifts descend from the last public stop into [[Roetkom]]. The Breath Banks stand on the upper slope and feed ducts at several levels. Beyond the lowest streets, haze collects in the unstable sinkhole of Nevelput.

## Physical characteristics

- **Diameter:** Approximately 18,350 km.
- **Surface gravity:** Approximately 1.52 g.
- **Mean density:** Approximately 5.82 g/cm³.

A relatively small metallic core lies beneath an enormous rocky mantle. Precious materials occur in concentrated deposits rather than throughout the interior. Heavy gravity makes assisted labor essential in the industrial levels, while wealthy residents can afford extensive gravity compensation.

## Sports

[[Nevelput Rising]] represents Quamridge Mass in Ironball. The **Weightworks** is a Raging Dust circuit through heavy-gravity industrial districts, where reinforced vehicles climb steep haulage roads and survive compression turns that would be modest elsewhere. [[Good Doctor]] sponsors both the team and the circuit, including species-specific medical support.
