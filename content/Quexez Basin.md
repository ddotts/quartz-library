**Demonyms** · Aproner / quexezano, -a

Quexez Basin is a capital basin on [[Lyore]]. Ore roads enter through the basin mouths and converge on the scales at [[Quexez]]. Reservoirs occupy higher benches above settlements and freight yards.

Quexez was Lyore’s cultural center before the [[Viewport Gate]] opened. It remains the principal center of established Lyorean culture and the economic center of Nova, while [[Mesa de Vetas]] has become more closely tied to Lisden.

The ore road east connects the basin to [[Mesa de Vetas]] through Bell Junction. The A75 heads south toward the turnoff for Juniper Wells in the [[Carvaz Springlands]].

## Bell Junction

**Demonyms** · Bellian / bellense

Bell Junction is an assay service town in Quexez Basin. Assayers use counters facing an ore road into the basin and keep their ledgers behind the benches.

It is named for the freight stop’s first assayer.

Trucks from [[Mesa de Vetas]] pass through the town on their way to [[Quexez]].
