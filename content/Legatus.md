# Legatus

Legatus is an Ironball club based in [[Din]]. It markets itself through old imperial traditions without claiming to represent all of [[Avergwyn]]. [[Embrace Group]] sponsors both its Prime and Elite squads.

## Notable players

### Gonçalo Figueiró

Gonçalo Figueiró is a Prime League possession specialist from a Dindessian-speaking farming district of Avergwyn. The compact Harengon has long swept-back ears and uses a low stance, sudden changes of direction, and acceleration-driven passing to protect the ball without striking. [[Stinson's]] sponsors his public appearances, formalwear, and match gloves.
