The Vanguard Corps is a private military company contracted by the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] to eliminate dangerous creatures and secure frontier operations. It was formed from elements of the dissolved [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] military and continues to provide most large-scale armed support for CCS activities.
