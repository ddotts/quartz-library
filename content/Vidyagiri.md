**Demonyms** · Vidyagirian / vidyagirense

Vidyagiri is an academic uplands on [[Eden]]. University towns and instrument makers share Eden’s academic uplands with farms and ridge-top field stations. [[Naya Sarai]] hosts the [[Eden University of the Universe]].

The name means roughly “Hills of Learning” in Melissian.

Vidyagiri’s universities are among the galaxy’s most renowned and form its leading center for analog arcane development. At the [[Eden University of the Universe]], scholars established [[Romanticism]] in AC 436 and gave [[Surrealism]] its first stable formulation in AC 889.

A ridge railway climbs northeast from Navanagar in [[Navan Plain]] to [[Naya Sarai]].
