**Demonyms** · Devrajan / devrají; plural devrajíes

The Devraj Plain is a Melissian farming and workshop district on [[Avergwyn]]. Windbreaks separate long strips of cropland and workshops stand at crossroads along straight farm lanes.

A road from the sheltered inlets of the Geian peninsula climbs east onto the plain toward Ashapura. [[Vineflare]]’s apprentice school sits in a rail town west of the city.

## Ashapura

**Demonyms** · Ashapuran / ashapurense

Ashapura is a plain workshop city in Devraj Plain. Ashapura’s machine sheds line broad service roads. Apprentice housing opens onto shared yards, and tree belts shelter equipment trials. [[Vineflare]]’s Avergwyn school supplies some of the skilled applicants for the city’s workshops.

The name means roughly “City of Hope” in Milessian.
