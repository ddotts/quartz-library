# Desponia

## Overview

Largest datacenter in the galaxy; located at the north pole of [[Demeter]]. [[Ruby Abyss]] ([[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]], navigation/computation) operates the second largest non-Bank private datacenter in known space, described specifically as second only to Desponia. Desponia is the largest datacenter in known space and the benchmark against which Ruby Abyss's private installation is measured.

Its scale makes it natural infrastructure for [[Space Union]] administration and interstellar commerce, but the exact division between Federation ownership, Union use, and private tenants remains politically important. [[Ruby Abyss]]'s counterintelligence business and the [[Universal Credit Bank]]'s financial reach ensure that nominally separate [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] interests still depend on Desponia. [[Demeter]]'s polar climate provides cooling and physical isolation, while the [[Blue Coast Node]] provides the connectivity that makes the remote location viable.
