**Demonyms** · Thermoreman / thermoremense

Rema Thermí is a warm ocean gyre on [[Nereid]]. Warm surface water circulates above a cold return flow. Floating weed shelters young fish and attracts filter feeders. Seamounts break up the sediment-covered floor and divert the deep return flow. The seasonal drift governs where fleets and mobile platforms harvest, including [[Plovmreža]].

The name means roughly “Warm Current” in Bloc Deresian.

Coral rifts draw visitors to the gyre. Its fisheries supply some of the galaxy’s most prized seafood.

The gyre guides [[Plovmreža]] to the fishing grounds of [[Banco delle Reti]]. Surface tenders follow it out to Santa Nerina in [[Isole di Cinza]].
