# BLACK LABEL

BLACK LABEL is a [[Tybalt]] sports, sporting-goods, and energy-drink company founded in the late AC 900s. [[Jericho Black]] leads the company.

## Sports sponsorships

BLACK LABEL concentrates on the most extreme form available in each sport. It sponsors Elite Ironball club [[Tandhaven Flux Gilde]]; the Aerial X circuits [[Festo#Cinderwhip Line|Cinderwhip Line]], [[Procne#Tempest Chase|Tempest Chase]], and [[Bahamut#Abyss Dive|Abyss Dive]]; the Raging Dust circuit [[Demeter#Icicle Fall|Icicle Fall]]; and Mixed War Arts fighters [[Bogdan Koen]] and retired champion [[Thea Cucuruna]]. It also sponsors Geelwaard's [[Geelwaard#King in Yellow|King in Yellow]] and contributes to [[Portas#Los Cuatro Senderos|Los Cuatro Senderos]]. Thea Cucuruna was the company's first individual athlete, and her present medical-governance work remains part of its public history.
