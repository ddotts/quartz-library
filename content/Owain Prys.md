# Owain Prys

Owain Prys is a retired Sator Gnome Aerial 0 pilot from a multilingual shipyard family and a former [[Opera#Hourglass Run|Hourglass Run]] champion. Short and wiry, with ash-gray curls, he carries an old analog timing board to every broadcast. [[Vineflare]] sponsored his racing career; he now works as a public technical journalist.
