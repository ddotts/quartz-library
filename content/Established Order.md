The Established Order is the broad diplomatic order encompassing the recognized non-Nova powers. Since the AC 855 settlement ending the [[Corporate War and Nova Independence War]], its members have maintained a period of stability and peace among themselves, at least on the surface, despite commercial rivalry, concessionary dependence, and disputes within the [[Space Union]].

The Established Order includes the Space Union and its member powers, the [[Old World Powers]], the [[Concessionary Bloc]], the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS Consortium]], and the [[Tsuiseki]]. These categories overlap: governments may belong to the Union and the Concessionary Bloc, while the Tsuiseki also count among the [[New Frontier Powers]].

Historians also use the term retrospectively for the predecessor institutions that sent labor, trade, and military cohorts into Nova before AC 855. In that usage, references to the old-world powers describe the lineage of the Established Order rather than claiming that its postwar settlement already existed.

The [[Alliance of Worlds]] is not part of the Established Order. Its opposition to concessionary governance and sector-level authority places it outside the settlement's recognized institutional framework.
