Roca Perdida is a modified Ymir-3 mining station in the Frankeus asteroid belt, part of the outer belt of [[Lagal System|Lagal]]. The [[Haulers Guild]] and independent groups associated with [[NOX ULTRA]] assembled it from reused colonial modules.

The station began at the edge of the law, smuggling material to buyers outside [[Space Union]] jurisdiction. As NOX consolidated its independence, Roca Perdida became important to its fleet and commerce. Its construction remains a patchwork of incompatible corporate systems, personalized modifications, improvised repairs, and artifacts of uncertain provenance.
