House Cane was one of Elumere's oldest noble houses. The house was at a high point of political and arcane influence in the 14th century AP. Therion Cane advised King Morales Orchidee as court magician, while Thomas Cane headed the household and managed its intelligence network. Lydia Cane studied diabolism and carried out assignments for the house. Its specialists understood patron contracts well enough to alter their terms, a source of influence over nobles whose obligations were difficult to escape. The diminished Graf household lived under Cane patronage. Therion led the Dissidence during the [[Great War of Ecria]], bringing nobles from both sides of the border into a conspiracy to seize power in their respective countries.

By AC 998, Cane is a longstanding family name with a history of changing fortunes. Its noble status lapsed, and surviving public records do not trace an unbroken family line from the Elumere household. Members of the Cane family helped found [[Boxe–Cane–Kaste]], the firm that now owns [[Cane's]].

- Therion's revolt divided the court. The campaign accounts disagree on the precise sequence of the killings and break with the crown.
