# Lee Brown

## Overview

Lee Brown became chief executive of [[Good Doctor]] after founder [[Abel Hawkins]] murdered [[Emily Montenegro]] in AC 984. Brown inherited a company weakened by scandal, renewed competition from [[DAWAN]], and the collapse of its recent gains against [[deGraf's]].
