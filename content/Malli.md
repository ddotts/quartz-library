# Malli

Malli is a [[Maguuma]] food and biomass producer founded in the late AC 700s. It specializes in plant products and insect farming, controls a major share of mass-market food chains, and is led by [[Elizabeth Ezten]].

At The Bees Knees in [[Khetvana]], Malli dries insect meal made from surplus fruit and milling scraps for shipment to offworld manufacturers.

## Sports sponsorships

Malli sponsors retired Iron Fist champion [[Nilüfer Rostami]] and Maguuma's [[Maguuma#Monsoon Floodrun|Monsoon Floodrun]]. Her present fitness and youth-hall work gives the food producer a public-health partnership beyond her competitive career. Malli also contributes to [[Portas#Los Cuatro Senderos|Los Cuatro Senderos]].
