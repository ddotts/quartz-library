The Free Companies are a broad alliance of mostly independent mercenary crews associated with [[Nightwatch]]. They provide one route into full Company status. Former pirates entering [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] service after the AC 961 amnesty could organize as Free Companies alongside joining existing Companies or local Guardsmen.
