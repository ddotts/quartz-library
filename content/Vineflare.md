## Overview

Vineflare was founded on [[Avergwyn]] in 1390 BP and descended from Margaret Vineflare's enterprise. Vineflare is headquartered on [[Sator]] from AC 97 to 700 and on [[Eden]] thereafter. Vineflare supplies civilian and transport vehicles, power systems, reactors, capital ships, combat equipment, and the classic Federation elemental and meteor weapon lines.

## From generation ships to CCS

Avoided the [[Corporate War]] and joined the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] afterward through the [[Space Union]] settlement alongside [[deGraf's]]. Vineflare retains a major [[Universal Federation]] military contract despite CCS membership. Closed its generation-ship division after [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]] departed in AC 209.

## The apprentice school and heirlooms

Vineflare maintains an apprentice school on [[Avergwyn]]. Vineflare produces limited Heirloom Series weapons bonded to individual owners through arcane registration. [[Maria Vineflare]] has served as CEO since AC 995.
