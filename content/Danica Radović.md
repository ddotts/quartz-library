# Danica Radović

Danica Radović is an Islandic Deresian Hadozee pilot from a family tied to station and transport cooperatives. She has tawny fur, a close-shaved crest, and a broad control harness supporting her gliding membranes. [[Cofradia]] sponsors her Aerial 0 career.
