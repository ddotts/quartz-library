# Jacob Leer

Jacob Leer is an Eden Human and active Iron Fist pressure fighter raised in the planet's dense tournament circuit. Broad-shouldered and shaven-headed, he wears simple white training clothes marked only with civic-club colors. He is known for sustained body pressure and refusing theatrical grudges in promotion. [[Reybar Pharma]] sponsors him.
