[[Dome Becket]] is the closest dome to the start of the pilgrimage. From Becket, the route crosses [[Ping]]'s Mar de Polvo toward the Valley of Silver. The surrounding desert includes the Obsidian Lake, Monte Ahuyante, and the Quartz Caverns. The Obsidian Tower at the lake preserves a runic tradition involving obsidian, lava, and souls.

The route enters the valley at Nila Argentum, a trading city whose banners show a hand with two silver fingers. Market roads pass between red-stone escarpments lined with houses cut into the rock and lead toward fortified gates. Beyond Argentum lies Nila Risator, the northern capital of Rio Atorix, the Blue Sultan around AC 997.

In _The Sultans of Ping_, Caleb describes Nila Aureum as the final destination and says the Sultans control passage to its Chamber. Nila Aureum's existence remains disputed.

## Desert approaches

Pilgrims leaving [[Dome Becket]] and travelers moving between the Nila settlements usually go on foot, by hover bike, or by mount.

Across the Mar de Polvo, winds erase tracks from the flat salt desert. Below the rims of the Valley of Silver, shaded benches shelter settlements above dry channels. Deep awnings shade Nila Argentum’s rock-cut shops, and stepped courts below the escarpment receive caravans. At Nila Risator, a bent gate leads into a blue-tiled reception court with side galleries for petitioners.

The surface of Obsidian Lake can split and seal again between crossings. The Obsidian Tower rises above it, with sheltered openings exposing bands of runic masonry. Monte Ahuyante’s caves howl as wind passes through their narrow throats. The Quartz Caverns offer better shelter, though desert animals occupy their chambers and quartz distorts magic.

The corridor is the heart of Ping’s northern desert communities and the center of their religious and political traditions.
