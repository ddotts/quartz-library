# Brigitte Mohn

Brigitte Mohn is a Sator Bugbear driver descended from Schil industrial migrants but identified primarily with her fabrication town. Long-armed, with singed brown fur and heat-resistant hand wraps, she drives from a low posture. [[Faswell–Kron]] sponsors her.
