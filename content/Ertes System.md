# Ertes System

## Overview

- **First arrival:** [[Potemkin 1]], AC 127; [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]], AC 320.
- **Blue Coast node:** AC 383 (The [[Odyssey]]).
- **Faction history:** part of [[Imperium]] (AC 546–697); part of [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] (AC 697–855); joined [[Universal Federation]] post-AC 855.

Ertes moved through every major [[Transpace]] order: independence from the [[Universal Federation]], founding membership in the [[Imperium]], corporate continuity through the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]], and reintegration into the Universal Federation after AC 855. [[Palade]] made the system the political center of both the Imperium and SCF until the Cataclysm rendered it uninhabitable in AC 799 and forced the corporate government to [[Procne]]. [[Aaaros]] preserves a deeper technological past through its abandoned mechanical megastructures, placing archaeological power beneath the better-documented Imperial and corporate layers.

The system's present relationship with the [[Universal Federation]] is necessarily shaped by surviving [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] corporations, unresolved Cataclysm claims, and the assets retained after the [[Corporate War]]. Currently the system has little more than a few dozen research stations with no surviving permanent settlements of any kind.
