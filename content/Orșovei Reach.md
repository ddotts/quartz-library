**Demonyms** · Orșovan / orșovano, -a

Orșovei Reach is an interior cultural province on [[Typhon]]. Market walks bridge depressions in the inner shell. The Reach contains [[Orșova]], whose name survives in the Islandic Deresian form Ținutul Orșovei.

Orșovei is the Islandic Deresian possessive of Orșova. The reach spans several inhabited shell depressions.

[[Orșova]] is Typhon’s cultural center. Its position deep in the Hollow makes it an unusual destination even for experienced offworld travelers.

The descent from [[Brücker Pforten]] passes the Hanging Acres on the way to [[Orșova]]. A freight span serves the farms directly from the [[Veles Industrial Belt]].

## The Hanging Acres

The Hanging Acres are a suspended farm complex in Orșovei Reach. Anchored frames suspend growing beds below the inner shell. The beds supply food during shaft interruptions.

It is named for the acres of growing beds suspended from Typhon’s inner shell.

A freight span connects the farms to the [[Veles Industrial Belt]]. The descent to [[Orșova]] passes the farms.
