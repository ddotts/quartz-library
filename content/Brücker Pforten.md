**Demonyms** · Brücker / brückense

Brücker Pforten is an inner shaft sector on [[Typhon]]. Typhon’s busiest access shafts connect [[Shellside]] to [[The Hollow]] here. Passenger and cargo platforms occupy separate levels, and crews coordinate emergency access when one route closes.

The name means roughly “Gates of Brück” in Cthelic. The name refers to the region around Typhon’s busiest access shafts.

The concentration of traffic makes the interchange a chokepoint for Typhon’s industry and commerce.

Tiefenbrück receives the lift from Oberwacht in [[Shellside]]. From the interchange, a short shellward branch reaches the [[Veles Industrial Belt]], and a longer descent leads toward [[Orșovei Reach]].

## Tiefenbrück

**Demonyms** · Tiefenbrücker / tiefenbrückense

Tiefenbrück is a shaft gateway town in Brücker Pforten. Pressure doors frame the gateway. Service rooms step down toward the shaft, with freight queues on broad landings.

The name means roughly “Deep Bridge” in Cthelic.

The lift from Oberwacht in [[Shellside]] arrives here.
