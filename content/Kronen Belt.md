**Demonyms** · Kronener / kronenés, -esa

Kronen Belt is a machine industry belt on [[Tybalt]]. Worker towns alternate with foundries and assembly yards linked by goods sidings.

The Kronen Goblin Tribes are known for their expertise across several artisan trades. Tybalt’s leading manufacturing facilities and the headquarters of [[Faswell–Kron]] are in the belt.

An eastern tributary of [[Veen Reach]] and connecting goods sidings carry freight to the belt. Werkhof stands on the riverside edge of Kronenburg.

## Kronenburg

**Demonyms** · Kronenburger / kronenburgués, -esa

Kronenburg is a manufacturing city in Kronen Belt. Casting shops occupy large blocks. Technical schools share streets with suppliers to the heavy workshops.

The name means roughly “Crown Fort” in Firan Creole.

## Werkhof

**Demonyms** · Werkhofer / werkhofense

Werkhof is a tool repair suburb in Kronen Belt. Fitters share a repair yard beneath low sheds, displaying used fittings above their benches. Housing stands within walking distance.

The name means roughly “Workshop Yard” in Firan Creole. It takes its name from the shared workyard where local fitters set out their benches.
