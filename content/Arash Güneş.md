# Arash Güneş

Arash Güneş is a Ping Triton and active Mixed War Arts fighter from a buried-water community whose family maintains ties beneath the dome corridor. Teal-skinned, with swept head fins and a hydration collar, he uses a short spear in dry and flooded arenas and combines close combat with water shaping. [[EZ]] sponsors him.
