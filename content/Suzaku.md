# Suzaku

## Overview

Suzaku is the [[South Sea]]'s capital ship, combining regional government, convoy coordination, and emergency response in a hull that can move when routes among fragments change. Its authority comes from maintaining contact rather than occupying a permanent capital; communities that become temporarily isolated can still recognize the ship as the Confederation's local presence. [[Cofradia]] is a major institutional influence on its construction and maintenance because of the company's Deresian origin and long history building capital ships.

The current concessionary order creates a structural conflict of interest: the corporate network capable of keeping Suzaku operational also holds extraction rights that the ship may be required to administer or protect.
