The Abyss Watchers are an independent order of ascetic mages and monks who follow the old ways of the [[Astral Lighthouse]]. Their monastery stands on the cliffs above [[The Abyss]] on [[Eoccenon]]. They monitor the fissure, regulate access to the world, and historically shared findings with [[NOX ULTRA]], the [[Bugyn Orda]], and members of the [[Space Union]].

## Campaign notes (OS1)

Around AC 994, all contact with the Watchers suddenly ceased. Attempts to reach them failed, prompting the expedition portrayed in _The Abyss Watchers (OS1)_.
