# Nova Sector

## Overview

Nova Sector is governed by [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] since AC 961; previously a frontier without a unified sector government. Before NOX consolidation, mobile custom and ad hoc agreements governed space while [[Lyore]]'s errant tribes and [[Lisden]]'s kingdoms governed locally. The economy rests on three foundations:

- Resonant-mineral mining on [[Lyore]] and [[Lisden]].
- Gas extraction at Unzan.
- Salvage, shipbuilding, and rare-mineral extraction in the [[Moont System|Moont]] belt.

Pirate kingdoms continue operating in the approaches, centered on the [[Thunderdome]]. Nova's mixed-species, mixed-origin crews embrace an intentionally cosmopolitan identity.
