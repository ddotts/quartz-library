## Overview

- **First arrival:** [[Potemkin 1]], AC 0 (no node); [[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]], AC 42 (node).
- **Blue Coast node:** AC 42 ([[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]]).
- **Federation of Barei:** [[Space Union]] member; under [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] extraction rights.

[[Potemkin 1]] reached Barei in AC 0 before a node existed; [[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] created the second Coast node there in AC 42, making the system an early bridge between exploration and network travel. [[Ping]]'s reflective [[Pink Salt]] made it a natural first contact point even before [[OSP3]] became the trading center between [[Farspace]] and [[Transpace]]. The Federation of Barei holds a [[Space Union]] seat while [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] extraction rights and control of OSP3 give the commercial bloc practical command over its main infrastructure.

## Later crises

Barei later became central to two distinct crises: the [[OSP3]] Illithid quarantine and the AC 970 [[Plane Shift Disaster]] at [[Dome Yüceil]]. [[Pink Salt]], trade access, and OSP3 make the system too important for any single [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] company, encouraging shared control and internal rivalry rather than a simple one-company administration.
