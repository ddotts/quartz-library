# Bugyn Orda

## Overview

Bugyn Orda is an independent alliance of former pirates, mercenaries, and other groups under the astral dragon [[Burkhan Luu Buga]]. Allied with [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] but historically hostile to old-world powers. Bugyn Orda fought with NOX against the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]], then fought NOX over [[Nough System|Nough]] during the [[Nova Civil War]].

The AC 961 peace preserved the Orda's independence and its role as [[Lisden]]'s Protectors of Peace while granting it no authority to govern the planet. Bugyn Orda works with [[Nightwatch]] against piracy but remains outside [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] government and military command. Its capital ship is the [[Khan]], a dreadnought rebuilt from a captured [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] capital frame.

Orda histories place [[Burkhan Luu Buga]]'s age at more than one thousand years; the location of his original lair is unknown. The [[Dragonbrood]] follows a feudal oath structure. Clan-captains, called Akhmad (plural Akhmaduud), swear personally to the Burkhan. The highest distinction among them is Ariun Akhmad. Anyone who swears to an Akhmad or directly to the Burkhan joins the Dragonbrood.

People living in Orda territory or working under Orda contracts form its flock. They receive protection without becoming [[Dragonbrood]]. During the [[Lisden Civil War]], the Orda supported the [[Fae Alliance]] against the warring kingdoms. The victorious Houses formed the [[Fae Court]] and retained the Orda as Protectors of Peace. Most Houses continued supporting the Orda after the Court entered [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] politics, creating the central political tension behind the [[Nova Civil War]].

The Orda divided internally during the war. [[Burkhan Luu Buga|The Burkhan]]'s decentralized rule allowed violent clans to escalate while collaborationist clans pursued a settlement.
