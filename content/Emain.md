Emain is a religious-goods and martial-equipment retailer in [[Vile]] owned by [[Embrace Group]]. It supplies sculpture, ornaments, armor, heavy weapons, and clerical services including blessings. Its regular manufacturers are [[Weiß]], [[Stinson's]], and [[Vineflare]].
