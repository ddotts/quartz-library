## Overview

- **First arrival:** [[Potemkin 1]], ~1620 BP.
- **Blue Coast node:** AC 0 (first node in the network).

Aldan System is under [[Universal Federation]] control ([[Federation of Avergwyn and Aldan]] — [[Space Union]] member). Aldan was reached by [[Potemkin 1]] around 1620 BP and became the construction base for the later Potemkin projects, placing it at the hinge between generation-ship expansion and the [[Blue Coast]] era. [[Sator]], [[Opera]], and the [[Rotator Stations]] form the system's economic core. Building all three Potemkin-class vessels gives Aldan leverage over every major political tradition that grew from them.

## The first Coast node

The first [[Blue Coast Node]] was established here in AC 0, the same year the Astral Calendar began, [[Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] departed, and [[Potemkin 1]] reached [[Barei System|Barei]]. Aldan's infrastructure is therefore inseparable from the calendar's foundational moment. The [[Federation of Avergwyn and Aldan]] represents the system in the [[Space Union]] alongside Core, despite the [[Universal Federation]]'s broader historical claims over the old expansion.
