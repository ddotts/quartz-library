## Overview

- **First arrival:** [[Potemkin 1]], ~1620 AP.
- **Blue Coast node:** AC 0 (first node in the network).

Aldan System belongs to the [[Confederation of Avergwyn and Aldan]], a [[Space Union]] member. The [[Universal Federation]] retains substantial influence through the dual membership of the [[Melissian Federation]], but it does not govern the Confederation. Aldan was reached by [[Potemkin 1]] around 1620 AP and became the construction base for the later Potemkin projects, placing it at the hinge between generation-ship expansion and the [[Blue Coast]] era. [[Sator]], [[Opera]], and the [[Rotator Stations]] form the system's economic core. Building [[The Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] and [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]] gives Aldan direct historical ties to the [[Tsuiseki]] pilgrimage and the station network that followed it, while Potemkin 1 remains an [[Avergwyn]] construction.

The early workforce included a large migration from [[Schil]] on Avergwyn. Their mutual aid associations, technical schools, and secular institutions became especially influential on Sator and in the Rotator shipyards. Sator later became the principal staging ground for Schil-descended settlement in [[Cthel System|Cthel]].

## The first Coast node

The first [[Blue Coast Node]] was established here in AC 0, the same year the Astral Calendar began, [[The Odyssey|Potemkin 2]] departed, and [[Potemkin 1]] reached [[Barei System|Barei]]. Aldan's infrastructure is therefore inseparable from the calendar's foundational moment. The [[Confederation of Avergwyn and Aldan]] represents the system in the [[Space Union]] alongside Core, despite the [[Universal Federation]]'s broader historical claims over the old expansion.
