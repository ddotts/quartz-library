Iria Solavel was a systems architect at [[Sator Polytechnic]] who translated [[Realism|realist]] theory into shared component notation and testing procedures. Her work culminated in the AP 1764 [[Standard Cascade Circuit]], whose independently manufactured, inspected, and replaceable components drove the second industrial revolution in [[Applied Magic]].
