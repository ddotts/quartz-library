**Demonyms** · Plovmrezan / plovmrezense

Plovmreža is a floating city whose seasonal circuit follows [[Rema Thermí]] and calls at [[Banco delle Reti]]. Its neighborhoods occupy self-propelled hulls that separate for rough crossings. Schools and clinics travel with the residents, while cold stores and net racks occupy the working hulls.

The name means roughly “Floating Net” in Belter Deresian.
