## Overview

South Sea is one navigational region within the field of merged fragments, defined by Confederation charts relative to [[Supervoid|the Supervoid]] rather than by a fixed down direction. It is the most stable of the four Seas and the principal route between the [[Broken Isles]], [[Transpace]], and the wider settled galaxy. Its relative stability has concentrated customs facilities, long-range transport, and foreign institutions in the region. Most traffic entering Deres passes through the South Sea before continuing to the other Seas.

## Suzaku and regional authority

[[Suzaku|Capital Ship Suzaku]] gives the region a mobile administrative and defensive anchor suited to settlements whose material and astral positions do not always align cleanly. Its relationship with the central Confederation is mediated by travel reliability: when routes shift, Suzaku may be the only institution able to maintain continuous contact among communities.
