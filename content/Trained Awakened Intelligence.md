# Trained Awakened Intelligence

## Overview

Trained Awakened Intelligence is [[DAWAN]]'s process for creating automaton intelligence. A prototype automaton is awakened, its temporary consciousness is recorded, and that model is replicated in later units. Opposition from automata-rights movements led to the [[Prototype Protection Act]] of AC 975.
