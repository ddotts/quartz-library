Trained Awakened Intelligence is [[DAWAN]]'s process for creating automaton intelligence. A prototype automaton is awakened, its temporary consciousness is recorded, and that model is replicated in later units. Opposition from automata-rights movements led to the [[Prototype Protection Act]] of AC 975.

TAI can be used to refer to either the process or the resulting intelligence, however sometimes the term Awakened Artificial Intelligence (AAI) is used for the resulting units, which when used to describe conscious automatas is sometimes considered a pejorative term.

[[Source Language|Source]] is used both for machine-readable instruction and for communication among conscious automata. Whether a Source statement is treated as executable property, personal expression, or both is central to disputes over TAI agency.
