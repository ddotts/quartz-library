## Overview

Typhon has a rough, rocky surface that cannot support habitation.

## Appearance

Typhon resembles a dull iron cinder, broken only by the illuminated rings of its shaft cities and faint golden light escaping from passages into its inhabited hollow.

## The hollow world

Hollow interior; center contains a nano-sun — an arcane 1cm sun that makes the inside habitable. [[deGraf's]] ([[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] corporation, family-held, founded 1432 AP) is officially headquartered here due to its shipment agreements with the CCS, [[Space Union]], and Nova — though its major industrial presence is in [[Tybalt]] ([[Firan System]]). Typhon is a small hereditary order of Imperial loyalists (a few dozen old families who keep the [[Redmond Daeson|Daeson]] genealogy) has met annually inside Typhon's hollow interior for two centuries since the [[Imperium]]'s dissolution (AC 697). Modern intelligence assessments do not classify the order as a security threat, while employment records connect some of its archivists to CCS legal departments specializing in historical case law.

## Settlement

The hollow interior is roughly spherical and large enough to hold an entire civilisation. There is some population in the surface in the space stations that were the first settlements before the interior of the planet was explored and the nano-sun stabilised. The nano-sun is an arcane construct of unknown origin. Some archaeologists attribute it to the Umbra; surviving Imperial records document its later stabilization by [[Imperium]] engineers.

## Interior climate and access

Interior atmosphere is breathable and self-circulating; the geometry produces unusual weather patterns. Surface entry to the interior is via a small number of carefully maintained shaft cities.
