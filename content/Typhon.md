# Typhon

- **Capital:** [[Orșova]]
- **Population:** Approximately 700 million residents.
- **Species:** [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 62%; additional Dwarf concentration 6%; Gnome 4%; Bugbear 4%; Goblin 4%; Hobgoblin 4%; Goliath 4%; Dragonborn 4%; Kobold 4%; Construct 4%.
- **Languages:** [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 84%; [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 40%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 22%; [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 8%; [[Deresian|Islandic Deresian]] 40%; [[Common Language#Regional varieties and descendants|Cthelic]] 50%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 11%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 6%; [[The Moiety]] 8%; local traditions 17%; atheism 58%.

## Overview

Typhon has a rough, rocky surface that cannot support habitation.

Typhon belongs to independent [[Cthel System|Cthel]] and hosts the official headquarters of [[deGraf's]]. Its exports include repairs, components, and prosthetic services. Typhon imports food supplements and relies on imported metals and chemicals for industry.

## Appearance

Typhon resembles a dull iron cinder, broken only by the illuminated rings of its shaft cities and faint golden light escaping from passages into its inhabited hollow.

## The hollow world

Hollow interior; center contains a nano-sun — an arcane 1cm sun that makes the inside habitable. [[deGraf's]] ([[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] corporation, family-held, founded 1432 AP) is officially headquartered here due to its shipment agreements with the CCS, [[Space Union]], and Nova — though its major industrial presence is in [[Tybalt]] ([[Firan System]]). Typhon is a small hereditary order of Imperial loyalists (a few dozen old families who keep the [[Redmond Daeson|Daeson]] genealogy) has met annually inside Typhon's hollow interior for two centuries since the [[Imperium]]'s dissolution (AC 697). Modern intelligence assessments do not classify the order as a security threat, while employment records connect some of its archivists to CCS legal departments specializing in historical case law.

## Settlement

The hollow interior is roughly spherical and large enough to hold an entire civilisation. The first permanent communities grew from station crews and settlers arriving through [[Sator]] after [[Potemkin 1]] reached Cthel. Their [[Schil]] ancestry established mutual aid associations and secular traditions still visible beneath modern Bloc culture. There is also a population on the surface in the space stations that preceded exploration of the interior and stabilization of the nano-sun. The nano-sun is an arcane construct of unknown origin. Some archaeologists attribute it to the Umbra; surviving Imperial records document its later stabilization by [[Imperium]] engineers.

Dwarf, Gnome, Bugbear, Goblin, Hobgoblin, Goliath, Dragonborn, Kobold, and Construct communities largely descend from this Satorian migration. Life inside the Hollow reshaped their Schil and Satorian inheritance into Cthelic institutions rather than preserving an unchanged old-world culture.

Cthelic descends from Schilean Dindessian. It diverged over generations in the Hollow, shaped by Typhon’s long history of independence movements. Modern Cthelic bears only a distant resemblance to Dindessian.

## Interior climate and access

Interior atmosphere is breathable and self-circulating; the geometry produces unusual weather patterns. Surface entry to the interior is via a small number of carefully maintained shaft cities.

## Regional geography

On Typhon’s rocky exterior, Oberwacht in [[Shellside]] serves the shaft down to Tiefenbrück in [[Brücker Pforten]]. The shaft opens onto the inhabited inner shell of [[The Hollow]]. A short shellward branch from the interchange leads to Caer Gof and the [[Veles Industrial Belt]], where Perlenfäden hangs below a sheltered span of the Rib.

A longer descent follows the inner shell past the Hanging Acres toward [[Orșova]] in the [[Orșovei Reach]]. A separate freight span along the shell carries goods directly between the industrial works and the suspended farms.

## Physical characteristics

- **Exterior diameter:** Approximately 10,890 km.
- **Interior cavity diameter:** Approximately 8,030 km.
- **Interior gravity:** Natural gravity is approximately 0.40 g at the inner shell and increases toward the nano-sun. Gravity-regulating devices maintain an average of approximately 0.60 g throughout the inhabited structures.

Interior settlements are anchored to the shell and hang inward toward the core under natural gravity. The regulating devices adjust this gravity within the structures.

## Sports

[[Orșova Gol Negru]] represents Typhon in Ironball. The **Sonnenkehre** is an Aerial 1 circuit around solar collectors, heat sinks, and the bright terminator of a major habitat complex. [[DAWAN]] sponsors the race and supplies its telemetry network.
