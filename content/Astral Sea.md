The Astral Sea is a plane parallel to the prime material plane. Fractal currents of aether produce a non-Euclidean space in which distances are compressed and apparent straight lines cross additional dimensions.

The plane's heightened awareness allows trained navigators to project into it while retaining control of their material bodies. From there they can identify and communicate routes for [[ATP Jumps]]. Its other conditions are hostile enough to make ordinary travel impractical.
