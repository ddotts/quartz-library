**Demonyms** · Roetkommer / roetcomense

Roetkom is a company town in [[Nevelbodem]]. Sealed walkways link crowded housing beneath intake shafts tall enough to draw air above the haze for whole streets. Carrying groceries or air canisters uphill in Quamridge’s gravity makes the private lifts essential. Industrial landlords employ tenants to strip worn machinery and bag spent filters. The same landlords lease their flats and control the lifts above the last public stop. Tiefling families are routinely steered into the lowest blocks, where air outages last longest. A layoff can cost a family its income, home, and lift access at once.

The name means roughly “Soot Basin” in Firan Creole. The name refers to the soot that settles in the bowl between the slopes.

Travelers from [[Hamerkom]] reach the rim by public lift and transfer to private lifts for the descent into town.
