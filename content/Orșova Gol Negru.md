# Orșova Gol Negru

Orșova Gol Negru is an Ironball club inside [[Typhon]]'s Hollow, associated with shaft-city mutual-aid societies rather than the surface stations alone. [[Stinson's]] supplies provenance-marked ceremonial equipment and markets the Hollow as an exclusive destination.

## Notable players

### Lorcan Menzies

Lorcan Menzies is a Cthelic Goliath and Elite League shield carrier from a shaft-city mutual-aid family. Long-limbed and slate-skinned, he uses a compact shield designed for the Hollow's regulated gravity and specializes in orientation changes and controlled contact. [[deGraf's]] sponsors his equipment and medical monitoring.
