# Protectors of the North

## Overview

The Protectors of the North are frontier troops who secure established settlements and routes in the [[North Sea]] of the [[Broken Isles]]. Some members are later recruited into the [[Vanguard Corps]], connecting local defense forces with the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] campaign coordinated by [[Cofradia]].
