# NOX Founding Charter

## Overview

The [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] Founding Charter established NOX ULTRA as an independent mobile democracy in AC 731. Representatives of thirteen old-world labor and trade cohorts signed it four years after the [[Corporate War]] began. The charter remains the constitutional basis of NOX government and the later [[Nightwatch]] command structure.
