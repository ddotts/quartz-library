The [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] Founding Charter established NOX ULTRA as an independent mobile democracy in AC 731. Representatives of thirteen [[Established Order|old-world labor and trade cohorts]] signed it four years after the [[Corporate War]] began. The charter remains the constitutional basis of NOX government and the later [[Nightwatch]] command structure.
