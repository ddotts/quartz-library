The Core System Convention of Arcane Arts was the AC 138 convention at which [[Sevrin Cajal]] of the [[Piesmorados University of Magic and Philosophy]] presented the theory of [[Rationalism]]. Cajal argued that thought rather than ritual is the actuator of magic while aether remains its medium. The argument remained disputed until [[Nadia Hopper]]'s later work in [[Psionics|psionics]].
