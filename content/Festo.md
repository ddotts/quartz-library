# Festo

- **Population:** No surface residents; approximately 30,000 people live in associated extraction habitats.
- **Species:** Among habitat personnel, [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 92%; Aarakocra 8%.
- **Languages:** Among habitat personnel, [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 78%; [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 58%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 28%; [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 12%.
- **Beliefs:** Among habitat personnel, [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 8%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 6%; [[The Moiety]] 9%; local traditions 9%; atheism 68%.

Festo is a compact rust-red gas giant in the [[Lagal System]]. Dark vortices mottle its atmosphere, giving it the appearance of a banked coal, while smaller extraction facilities work its resources. Its Aarakocra community belongs to the intersystem station chain formed through atmospheric extraction, family migration, and labor organizing.

## Racing

Festo hosts the **Cinderwhip Line**, an Aerial X race through furnace glare, slag plumes, and heat-warped factory canyons. [[BLACK LABEL]] sponsors the circuit and markets its most dangerous legal passages.
