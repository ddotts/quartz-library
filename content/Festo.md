- **Population:** No surface residents; approximately 30,000 people live in associated extraction habitats.
- **Languages:** Among habitat personnel, [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 78%; [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 58%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 28%; [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 12%.

Festo is a compact rust-red gas giant in the [[Lagal System]]. Dark vortices mottle its atmosphere, giving it the appearance of a banked coal, while smaller extraction facilities work its resources.
