# Tandhaven Flux Gilde

Tandhaven Flux Gilde represents [[Tybalt]] workshops that build for magnetic reversal, heavy gravity, and graceful failure. Its Firan Creole culture includes long-settled residents and smaller Typhon-linked communities. [[BLACK LABEL]] sponsors the club as Ironball's most physically extreme major team.

## Notable players

### Imke Tjarks

Imke Tjarks is an Elite League systems breaker from a Tybalt Goblin repair family. Small, close-cropped, and protected by insulated tusk caps, she wears visibly modular armor and deliberately forces opponents onto backup systems during magnetic disruption. BLACK LABEL sponsors her as the league's most uncompromising active defender.
