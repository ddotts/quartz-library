The Rumasa took the leading administrative role in Mach after the decline of the Piesmorados. Their standing rested on the city's churches, the wine trade, and their relationship with the Barroco. During the [[Great War of Ecria]], both House Rumasa and House Piesmorados came under Returned leadership and through their alliance spearheaded Schil’s efforts to reintegrate the Returned.

The name survives in the records of Mach's religious estates and vineyard holdings. The family's period of direct administration ended as those institutions and city government took on separate roles.
