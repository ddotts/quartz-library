## Overview

- **First arrival:** [[Potemkin 1]], AC 253; [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]], AC 256.
- **Blue Coast node:** AC 419 (The [[Odyssey]]).
- **Faction history:** continuously part of the [[Universal Federation]]; heavily contested during the [[Imperial Campaign]].

[[Eden]] is the capital of both [[Transpace]] and the [[Universal Federation]]. [[Potemkin 1]] reached Godon in AC 253 and [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]] followed in AC 256, but the [[Blue Coast Node]] technology was not ready until the later visit of the [[Odyssey]] in AC 419. Eden was the first planet encountered with environments comparable to [[Avergwyn]]. Federation settlers made it their new home, and Godon has remained part of the Universal Federation ever since.

## Political and economic roles

[[Eden]] supplies the [[Universal Federation]]'s political center, while [[Maguuma]] supplies strategic biomass, food inputs, oil, and high-quality wood. Godon remained loyal to the Universal Federation during the [[First Galactic War]] and became one of the central territories contested during the later [[Imperial Campaign]]. The Universal Federation retained Godon after the Imperial Campaign, preserving Eden as its political and cultural home.

## A legacy of centrality

Godon's political culture is built around continuous centrality. [[Eden]] was the Federation's adopted home before the [[First Galactic War]], remained loyal through the secessions, and survived the [[Imperium]]'s later attempts to take the system.
