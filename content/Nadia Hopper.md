Nadia Hopper was a Cathar researcher associated with the [[Mǔ shī Technical Institute]] aboard [[The Odyssey]]. During the ship's AC 231 passage from [[Firan System|Firan]] to [[Higys System|Higys]], she isolated practitioners from ritual implements and conventional magical cues. Her experiments established modern [[Psionics|psionics]], confirmed [[Rationalism|rationalism's]] account of thought as an actuator, and raised doubts that aether was the only possible medium.
