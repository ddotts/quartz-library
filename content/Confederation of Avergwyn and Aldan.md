## Overview

The Confederation of [[Avergwyn]] and [[Aldan System|Aldan]] is a [[Space Union]] member representing the Core and Aldan systems. It holds two of the Union's nine standing seats. Together with the [[Universal Federation]], it forms the [[Old World Powers]] within the wider [[Established Order]].

The Confederation joins the [[Empire of Avergwyn]] with the worlds, habitats, and stations of Aldan. Its members retain their own governments and institutions while coordinating external policy, defense, and representation in the Space Union.

## Relations with the Universal Federation

The [[Melissian Federation]] belongs to both the Empire of Avergwyn and the Universal Federation. This dual membership creates recurring conflicts of interest within Avergwyn and in Space Union politics, especially over Aldan, the Potemkin legacy, and the pace of political and technological change.

Despite that friction, the Confederation and Universal Federation remain closer allies to each other than either is to the members of the [[Concessionary Bloc]]. Their cooperation gives the Old World Powers four of the Union's nine seats when they act together.
