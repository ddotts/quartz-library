# Burkhan Luu Buga

## Overview

Burkhan Luu Buga is the ancient astral dragon who leads the [[Bugyn Orda]] from the [[Khan|capital ship Khan]]. Orda histories place his age at more than one thousand years, although the location of his original lair is unknown. His decentralized rule allowed both violent and collaborationist clans to act during the [[Nova Civil War]], while he kept the Khan out of major engagements and neither endorsed nor denounced the conflict.
