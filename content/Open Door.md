The Open Door is a rule of pirate law observed aboard the [[Thunderdome]]. Anyone who reaches the vessel under their own power receives its protection, although approaching the ship is dangerous and normally met with force. [[Nightwatch]] Companies and Inquisitors have both visited under this custom.
