Kur is known through historical records of shifts into and out of [[Avergwyn]]. [[Daim Arus|Dr. Daim Arus]] used those events as the principal comparison when investigating the AC 970 [[Plane Shift Disaster]].

## Zugon and Zu Bal Sair in the early record

Kur's earliest history is only partly understood. Sources connected with its shifts into and out of [[Avergwyn]] preserve accounts of Zugon and Zu Bal Sair, but their geography and chronology are fragmentary.

A surviving history credits an unnamed god-empress and ten generals with extending Zugon's rule across the Luvic Ocean. After her death, regional houses fought over succession and elevated their former generals as supreme patrons. Monasteries broke with the nobility, sheltered civilians, and helped reunify the empire by presenting the generals as champions of older gods. The restored dynasty governed with a senate of regional house heads.

Zu Bal Sair appears in old records as both a remote city and a cult of Mordiggian governed by the Yin-Shou. Bone and metal prostheses were reportedly regarded as signs of failure. Silver replacements called gifts of Argentum marked advancement. The record offers little certainty about the cult's earlier history or its eventual fate.
