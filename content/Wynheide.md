**Demonyms** · Wynheidan / wynheidense

Wynheide is an exposed uplands on [[Tybalt]]. Windswept pasture covers the uplands, with proving grounds on exposed ridges above sheltered villages.

The name means roughly “Wind Heath” in Firan Creole.

The uplands are Tybalt’s most fertile region and supply food to much of the planet.

A road from the last navigable landing in [[Veen Reach]] climbs into the uplands. Griphal’s proving grounds sit on a broad bench below Windkerf.

## Windkerf

Windkerf is an upland wind-cut ridge in Wynheide. Wind has cut a notch across the ridge. Grass lies flat on the crest and loose stone collects in its lee.

The name means roughly “Wind Cut” in Firan Creole.

## Griphal

Griphal is a racing and equipment-testing facility in Wynheide. Concentric asphalt ovals serve races ranging from foot races to vehicle events.

The name means roughly “Grip Hall” in Firan Creole.

The grounds occupy a broad bench below Windkerf.
