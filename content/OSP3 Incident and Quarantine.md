# OSP3 Incident and Quarantine

## Overview

[[OSP3]] Incident and Quarantine is an Illithid parasite incapacitated Potemkin 3 in orbit around [[Ping]]. One section of the ship was blown away and placed in stasis. The parasite may have been attracted by unstable magic reverberating through Ping's [[Pink Salt]] deserts.

Decontamination lasted nearly a decade, but some compartments reportedly remained sealed through every later [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] audit. [[OSP3|Potemkin 3]] became Orbital Station Potemkin 3 in AC 489. Most of the vessel entered service as a station while the affected section remained quarantined until AC 616.
