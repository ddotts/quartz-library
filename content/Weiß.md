# Weiß

## Overview

Weiß is the oldest and wealthiest [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] member, founded in 1398 BP. Weiß produces premium goods across most sectors and operates logistics, private transport, and combat fleets. Weiß was investigated for possible [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] involvement, but the available evidence did not meet the standard required for postwar sanctions.

Weiß owns proprietary remote-delivery and [[Weiß Demiplane Storage|demiplane-storage technology]]. The [[Gala|capital ship Gala]] hosts many administrative seats. Operated without an official headquarters until AC 965, when it established one in [[Procne]]; before then it functioned primarily through its demiplane.

Weiß maintains the galaxy's largest conventional shipping network and the [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]]'s largest private intelligence apparatus. Its combat fleet uses salaried crews rather than mercenaries. Weiß is an agreement with [[DAWAN]] permits Weiß variants of DAWAN ships in exchange for premium services and patent access.

[[David Smith]] has served as CEO since AC 972.
