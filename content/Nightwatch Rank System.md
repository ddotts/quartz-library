## Overview

[[Nightwatch]] maintains separate but related ratings for Companies and individual personnel. Senior officers command higher-rated Companies or serve as their deputies. Commodores and Admirals form the lower command chamber, while five Admirals sit in the higher chamber created after the [[Nova Civil War]].

## Company ranks

| Rating | Classification |
|---|---|
| CR5 | Junior Free Company |
| CR4 | Junior Company |
| CR3 | Senior Free Company |
| CR2 | Senior Company |
| CR1 | Elite Company |
| CR0 | Commanding Company |

## Individual ranks

| Rating | Rank | Typical responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| — | Guardsman | Local ground force |
| MR5 | Officer | Local administrator, senior Guardsman, or member of a CR4 Company |
| MR4 | Lieutenant | Commander of a CR5–4 Company or member of a CR3 Company |
| MR3 | Commander | Commander of a CR3 Company or member of a CR2 Company |
| MR2 | Captain | Commander of a CR2 Company or deputy of a CR1 Company |
| MR1 | Commodore | Commander of a CR1 Company or deputy of a CR0 Company |
| MR0 | Admiral | Commander of a CR0 Company |
