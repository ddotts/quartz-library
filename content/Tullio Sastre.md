# Tullio Sastre

Tullio Sastre is a Triton Aerial X pilot raised in a deep settlement on [[Nereid]]. Green-blue, with finned forearms and pressure tattoos, he operates a cockpit partly through water-filled controls and competes on [[Nereid#Elica Sommersa|Elica Sommersa]]. [[Newland–Yuriel]] sponsors him.
