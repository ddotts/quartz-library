# Daeson Directive

## Overview

The [[Redmond Daeson|Daeson]] Directive was the codification of law used by Redmond Daeson's [[Imperium]]. Parts of it survived through successor corporations after the Imperium's dissolution and remain persuasive precedent in courts associated with [[Collective Commercial Space]].
