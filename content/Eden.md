# Eden

- **Capital:** [[Naya Sarai]]
- **Population:** Approximately 1.50 billion residents.
- **Species:** [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 85%; Aarakocra 5%; Kenku 5%; Owlin 5%.
- **Languages:** [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 94%; [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 38%; [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 18%; [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 10%; [[Common Language#Regional varieties and descendants|Naya Milessian]] 60%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 12%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 6%; [[The Moiety]] 11%; local traditions 9%; atheism 62%.

## Overview

Eden is a habitable world with diverse biomes, much like [[Avergwyn]].

Eden seats the [[Universal Federation]]’s congress and the headquarters of [[Vineflare]] and [[Atlas Group]]. Its exports include precision magitech circuitry, academic training, and passenger travel services. Eden grows most of its own food. It relies on imports for military equipment and its largest machinery and vehicles.

## Appearance

Eden presents the familiar blue, green, and white face of an ideal inhabited world, though its nightside is distinguished by an almost continuous web of urban light.

## Seat of government

Capital of [[Transpace]] and the [[Universal Federation]]. Eden is the capital of both the Universal Federation and Transpace, giving one planet influence over a region that also contains independent [[Cthel System|Cthel]] and several politically exceptional systems. The Federation's syndicalist congress is seated here: corporate and general parties occupy the Senate, while regional parties form the lower house.

Naya Milessian is a variety of [[Common Language|Milessian]] used in casual conversation and familiar settings by families who have lived on Eden for generations. It is rarely heard in official or formal settings.

## Open-sky migration

Aarakocra, Kenku, and Owlin migrated to Eden during the expansion of the Universal Federation. Many came from stations and hazardous settlements built without their communities in mind. Eden's open skies, stable environment, education, and Federal citizenship supported permanent settlement. Their shared political networks developed on Eden, but they preserve separate origins and do not constitute one avian culture.

## Political symbolism

The planet's similarity to [[Avergwyn]] reinforces the [[Universal Federation]]'s claim to inherit the old Melissian project, but it also invites comparison with the separate [[Confederation of Avergwyn and Aldan]]. The AC 546 peace ending the [[First Galactic War]] was signed on Eden even though [[Palade]] became the new [[Imperium]]'s capital; the treaties ending the Corporate and Nova Independence Wars were also signed here in AC 855. Eden's authority is repeatedly exercised through postwar settlements: it formalized the loss of three systems in AC 546 and the destruction of the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] plus Nova's independence in AC 855. Its diplomats frame this as constitutional durability; its rivals frame it as the Federation's ability to write the history of wars it did not win alone.

## Magical scholarship

The [[Eden University of the Universe]] hosts the recurring [[Eden Summit of Universal Academics]]. [[Romanticism]] was established there in AC 436, and [[Surrealism]] received its first stable formulation there in AC 889.

## Regional geography

[[Samudratat]] occupies Eden’s western shore. A railway crosses the coastal hills from Naukāghāt to the factories of [[Navan Plain]] in central Eden. At Navanagar, one line climbs northeast along the ridges into [[Vidyagiri]] toward the capital, [[Naya Sarai]]. Another runs south into the river country of [[Nadipradesh]], carrying produce up from Phalmandi. Barges can also carry produce west down the navigable river to a transshipment quay on the Samudratat coast.

## Physical characteristics

- **Diameter:** Approximately 9,750 km.
- **Surface gravity:** Approximately 0.85 g.
- **Mean density:** Approximately 6.13 g/cm³.

## Sports

[[Sarai Soar Sabha]] represents Eden in both Ironball leagues. The world also hosts the **Zenith Marg**, an Aerial 1 circuit that climbs from cultivated lowlands into the thin air above the upper terraces before plunging back toward the finish. [[Atlas Group]] sponsors both the team and the race, presenting them as showcases of Eden's shared civic culture.
