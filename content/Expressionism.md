## Response to romanticism

Expressionism arose in response to [[Romanticism|romanticism's]] rigidity. Romanticism modernized the classical schools but retained them as established systems; expressionism argued that practitioners could compose personal rituals from parts of several intertwined systems. Ritual remains the actuator and reality the medium, but the ritual is designed for the intended effect rather than confined to one inherited school.

## The Open Forms Debate

[[Shannon Claude]], a traveling lecturer from [[Avergwyn]], presented the argument during the AC 711 [[Open Forms Debate]] aboard the [[Omnicron|Capital Ship Omnicron]]. The timing was significant: the [[Space Union]] and its mobile capital had been created in AC 697, while the [[Corporate War]] would not begin until AC 727. The Omnicron's early peaceful rotations allowed scholars from former Imperial, Federation, and independent traditions to compare practices without locating the debate inside a belligerent capital.

## Practice

Expressionists compare magic to cooking: distinct fractions of different systems are combined to produce an intended result, and personal expression is part of the method rather than ornament. The practice is difficult to standardize because copying the visible form of a working may fail if the relationships chosen by its creator are not reproduced.
