# Moont System

## Overview

Moont System joined the [[Blue Coast]] in AC 690. Moont System is a dense asteroid belt surrounds a small, cool yellow star with no major inner planets.

> **Appearance:** The belt forms a broad, glittering torus of broken stone and metallic dust, threaded by the orderly lights of stations and managed transit channels.

The belt formed from a disrupted protoplanet. All inhabitants live in vacuum-rated stations and habitats. Five linked stations form the Moont Belt cooperative under [[Haulers Guild]] oversight.

From [[Santa Maria Station]], the Naval and Holy channels provide two managed transit spines through the ring. The system supplies hull-grade alloys, rare minerals, salvage, and shipyard capacity to [[NOX ULTRA|NOX]] and the [[Haulers Guild]].
