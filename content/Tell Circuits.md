## Overview

Tell Circuits are descendants of the early cascade circuits demonstrated by [[Wyatt Tell]] in AP 1448. They use linked low-level sigils to reproduce multiple effects of higher-order spells by triggering smaller magical operations in sequence.

## Use and limitations

[[Potemkin 1]] used Tell Circuits in its life-support, fabrication, and propulsion systems before its AP 1500 launch. The circuits were inefficient, highly specialized, difficult to repair, and dependent on trained crews, but they made magical labor divisible and programmable before [[Realism]] supplied a common theory of their operation. They are characteristic of [[Proto-realism]] and magic's first industrial revolution.
