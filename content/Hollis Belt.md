**Demonyms** · Holliser / hollisense

Hollis Belt is an inhabited equatorial belt on [[Demeter]]. Ridges shield civilian settlements in Demeter’s narrow equatorial strip. Charters share heating costs and maintain seasonal roads that carry freight to both poles. [[Zaliha]] anchors civilian freight.

The belt is also Demeter’s base for extreme sports and cold-weather expeditions. It is the planet’s only inhabited area outside the secured polar facilities.

Seasonal supply roads from [[Zaliha]] run north to [[Desponia]] and south to [[Tartarus]]. A shorter southern spur from Zavetrie reaches [[Whiteglass Basin]] at the ice margin.

## Zavetrie

**Demonyms** · Zavetran / zavetrense

Zavetrie is a sheltered relay settlement in Hollis Belt. Low buildings cluster behind a lee ridge near routes toward [[Whiteglass Basin]]. The relay has a turnaround bay for convoys.

The name means roughly “Lee Side” in Belter Deresian.

A southern spur from the relay reaches [[Whiteglass Basin]].
