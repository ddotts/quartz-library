# Higys System

## Overview

- **Blue Coast node:** AC 268 (The [[Odyssey]]).
- **Faction history:** part of [[Imperium]] (AC 546–697); part of [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]] (AC 697–855); Republic of [[Procne]] now a [[Space Union]] member under [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] extraction rights.

Higys fought alongside [[Ertes System|Ertes]] and [[Cthel System|Cthel]] in the [[First Galactic War]], then founded the [[Imperium]] with them and later became a founding system of the [[Space Commerce Federation|SCF]]. After the [[Corporate War]] it did not return to the [[Universal Federation]]; the Republic of [[Procne]] became a [[Space Union]] member under [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] extraction rights. Procne inherited the SCF headquarters after the [[Palade Cataclysm]] and then became the headquarters of the CCS, making the system the clearest case of corporate continuity across the AC 855 settlement.

[[Blue Coast|The Coast]] node predates all of those regimes, having been established by the [[Odyssey]] in AC 268. The planet's permanent storm supports atmospheric harvesting while its stabilized eye concentrates government, banking, and corporate administration in one [[Tsuiseki Civilian Classes|protected]] city.
