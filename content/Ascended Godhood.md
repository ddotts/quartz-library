Ascended Godhood is achieved when a mortal becomes the ideal representation of an aspect of the world they inhabit. Unlike an [[Anima]], an Ascended God was once mortal.

Ascended divine power is planetary rather than universal. Ascended Gods lose that power when they leave their world, including during space travel. They can therefore be overwhelming on their home planets while remaining strategically narrower than interstellar governments and corporations. Their followers may knowingly venerate a formerly mortal figure whose ideals, goals, or character they support.
