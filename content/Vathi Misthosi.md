**Demonyms** · Misthosan / misthosense

Vathi Misthosi is a deep ocean basin on [[Nereid]], where dense cold water creeps along the floor beneath warmer layers. Steep walls descend to fine mud cut by deep channels. Oil seeps from fractures in the walls, where bacterial mats feed pale crustaceans and tube worms. Large scavengers range across the open floor.

The name means roughly “Lease Basin” in Bloc Deresian.

The basin is Nereid’s center for scientific research and oil extraction. Its submerged settlements are a cultural center for amphibious and aquatic peoples and draw visitors from other worlds.

Submersibles from the shelf of [[Banco delle Reti]] descend along the basin wall to Sottoporto’s ledge. Dnomera Station sits on the floor below.

## Sottoporto

**Demonyms** · Sottoportan / sottoportense

Sottoporto is an underwater transfer city in Vathi Misthosi. The submerged city occupies a ledge overlooking the basin. Pressure-rated blocks surround a transfer manifold, and locks connect passenger submersibles to living quarters and cargo berths below daylight.

The name means roughly “Submerged Port” in Bloc Deresian.

Submersibles reach the ledge by descending from the shelf break at [[Banco delle Reti]].

## Dnomera Station

Dnomera Station is a seabed survey installation in Vathi Misthosi. Its sealed pods sit on a frame with illuminated instrument arms, and weighted lines link sensors across the bottom. The name Dnomera means roughly “Depth Gauge” in Belter Deresian.
