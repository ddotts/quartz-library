# Singular Combat

Singular combat is the collective name for organized one-on-one fighting. Iron Fist is unarmed martial combat, the Steel League is armed melee combat, Aether Duels are competitive spellcasting, and Mixed War Arts permits weapons, unarmed techniques, and magic in the same bout.

## Notable combatants

- **Iron Fist:** [[Jacob Leer]] and the retired [[Nilüfer Rostami]].
- **Steel League:** [[Mark One]] and the retired [[Mechthild Kranz]].
- **Aether Duels:** [[Adela Morante]] and the retired [[Oriol Zubia]].
- **Mixed War Arts:** [[Bogdan Koen]], [[Arash Güneş]], and the retired [[Thea Cucuruna]].
