# Thea Cucuruna

Thea Cucuruna is a retired Nereid Lizardfolk Mixed War Arts champion from a shallow-water settlement linking platforms and aquatic infrastructure. Blue-green, broad-tailed, and fitted with gold dental caps, she wears a formal pressure cloak at public hearings. She was [[BLACK LABEL]]'s first sponsored individual athlete and now chairs the Combat Sports Medical Council while advocating long-term injury reporting across species.
