The Puss in Boots is a [[Valgrath]] animal and field-supply retailer owned by [[Embrace Group]]. It provides pets, hunting animals, ranged equipment, and supplies for druids and rangers. Its regular manufacturers are [[Stinson's]], [[DAWAN]], [[Good Doctor]], [[Vineflare]], and [[deGraf's]].
