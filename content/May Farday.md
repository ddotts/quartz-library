May Farday was a natural philosopher from [[Avergwyn]] attached to [[Sator Polytechnic]]. Drawing on the [[Aether Programme]] and the development of [[The Odyssey|Potemkin 2]], she presented the realist synthesis in AP 1711. Her account reduced magic to evocation and transmutation acting through aether and established [[Realism]] as a unified theory.
