Unzan Station is a [[Haulers Guild]] gas-extraction platform suspended inside the upper atmosphere of the giant Unzan rather than orbiting above it. Lift-rated thrusters maintain its altitude. Three major refits have adapted it to changing storm stresses; the next refit is overdue.

- **Species:** Among station personnel, [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 92%; Aarakocra 8%.

Unzan's Aarakocra community descends largely from migrants trained on Eden, Procne, and earlier atmospheric facilities. The first crews successfully pressed for larger flight spaces, open shafts, and less restrictive habitat architecture, after which family migration turned temporary work rotations into a permanent community linked to other gas-giant stations.
