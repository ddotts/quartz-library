# Unzan Station

## Overview

Unzan Station is a [[Haulers Guild]] gas-extraction platform suspended inside the upper atmosphere of the giant Unzan rather than orbiting above it. Lift-rated thrusters maintain its altitude. Three major refits have adapted it to changing storm stresses; the next refit is overdue.
