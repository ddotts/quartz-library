# Sarai Soar Sabha

Sarai Soar Sabha is a civic Ironball club on [[Eden]]. Its Federal and public-facing identity attracts both sincere civic support and criticism of government branding. [[Atlas Group]] sponsors the club and bundles match travel with its passenger routes.

## Notable players

### Ishani Malkani

Ishani Malkani is a Prime League distributor from a Naya Milessian household. The lean Kenku has glossy black feathers, pale match markings across the beak, and expressive hand signals. She is known for disguising calls inside crowd noise and for youth-oriented tactical broadcasts. Atlas Group sponsors her travel and junior-tournament campaigns.
