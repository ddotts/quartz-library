**Demonyms** · Safiyan / safiyense

Safiye Chain is a western dome chain on [[Ping]]. Western domes occupy broken shelves between water stops. Market yards face the freight routes where civic councils negotiate concessions. [[Dome Mostyn]] and the former [[Dome Yüceil]] site lie along the western corridor.

The chain takes its name from Safiye.

All of [[DAWAN]]’s factories for [[Trained Awakened Intelligence]] are in the chain.

Passengers and freight between the Chain’s domes, and onward to [[Su Defteri]], transfer by shuttle at [[OSP3]]. The Dead Pens sit in an outlying concession beyond the former [[Dome Yüceil]] site.

## Dome Çarşı

**Demonyms** · Carsian / carsiense

Dome Çarşı is a western trade dome in Safiye Chain. Caravan loads wait by the outer gates. Curving market lanes run past civic meeting rooms beneath the dome.

The name means roughly “Market Dome” in Issiz.

Freight for Dome Āb in [[Su Defteri]] goes up to [[OSP3]] and down on a separate shuttle.

## The Dead Pens

The Dead Pens is an abandoned screened dawan site in Safiye Chain. Abandoned DAWAN test bays stand in an outlying concession tract. Shielding once suppressed Ping’s echoes and gave the yards their name.

DAWAN crews named the bays for the way Ping’s signal echoes went dead inside the shielding. The nickname stuck after the site was abandoned.
