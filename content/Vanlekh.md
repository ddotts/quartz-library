**Demonyms** · Vanlekhan / vanlekhense

Vanlekh is Maguuma’s administrative capital in the uplands of [[Varṣāvana]]. Land offices overlook the streets and administer territory in the surrounding forest.

The name means roughly “Forest Register” in Melissian.
