Embrace Group is a conservative [[Procne]] retail group founded in the late AC 400s. It owns the Dindessian Court, Emain, Elmier Woods, and the Puss in Boots. The group belonged to both the [[Imperium]] and the [[Space Commerce Federation]], but sanctions removed its weapons and magical-item divisions. [[Emilia Sartori]] leads the modern company.
