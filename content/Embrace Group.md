# Embrace Group

Embrace Group is a conservative [[Procne]] retail group founded in the late AC 400s. It owns [[The Dindessian Court]], [[Emain]], [[Elmier Woods]], and [[The Puss in Boots]]. The group belonged to both the [[Imperium]] and the [[Space Commerce Federation]], but sanctions removed its weapons and magical-item divisions. [[Emilia Sartori]] leads the modern company.

## Sports sponsorships

Embrace Group sponsors [[Legatus]] and Avergwyn's [[Avergwyn#Poise Masima|Poise Masima]]. The pairing connects its conservative retail identity to an established club and an Iron Fist tournament built around poise and restraint. Embrace Group also contributes to [[Portas#Los Cuatro Senderos|Los Cuatro Senderos]].
