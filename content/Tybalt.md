# Tybalt

- **Capital:** [[Tandhaven]]
- **Population:** Approximately 330 million residents.
- **Species:** [[Species and Migration|Common distribution]] 62%; additional Dwarf concentration 6%; Gnome 4%; Bugbear 4%; Goblin 4%; Hobgoblin 4%; Goliath 4%; Dragonborn 4%; Kobold 4%; Construct 4%.
- **Languages:** [[Deresian|Belter Deresian]] 68%; [[Common Language|Common (Bloc)]] 60%; [[Deresian|Bloc Deresian]] 38%; [[Common Language|Common (Milessian)]] 34%; [[Common Language|Common (Dindessian)]] 10%; [[Common Language#Regional varieties and descendants|Firan Creole]] 40%.
- **Beliefs:** [[Tsuiseki|Followers of the Path]] 12%; [[Her Church of Light|Her Church of Light]] 24%; [[The Moiety]] 5%; local traditions 11%; atheism 48%.

## Overview

Tybalt has diverse biomes, but its conditions are harsh.

Tybalt seats the [[Firan Confederation]] and hosts major [[deGraf's]] operations as well as [[Faswell–Kron]] and [[BLACK LABEL]]. Tybalt exports resilient machinery and hydraulics. Its industry relies on imported feedstocks and specialist chemicals. Filtration crews and water haulers keep treated supplies moving when magnetic reversals interrupt equipment.

## Appearance

Tybalt is a bruised blue-green world surrounded by restless auroral bands that wander, divide, and reverse whenever its magnetic field turns.

## Magnetic instability

Magnetic field fluctuates and flips frequently, heavily limiting technological infrastructure. Fluctuations allow solar winds to penetrate the atmosphere. Seat of the [[Firan Confederation]] ([[Space Union]] member); the Confederation represents both Tybalt and [[Quamridge Mass]].

## Technology and survival

Frequent magnetic reversals expose Tybalt to solar wind and make ordinary electronics unreliable, rewarding the bare-metal and mechanically resilient technologies associated with [[deGraf's]] and [[Ruby Abyss]]. deGraf's major industrial presence here follows directly from that environment: its hydraulics, repair network, and specialized legacy parts remain useful when more integrated magitech systems fail. The expansion turned an old [[Schil]] family company into one of the principal industrial institutions of the modern CCS. [[Faswell–Kron]] and [[BLACK LABEL]] are also based on Tybalt, giving the planet [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] interests in extraction, construction, sport, and consumer culture alongside the Confederation government.

Firan Creole is a working-class variety of [[Common Language|Bloc]] shared with [[Quamridge Mass]].

## Migration from Typhon

Tybalt receives a smaller reverse flow from [[Typhon]] rather than forming an equal fourth center of the Schil–Sator–Cthel migration. Families followed [[deGraf's]] contracts, independent workshops, and kin connections into Firan, seeking surface settlements, open sky, and ordinary horizons despite Tybalt's heavy gravity and magnetic instability. The present Dwarf, Gnome, Bugbear, Goblin, Hobgoblin, Goliath, Dragonborn, Kobold, and Construct concentrations also include direct Firan and corporate migration, so their size does not measure the strength of the Typhon flow alone. Most Typhon migrants entered existing Firan Creole communities instead of recreating Cthelic society.

## Infrastructure

Any planet-wide communication or power network must tolerate field changes rather than assuming a stable orientation, making maintenance expertise a central civic resource. Tybalt is a leading testing ground for graceful failure. Devices proven in the harsh environment gain a strong reputation across frontier settlements, ships, and disaster zones, giving local engineers bargaining power in relationships otherwise centered on extraction.

## Regional geography

[[Veen Reach]] links Tybalt’s settlements by river, with [[Tandhaven]] as the main freight port. Downstream, a canal reaches Geeldam’s navigation lock in the reclaimed lowlands of [[Geelwaard]]. Barges bring treated water back up to the Reach. An eastern tributary and connecting goods sidings serve the [[Kronen Belt]], where Werkhof’s repair yards stand at the riverside edge of Kronenburg.

Above the last navigable landing, a road climbs into the fertile uplands of [[Wynheide]]. Windkerf marks an exposed ridge, and Griphal’s proving grounds occupy a broad bench below the notch.

## Physical characteristics

- **Diameter:** Approximately 12,610 km.
- **Surface gravity:** Approximately 1.32 g.
- **Mean density:** Approximately 7.35 g/cm³.

Its metal-rich interior produces high density. Heavy gravity compounds the difficulty of industrial work, reinforcing dependence on robust mechanical equipment.

## Sports

[[Tandhaven Flux Gilde]] represents Tybalt in Ironball. [[Faswell–Kron]] sponsors the world's two principal racing circuits.

### Magneetvlucht

The **Magneetvlucht** is an Aerial 1 circuit through shipyard gantries and magnetic freight lanes. Pilots must account for shifting industrial fields as well as the wake of heavy traffic.

### Yellow Line

The **Yellow Line** is a Raging Dust circuit laid across marked extraction roads and reclaimed industrial ground. Its strict boundary line gives the race its name: crossing it incurs an immediate penalty even when doing so would shorten the route.
