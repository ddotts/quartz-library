# Tybalt

## Overview

Tybalt is diverse-biome planet, but conditions are harsh.

> **Appearance:** Tybalt is a bruised blue-green world surrounded by restless auroral bands that wander, divide, and reverse whenever its magnetic field turns.

Magnetic field fluctuates and flips frequently, heavily limiting technological infrastructure. Fluctuations allow solar winds to penetrate the atmosphere. Seat of the [[Firan Confederation]] ([[Space Union]] member); the Confederation represents both Tybalt and [[Quamridge Mass]].

Frequent magnetic reversals expose Tybalt to solar wind and make ordinary electronics unreliable, rewarding the bare-metal and mechanically resilient technologies associated with [[deGraf's]] and [[Ruby Abyss]]. deGraf's major industrial presence here follows directly from that environment: its hydraulics, repair network, and specialized legacy parts remain useful when more integrated magitech systems fail. [[Faswell–Kron]] and [[BLACK LABEL]] are also based on Tybalt, giving the planet [[Collective Commercial Space|CCS]] interests in extraction, construction, sport, and consumer culture alongside the Confederation government.

Any planet-wide communication or power network must tolerate field changes rather than assuming a stable orientation, making maintenance expertise a central civic resource. Tybalt is a leading testing ground for graceful failure. Devices proven in the harsh environment gain a strong reputation across frontier settlements, ships, and disaster zones, giving local engineers bargaining power in relationships otherwise centered on extraction.
